The initiative in one pictureTwo geometries, one bridge

Cartographer's shapes are survey-exact but unplaced (SRID:0, feet, the plat's own north). The SIR's county parcel ring is placed but tax-map-grade (WGS84). Anchoring solves one similarity transform per frame that lays the exact shape onto the county's own State Plane grid, then writes sibling rows into the same geo table the SIR map already draws.

PhasesDependency order

P0anative SR→P0blink docs→P0cextract metadata→P1anchor.ts math→P2anchor-geometry runbook→P3scale + viewer

P0a, P0b and P0c are independent and can land in parallel. P1 needs only P0c's fixtures (the car-wash plat). P2 needs all of P0 plus P1. P3 comes after accuracy has been measured.

Why fit-to-parcel is the workhorse

Across the 10 newest SIRs (315 geometries): metes and bounds 42%, closed shapes with no position 51%, printed State Plane values in only about 1%. See the IG survey.

Honesty boundary

Agents never produce coordinates. They name correspondences (ids and indices) and judge overlays. Every number comes from anchor.ts, and every anchor carries an accuracy class: survey / tied / gis_fit / approximate.
