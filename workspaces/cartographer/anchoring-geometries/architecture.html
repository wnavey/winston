CARTOGRAPHER · ANCHORING GEOMETRIES

Anchoring extracted geometries to the county parcel

Architecture companion to DESIGN-SPEC.md (winston#279). Cartographer's shapes are survey-exact but float (SRID:0, feet, the plat's own north); the SIR's county parcel ring is on the map but only tax-map-grade. This spec joins the two with one similarity transform per frame. Research: IG geometry source survey.

Why fit-to-parcel is the workhorse

Across the 10 newest SIRs (315 geometries): metes and bounds 42%, closed shapes with no position 51%, printed State Plane values in only about 1%. Printed coordinates and corner ties are upgrades where they exist (§1.2).

Honesty boundary

Agents never produce coordinates. They name correspondences (ids and indices) and judge overlays. Every number comes from anchor.ts, and every anchor carries an accuracy class: survey / tied / gis_fit / approximate (§3.1, D7).

02 · how it worksThe anchor transformOne similarity transform per frame; how 3.1 solve picks a solver, what each solver computes, where the target system comes from, and the status of an anchor row.

E,N = s·R(θ)·(x,y)·kunits + (tE, tN). θ rotates the plat's basis of bearings onto grid north, (tE, tN) puts the point of beginning on the ground, s is 1 unless the plat states a combined scale factor, and kunits converts feet (or varas) into the target system's units.

D7aWhich solver 3.1 solve picks: the eligibility ladder

Run once per frame by 3.1 solve, a script. It reads what 2.1 correspond supplied (control points, ties, ring matches, all by id and index) and the D6 evidence (basis of bearings, units, scale factor), and takes the first rung whose inputs are present. The agent never names a solver.

2.1 matched at least 2 figure vertices to printed N/E boxes, and the zone is stated on the plat or target_srid passes the sanity gate?yes →fromPrintedCoordinates · rigid least squares, scale fixed · class survey
no ↓ · also when a control point's residual fails the 1 ft gate: the rung is demoted and the readout says so (Q12)
2.1's ties fix all three unknowns θ, tE, tN? (2 vertex ties · 1 vertex + 1 edge tie · 1 vertex + a grid basis)yes →fromTies · θ from the edges or the grid, then t from the vertices · class tied
no ↓ · a lone edge tie or a grid basis still fixes θ and is handed down as a constraint
A figure in the frame has a same_as or union_of ring match?yes →fitToRing · bearing votes → centroid → boundary-distance refine · class gis_fit
no ↓ · an easement-only frame matched within has nothing to fit
otherwiseunanchorable · never guessed

Claim (D7, D9): "the highest rung whose inputs are present is the one that runs"; "the class is the rung, never the score"; ties that cannot fix the transform "pass down as constraints"; with no correspondence at all a figure is "reported unanchorable, never guessed".

D7b–D7dWhat each solver needs and computes

SolverNeeds (from 2.1 + D6)SolvesHeld fixedIts own gateClass
fromPrintedCoordinates≥ 2 control points (figure vertex ↔ printed N/E) · the zone, transformed into target_srid when it differs · units · scale factorθ, tE, tN in closed form: centre both point sets, θ = atan2(Σ(x′N′ − y′E′), Σ(x′E′ + y′N′)), t = target centroid − s·R(θ)·local centroids = 1 or the stated combined factor · kunitslargest control-point residual ≤ 1 ft, else demotedsurvey

fitToRingthe fit figures (same_as or union_of matches) · the target = union of the matched geo rows' geom_local · any constraint handed downθ, tE, tN, minus what is constrained; steps belows = 1 · a free-scale re-solve is reported onlynone of its own: the operator judges IoU, mean distance and ambiguity against D11gis_fit

Claim (D7, §3.1): scale is "usually fixed at 1" and set "to the stated combined factor when the plat converts surface to grid distances"; every solver solves rotation and translation only, and the free-scale solve is "reported only as a diagnostic" (D8).

D7dfitToRing, step by step

StepWhatWhy
aUnits: each fit figure's ring + its frame offset, times kunitsvaras read as feet would scale the shape 2.78×
bCandidate rotations: every (figure edge, target edge) pair votes Δθ = ring bearing − figure bearing, weighted by min length ÷ (1 + length gap), into a 1° histogram; the top 4 peaks are the candidates; θ = 0 is always one on a grid basis; a handed-down θ is the only onea rectangle votes for 0° and 180° equally: the ambiguity is found before any fit
cInitial translation: rotate, then move the fit figures' area centroid onto the target's centroida start close enough for a local minimizer
dRefine (θ, tE, tN) by Nelder–Mead on the symmetric mean boundary distance: sample both outlines every 2 ft, nearest distance each way, mean of both; stop under 0.01 ft and 0.001°symmetric, so an extra or missing county vertex costs a little instead of derailing the fit
ePick and score: the lowest refined score wins; ambiguity = best ÷ second-best (1.0 is a coin flip; flagged above 0.8)look-alikes are reported, never chosen silently
fDiagnostics: one free-scale re-solve from the winner; IoU, Hausdorff, mean distance, area ratio, θ vs grid (D8)a scale drifting past 1% means wrong units or a wrong ring

Claim (D7): "candidate rotations from matching edge bearings and lengths; initial translation from aligning centroids; refine (θ, tE, tN) by minimizing symmetric boundary distance … which tolerates county rings with extra or missing vertices; scale fixed".

D3Where the target system comes from: the native-SR fallback chain

The county's parcel layer is stored in some coordinate system. It says which in one metadata request (?f=json → latestWkid: Austin 2277, Bexar 2278). getLayerSpatialReference(layerUrl) reads that listing (already fetched once per service by the layer-identity guard) and compares it to the native_sr the module recorded in its layer-identity.json; resolve-site then requires native_sr on every ring it writes, and sir_add_parcel_geo fills geom_local from it.

The layer's live ?f=json gives a projected latestWkid (else wkid)?yes →use it · srid_local = latestWkid · drifted from the module's recorded native_sr? flag the module for a prospector refresh
no ↓ · Web Mercator (3857/102100), geographic (4326) and custom-WKT layers land here directly
Curated county → State Plane table (FIPS → EPSG, units, basis) has an entry?yes →use it · basis = ngs_zone or observed_layer
no ↓
otherwisegeom_local NULL + flag it

Claim (D1, D3): "the county's parcel layer declares the zone; we record it, we don't choose it." The curated table is built per county from NGS zone definitions, "not by point-in-box lookup", which geom-local §2 showed to be ambiguous.

D12The life of one anchor row

proposed3.1 solve writes it→ acceptedoperator, 4.1→ stalethe file is re-extracted
proposed3.1 solve writes it→ rejectedoperator, 4.1 · no geo rows

Claim (D12, D14): an anchor whose run_slug ≠ the file's metadata.lastGeometryRun is stale by definition; "staleness never deletes anything": the geo rows stay, flagged properties.stale=true, until an anchor run on the new run_slug replaces them. A redo from the operator does not change status; it re-runs 2.1 in the same run.

watch outA bad county ring gives a confidently wrong anchor. Mitigations (§7): always show neighbouring rings in the overlay, check θ against a grid basis, report ambiguity, and in v1 always stop at the operator.

Anchors carry no FK to geometry ids because Cartographer's publish deletes and re-inserts a file's geometries; the (file_id, run_slug, frame_key) key survives that.

How accuracy gets measured (§3.8)
  • Car-wash plat 2024178771 (KY 3089): solve with printed coordinates (truth), then pretend there are none and fit to the Jefferson County ring. The per-vertex gap is the first real gis_fit error number.
  • Palm Bay Lot 7 (ALTA survey, FL East NAD83(2011) grid basis): fitToRing should give θ ≈ 0.
  • One Conroe tract (TxDOT right-of-way-map basis): θ is a real unknown; this tests the ambiguity metric.
Coordination and risks (§7)
  • extract-geometry-v2 is under active work by another session; P0c lands through that workstream.
  • Cartographer publish deletes geometry ids; D12/D14 exist so anchors survive as stale instead of vanishing through cascades.
  • Datum realizations (NAD83 vs NAD83(2011) vs WGS84) differ by 1–2 m: invisible at gis_fit accuracy, material for survey. Record them on the anchor; never silently mix.
  • Prod side note: SELECT * FROM geometry_columns errors because PostGIS parses geo_srid_local_matches as a typmod hint. Harmless to our code; a substation follow-up.
