Anchored geometry for every site

Claim (§8): schema decisions are cheap now and expensive later; runbooks can be built in any order.

watch out P0c is not cosmetic. Fit metrics are computed over a union outline that keeps the target's interior lot lines — 25.7 ft mean and 209 ft Hausdorff where per-lot it is 1.6–2.5 ft (§2.3 C5, winston#289 B3). Half the corpus is anchored by ring fit, so no auto-accept threshold can be set against a number that is wrong for the dominant case.

Claim (§3.3): 147 of 315 geometries appear in more than one document; Palm Bay's Lot 7 is reproduced in 12.

Why consolidation pays for itself
Twelve documents reciting Lot 7 collapse to one anchor run. Consolidation's second output is the anchor worklist — a minimal covering set of frames (D8).
Prefer a frame that can be fitted
An easement exhibit showing a bare strip has nothing to match against the county ring. A plat showing the parent boundary and the easement anchors itself and carries the easement along (D8).
Duplication is free accuracy
Twelve independent transcriptions cross-check each other. Eleven closing to 0.02 ft and one to 40 ft identifies a misread, not a disagreement about the world (§3.3).
What will not land on pass one
27% are located only by reference to another document and 17% are approximate sketches. A feature with identity and no position is a first-class outcome (D9, D10).

Claim (§5): "decoupled from SIRs" and "needs a parcel ring from somewhere" are the same sentence — the work is sourcing the ring.

the honest caveat For rung 1 the CRS genuinely is all you need — the parcel ring only feeds a zone sanity-check and the quality metrics, both optional in AnchorTolerances. But printed State Plane coordinates appear in 3 of 315 geometries. For the 51% that are a closed shape with no position, the ring is the anchor (§5).

05 · what to peel off, and what is unresolvedPriority and open questionsThe tiers in words, the tensions to carry, and the twelve questions this draft leaves open.

This is a plan of a plan. Decisions are directional; the questions outnumber them on purpose, and the spec expects to be iterated on for weeks with slices peeled off one at a time.

P0 · unblockers
Project ownership for geo, the derived-only invariant and a file origin, and the fit-metric fix. Small, and each one gates work downstream. Schema is cheap now and expensive later.
P1 · the SIR workflow
The triage runbook first — independently valuable, no new tables, no change to the SIR runbook. Then a full SIR by hand. Then features and consolidation, the biggest new design.
P2 · decoupling
The anchoring target, then the manual Cartographer path end to end as the test that proves it.
P3–P5 · later
Dependency-ordered placement for the 27%, the agent tool surface and a viewer, and the plan-set reader. P5 is blocked only on P0a.

TTensions to carry

#n raised in the spec + modeling consequence ★ affects the schema
A Identity
A1#1★Stable feature ids across re-runs — consolidation must propose merges and splits against existing features, not rebuild from scratch. A wrong merge is silent and expensive.
A2+★Geometry has a vintage — granted, amended, partially released, superseded by a replat. A feature is a shape as of a date, and title chains are where SIRs operate. No time dimension exists today.
A3+★Recorded versus proposed — geo will hold both, and the headline use case measures between them. Conflating them would be a quiet correctness bug.
B Trust and cost
B1#4Accuracy must reach the tool surface — an easement placed at gis_fit and a computed 2 ft setback violation is noise presented as a finding.
B2#5Triage is the product until extraction gets cheap — the first honest number P1a produces is how many documents per site actually carry a reconstructable boundary.
B3+Units and grid-versus-surface discipline must survive consolidation when two readings of one feature disagree.
B4+Consolidation feeds back into triage — the second pass over a site should be much cheaper than the first.
