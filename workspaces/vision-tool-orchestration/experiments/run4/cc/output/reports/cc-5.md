# Plan Content, Data Tables & Conditional Plan Requirements

### ADR-01: Is addressing information shown on an Addressing Plan or site plan sheet and listed in the Sheet Index?

**Status:** Fail

**Explanation:** No 'Addressing Plan' sheet is listed in the sheet index on Sheet 1. Addressing information appears integrated into site plan sheets rather than on a dedicated sheet.

**Evidence:** - Cover Sheet (Sheet 1)

---

### ADR-04: Are the main front entrance, building doors, entries, and access points labeled on the site plan?

**Status:** Pass

**Explanation:** Building entrances are labeled on Sheet 14 using Key Note 7 callouts with triangle symbols defined in the legend as 'PROPOSED BUILDING ENTRANCE'.

**Evidence:** - Dimensional Control & Site Plan 1 Of 2 (Sheet 14)

---

### ADR-05: Are driveway access points, handicap parking, garages, and sidewalk access identified on plans?

**Status:** Pass

**Explanation:** Driveway access points shown on site plans (Sheets 14-15); handicap parking labeled on garage plan (Sheet 37) with HC/VAN symbols; garage building identified on site plans; sidewalk access shown via ADA path symbols and ramp callouts.

**Evidence:** - Dimensional Control & Site Plan 1 Of 2 (Sheet 14)
- Dimensional Control & Site Plan 2 Of 2 (Sheet 15)
- Building Plan - Level 1 (Sheet 37)

---

### ADR-07: Is the project name free of the address number?

**Status:** Fail

**Explanation:** Project name '1700 SOUTH LAMAR' begins with the address number '1700', contrary to 911 Addressing Standards requiring descriptive project names without address numbers.

**Evidence:** - Cover Sheet (Sheet 1)

---

### ADR-08: Are multiple buildings labeled sequentially (Bldg 1, Bldg 2, etc.) per 911 Addressing Standards?

**Status:** Not Applicable

**Explanation:** Only one building (Proposed Building 1) is shown on the site plan. Sequential building labeling requirement applies only when multiple buildings are present.

**Evidence:** No evidence found.
