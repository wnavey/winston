# Base Sheet Requirements

### CC-2-02: Does the Engineer's Summary Letter identify the watershed location and classification?

**Status:** Pass

**Explanation:** Engineer's Summary Letter identifies West Bouldin Creek Watershed and classifies it as urban watershed in the Utilities and Stormwater Management section.

**Evidence:** - Engineer's Summary Letter

---

### CC-2-14: Do all plan sheets include the engineer's seal, signature, and date?

**Status:** Fail

**Explanation:** Building elevation and plan sheets (35, 36, 37) lack professional engineer seals and are marked 'NOT FOR REGULATORY APPROVAL, PERMITTING, OR CONSTRUCTION.'

**Evidence:** - Building Elevations (Sheet 35)
- Building Elevations (Sheet 36)
- Building Plan - Level 1 (Sheet 37)

---

### CC-2-16: Are boundary lines with bearings and dimensions shown on Existing Conditions and Overall Site Plan sheets?

**Status:** Pass

**Explanation:** Boundary lines with bearings and dimensions are shown on Sheet 8 (Existing Conditions) and Sheet 13 (Overall Site Plan), including Record Curve Tables with geometric data.

**Evidence:** - Existing Conditions & Demolition Plan (1 Of 2) (Sheet 8)
- Overall Site & Paving & Striping Plan (Sheet 13)

---

### CC-2-21: Are existing and future dedicated easements shown on all sheets?

**Status:** Pass

**Explanation:** Easements are shown on applicable site layout sheets where they exist (Sheets 14, 21, etc.). Legends consistently define easement line types across the plan set.

**Evidence:** - Dimensional Control & Site Plan 1 Of 2 (Sheet 14)
- Grading Plan (1 Of 2) (Sheet 21)

---

### CC-2-23: Are existing and proposed overhead utility lines shown on all sheets?

**Status:** Pass

**Explanation:** Existing overhead electric lines, power poles, and guy anchors are shown on Sheet 8 (Existing Conditions) with proper symbology and callouts. Project proposes underground utilities.

**Evidence:** - Existing Conditions & Demolition Plan (1 Of 2) (Sheet 8)

---

### CC-2-24: Are existing and proposed underground utility lines shown on all sheets?

**Status:** Pass

**Explanation:** Existing underground utilities shown on Sheet 8 (water, wastewater, storm, telecom, fiberoptics). Proposed underground utilities shown on Sheet 18 (water, wastewater, storm, electric).

**Evidence:** - Existing Conditions & Demolition Plan (1 Of 2) (Sheet 8)
- Overall Utility Plan (Sheet 18)
