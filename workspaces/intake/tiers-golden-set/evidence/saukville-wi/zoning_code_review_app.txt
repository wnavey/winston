    1. Plat of Survey (Residential & Commercial) prepared by a registered land surveyor showing the location,
       boundaries, dimensions, elevations, uses, and size of the following: subject site; existing and proposed
       structures; existing and proposed easements, streets, and other public ways; off-street parking, loading
       areas and driveways; existing highway access restrictions; existing and proposed street, side, and rear

       approved by the Engineer, who shall certify in writing that satisfactory, adequate, and safe sewage

       the Engineer, who shall certify in writing that an adequate and safe supply of water will be provided.

       Plumbing, or Health Inspectors.

       by chapter 205 of the Municipal Code.
