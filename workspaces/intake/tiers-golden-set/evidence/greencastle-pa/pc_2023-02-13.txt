                                         Borough of Greencastle
                                      Planning Commission Minutes
                                            February 13, 2023
                                                6:00 P.M.
                                         60 N. Washington Street
Members in Attendance: Ed Wine, Tony Homer, Jim Thomas, and Joe Degrange. Also present were Borough
Manager Emilee Little. Commission member Guy Camp was absent.

Wine called the meeting to order at 6:02 p.m and noted there was no quorum present at the January 9th Planning
Commission meeting.

Public Comment Period
None.

Minutes
On a Thomas/Degrange motion, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to approve the December 28, 2022
meeting minutes.

Old Business
The Commission reviewed a preliminary combined subdivision and land development plan as submitted by Unger
Surveying & Construction on behalf of Outdoor Contractors Inc. for the subdivision of parcel 08-2A00.-007.-000000
located at 650 N. Antrim Way and construction of a Dollar General Store on the same.

       Little reviewed the preliminary land development plan noting the plan details including improvements and
       zoning and reviewed all previous and current engineering comments. Little recommended the Commission
       table the plan pending further evaluation of outstanding comments.
       Wine asked if all Dollar General Stores follow a similar design. Dave Unger of Unger Surveying &
       Construction stated that basically yes the stores are similar and based on population. Unger also noted that
       this store would be a market style with fresh produce.
       The Commission stated they would need to see a lighting plan to review.
       Unger questioned the water easement along the southern property line. Little noted Unger could reach out to
       the Water Authority engineer directly to discuss.
       Little questioned if the plans were submitted to the PennDOT for review for a highway occupancy permit.
       Unger noting they would be submitting plans to PennDOT shortly. Little explained that PennDOT reviews
       can be prolonged and encouraged prompt submittal.
       Thomas questioned the proposed flow of stormwater on the parcel. Unger noted stormwater would follow
       the same path as it flows now and a new raingarden is proposed for this development.
On a Degrange/Thomas motion, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to table the preliminary combined
subdivision and land development plan as submitted by Unger Surveying & Construction on behalf of Outdoor
Contractors Inc. for the subdivision of parcel 08-2A00.-007.-000000 located at 650 N. Antrim Way and construction
of a Dollar General Store on the same pending further comments being addressed.


New Business
The Commission reviewed a land development plan as submitted by Antrim Design on behalf of Jeffrey Bitner for
the development of parcel 08-2B36.-026.-000000 located at 162 S. Carlisle St for the construction of three two-story
townhouses.
       Little reviewed the plans, the variance as approved by the Zoning Hearing Board, and the current engineering
       comments as submitted. Little noted the plans were incomplete and did not include information related to
       utilities or stormwater.
On a Homer/Degrange motion, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to table the land development plan as
submitted by Antrim Design on behalf of Jeffrey Bitner for the development of parcel 08-2B36.-026.-000000 located
at 162 S. Carlisle St for the construction of three two-story townhouses pending further comments being addressed.

Correspondence
None.

On a Degrange/Thomas motion, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to adopt the practice of not meeting
monthly unless plans or other matters are submitted for review and/or lengthy meeting minutes from the prior
meeting need to be reviewed for approval, pending guidance from the Planning Commission chairman.

Adjournment
On a Thomas/Degrange motion, the Planning Commission voted unanimously to adjourn the meeting at 6:48 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,
Emilee Little
Borough Manager
