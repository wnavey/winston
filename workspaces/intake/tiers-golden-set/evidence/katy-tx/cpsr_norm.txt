City of Katy Commercial Plan Submittal Requirements
The following is a list of documents required for the submittal and review of a Commercial New Construction building built inside of the City of Katy before a permit is issued. You will notice that each box follows the required document as well as the supplemental information required on each document below. The City of Katy is under the 2021 IBC and 2023 NEC. The minimum Wind Speed Design required is 130 MPH. All plan sheets must be of the same size and set to 1/8” scale.

☐ Irrigation Plan Set:
All irrigation plans used for construction must be drawn to 1/8” scale. The plan must include, at a minimum, the following information:
• The irrigator's seal, signature, and date of signing • All major physical features and the boundaries of the areas to be watered • A north arrow • A legend • The zone ﬂow measurement for each zone • Location and type of each: o Controller o Sensor (for example, but not limited to, rain, moisture, wind, ﬂow, or freeze) o Water source, such as, but not limited to a water meter and point(s) of connection o Backﬂow prevention device o Water emission device, including, but not limited to, spray heads, rotary sprinkler heads, quick-couplers, bubblers, drip, or micro-sprays o Valves, including but not limited to, zone valves, master valves, and isolation valves o Pressure regulation component; and o Main line and lateral piping o The scale used areas from heads showing head-to-head coverage (added so plan can show all coverage areas, per TCEQ rules); and o The design pressure.
