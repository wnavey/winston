       A. SITE PLAN REVIEW, 19-06-12-300-034, AIS CONSTRUCTION EQUIPMENT, 65809 GRATIOT
           AVENUE

             Mr. Cassin, Lenox Township Planner stated that AIS is requesting approval to construct a 5019-square-foot
             addition to the west side of the existing main building. The property is zoned Commercial General. The proposal
             has been reviewed, complies with zoning, and meets all setback requirements. The plan also complies with
             parking space minimums and complies with the landscaping requirements. There was no new onsite lighting
             proposed. The only issue is having a five-foot sidewalk, and it is shown now on the proposed site plan. He is
             recommending approval subject to Fire Department and Engineering reviews.
             Mr. Saif, Lenox Township Engineer explained that the proposal was lacking information, as follows:
                 •    the pond design
                 •    fire hydrate coverage
                 •    it is not known if the existing well and septic system could sustain the addition

     •    petitioners are aware that water and sewer available on the opposite side of Gratiot
     •    the current condition of the pavement and if it could support a fire truck. Other improvements were
          proposed for the pavement to bring it to current standards. Petitioners were made aware of the current
          Township standards
     •    the storm retention pond was not part of the site plan, but it could be addressed in the engineering stage
          without changing the layout of the site plan.
     •     If a fire hydrate needs to be brought in onsite, that can be reviewed during the engineering stage.

Mr. White, Lenox Township Public Safety Director, reported that the Township has adopted the International Fire
Code, IFC-2021, and all its appendices. In that code, there is a requirement that when an addition to a building
is done, it requires hydrants as municipal water is available. The petitioner has been very cooperative about
communicating with the Township about constructing a fire lane; equipment would not be stored in the fire lane
to allow emergency personnel to operate onsite. He would be comfortable with conditional approval with the
understanding that the building and site meet IFC-2021 and all its appendices.

Adam Harding, AIS Equipment reported that they would comply with the fire code.

Collin Finch, BK Civil Engineer, representing AIS, remarked that they are comfortable with the conditions that
have been expressed and would comply with the requests.

L. Rossell asked if AIS has considered completing a traffic study and a bypass lane. She asked if additional
employees would be hired.

Mr. Saif reported that he recommends a traffic study, but MDOT dictates whether a bypass lane is needed and
whether a study is necessary.

Pannell inquired if there is a plan to expand Gratiot in that area.

Mr. Saif commented that there is no expansion plan that he is aware of.

Pannell remarked that he would prefer the plans be revised before the meeting so the Planning Commission could
see the corrections.

Harding stated there may have been some miscommunication in getting details to the Engineer.

Mr. Saif commented that AIS elected to come before the Planning Commission and explain.

Turchi inquired if the new plans show the fire lane.

Harding stated that yes, the plans have included a fire lane.

Saif explained that there are still other missing components, but Mr. White has seen the plans with the fire lane
and is fine with it.

Rosseel asked if the site is on septic and well and if AIS plans on hooking up to water and sewer.

Saif remarked that the state mandates if there is public sewer and water, they would have to hook up to it. This is
an existing site with no additional demand, so they would not be required to tap in.

Harding reported that there are no additional employees; it is empty shop space.

Pannell inquired who makes the decision if they need to hook to the sewer, and how would anyone from the
Township know if they add anything.

Saif remarked that ultimately it is the Building Department, if there is an increase in fixtures, they would not be
allowed to upgrade their existing system.

Cassin stated that it would be on the site plan, and then they would need to hook into the water and sewer.

9. TOWNSHIP PLANNER
Cassin reported that the Governor signed legislation that was passed by the Senate and House that took away local control of
Solar Farms.    This means that a parcel that meets the minimum conditions, setbacks, and height requirements can
accommodate a solar farm within any township or community, subject to the approval of the Public Service Commission. The
Governor's agenda is to provide incentives to make Michigan greener. There will be more information to come.

Rossell inquired about the difference between a Solar Farm and a residential house. She asked about the status of the progress
of the Lenox Township Solar Panel Ordinance.

Cassin explained that he is in the process of doing the ordinances along with the Master Plan and that will all be included.
