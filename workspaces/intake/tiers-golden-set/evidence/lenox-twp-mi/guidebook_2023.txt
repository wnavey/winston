order to assist property owners, the development community, and the general public. It      Fee Schedule

development application and review process. In this Guidebook you will find applica- tion
forms and requirements, submittal checklists, easy-to-follow review process flow charts,

This document is not an ordinance. It is important to note that the ordinances of the
Township should be thoroughly researched before reaching any conclusions on ques-
tions related to development. Each case is unique and may require modification of the
general process.

                                                                                                  Pre-Application Meeting
A rezoning is a change in the zoning classification of a specific property, it is an                    (Optional)

with the Township Board.
                                                                                        Submission of the application, required docu-
                                                                                           ments, and fees to the Township Clerk
Who can initiate a rezoning request?

A rezoning may be initiated by the Township Board, the Planning Commission,
or an owner of real property within the Township.                                       Informational review by Township Engineer


What is a Conditional rezoning?
                                                                                       Review and recommendation by the Township
A conditional rezoning is a rezoning request with a voluntary offer of condi-                          Planner
tions attached. As part of a conditional rezoning request, an owner of proper-
ty may voluntarily offer in writing, and the Township may approve, certain use
and development of land as a condition to the approval of a rezoning.                  Public Hearing at Planning Commission Meeting

An offer of conditions submitted as part of a conditional rezoning request shall
bear a reasonable relationship to the property for which rezoning is request-
ed.

What is a Planned Unit Development?                                                            Pre-Application Meeting
                                                                                                     (Optional)
A Planned Unit Development (PUD) is a development option that provides a
degree of flexibility in regard to the use, area, height, bulk, and placement reg-
                                                                                     Submission of the application, required docu-
ulations for large-scale developments. These may include, but are not limited
                                                                                        ments, and fees to the Township Clerk
to, housing developments, shopping centers, industrial parks, office uses and
mixed use developments. Planned unit developments may be allowed when
the following criteria are met.                                                      Informational review by Township Fire Depart-
                                                                                             ment and Township Engineer
•   The site must be under the control of one owner or a group of owners
    with a unified development plan.
•   The minimum PUD site shall be 20 acres.                                          Zoning compliance review by Township Planner
•   The site must have frontage along and access to a road capable of accom-
    modating the anticipated traffic volumes..
•   The development must be designed as an entity, intended to be devel- oped        Public Hearing at Planning Commission Meeting
    in clearly identified stages.
•   The property has irregular or unique physical constraints, or significant
    natural or historic features that might otherwise be lost through conven-           Planning Commission Recommendation
    tional development methods.
•   The PUD will provide a complementary mixture of uses or housing types and
    amenities within a unique, high quality design as determined by the
                                                                                                Township Board Action
    Planning Commission.
•   The proposed type and density of use shall not result in and unreasonable
    increase in traffic or use of public services.
