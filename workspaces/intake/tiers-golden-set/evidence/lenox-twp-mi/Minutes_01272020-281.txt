3.   Public Hearing:
          A. Special Land Use Request – 19-06-23-300-007, Shahin Haddad/Co 2825 Lenox LLC., 61705 Gratiot Avenue.
          Clifford read aloud the Public Hearing notice.

         Motion: Clifford             Support: Vennard
         Motion to open the Public Hearing at 7:01 pm.
         Roll Call Vote: Clifford, Vennard, Ickes, Marino and Gennette. All Ayes. Motion carried.

         Township Planner Steve Cassin gave a brief summary of the Special Land Use Request. The Township Board granted a Provisionary
         Certificate for Medical Marihuana; this is a building review and type of amenities that would be on the site.
         T. Ciaramitaro, attorney representing 2825 Lenox LLC., was informed that certain documents were not included with the application
         request. They would be submitted to the Township within a month.
         C. Dallo, commented that the soil boring test report was not submitted, as their engineer received them last week. Their engineer
         would be working on the plans and would submit them soon.

         Motion: Vennard              Support: Clifford
         Motion to close the Public Hearing at 7:04 pm.
         Roll Call Vote: Vennard, Clifford, Ickes, Marino and Gennette. All Ayes. Motion carried.

8.   Communications Presented
        Township Planner Steve Cassin detailed the Michigan State Extension Citizen Planner Program, beginning February 20, 2020. The
        six-week educational program is on Thursday evenings for three hours. It provides a convenient way for community leaders to
        obtain the technical knowledge and proficiency they need to perform their duties effectively. Some of the topics are: Planning for
        the Future of Your Community, Implementing the Plan with Zoning, and Using Innovative Planning and Zoning. Macomb County is
        providing one scholarship to each community, interested members can contact Cassin.

9.   Township Planner
        a. Recreation Plan Update
        Steve Cassin stated that the Parks & Recreation Survey has closed, and he is in the process of tabulating the results. The
        demographic data has been assembled, and he is working with Township Administration to create an action plan.
