Discussion on Chapter 17.84 R-5 Planned Unit Development District
No action needed – The City’s R-5 Planned Unit Development (PUD) District is intended to provide for the
development or redevelopment of tracts of ground on a unit basis, allowing greater ﬂexibility of land use
and building locations than the conventional single-lot method provided in other sections of the Zoning
Code. Flexibility in Zoning was a topic highlighted in the recent presentation by Luke Seaberg of ISU
Extension and Outreach on a way to help address the housing shortage. The PUD has a minimum area of
ﬁve acres. These minimums vary, but typically do have a minimum between 2-5 acres. There are some
potential areas identiﬁed that PUD could be an option for development or redevelopment. Reducing our
minimum would allow for more of these opportunities. In researching other community’s PUD with
smaller area requirements, two were identiﬁed with no minimum. One is Knoxville, which has the PUD
established as it’s own zoning district, similar to Decorah. Knoxville’s PUD is included in the packet as
Dropbox File 008B. Another option would be to convert the existing zoning classiﬁcation to an overlay
district. Algona’s PRUD is established that way and included in the packet as Dropbox File 008C.
Garry likes the idea of reducing PUD from 5 acres to 2 acres. She questioned if there should be a
percentage of the lot dedicated to green space.
Reilly questioned if they area could be used as mixed use, such as, apartments and condos.
Goedken stated the lots may not be a perfect square, but traﬃc will still enter from a public street. He
also stated setbacks will still apply along with parking requirements. All zoning requirements still apply.
Klocke would like this to be a discussion topic at the next meeting.
