Consider a Resolu�on on Se�ng a Public Hearing Amending Title 17 – Zoning of the Municipal Code of the City of
Decorah related to Chapter 17.84 R-5 Planned Unit Development District
Action required – A public hearing is required by State Code for changes to a city’s zoning code. The City’s R-5
Planned Unit Development (PUD) District is intended to provide for the development or redevelopment of tracts of
ground on a unit basis, allowing greater ﬂexibility of land use and building locations than the conventional single-lot
method provided in other sections of the Zoning Code. Flexibility in Zoning was a topic highlighted in the recent
presentation by Luke Seaberg of ISU Extension and Outreach on a way to help address the housing shortage. The
PUD has a minimum area of ﬁve acres. These minimums vary, but typically do have a minimum between two and
ﬁve acres. There are some potential areas identiﬁed that PUD could be an option for development or
redevelopment. Reducing our minimum would allow for more of these opportunities.

Initial changes included decreasing the minimum acres from ﬁve to two acres, changing the format submission
from triplicate to a format determined by Zoning Administrator (likely one full size hard copy and a digital
submission), also changing the process required for modiﬁcations to the plan by removing a public hearing
requirement for modiﬁcations of less than 10% increase in density or change in use of the site (public hearings still
could be held).

The allowance for commercial use has been added to the previous draft revisions after the last meeting’s discussion.
Staﬀ has drafted three criteria for limited commercial use: 1. The building shall be located no closer than one
hundred-ﬁfty feet (150') from the closest point of an adjacent residential zoning district, measured in a straight line
from the nearest point of the structure to the nearest point of the residential district. 2. The combined commercial
units may not exceed ten percent (10%) of the total units in the development and must be identiﬁed on the
development plan. 3. All potential uses of commercial units must be submitted as part of the development plan.
Only those approved will be considered permitted uses for the development.

Public hearing would be held at the March 11th meeting.
