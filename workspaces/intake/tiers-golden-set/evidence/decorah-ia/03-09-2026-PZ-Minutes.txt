Consider Resolu on Approving Preliminary Plat of Massman Second Addi on Subdivision

Goedken gave the background notes

Action required – City Code Section 16.04.090 states: “The commission shall, upon receiving the report of
the city engineer, as soon as possible but not more than forty-five days thereafter, consider the report,
negotiate with the subdivider on changes deemed advisable and the kind and extent of improvements to
be made by the subdivider and pass upon the preliminary plat as originally submitted or modified. If the
commission does not act within forty-five days, the preliminary plat shall be deemed to be approved;
provided, however, that the subdivider may agree to an extension of the time for a period not to exceed
an additional sixty days. The commission shall then set forth its recommendations in writing, whether of
approval, modification or disapproval.”

The City Engineer’s review of the proposed preliminary plat’s conformance with City Code is included in
the packet as Dropbox File 004D but are summarized below:
            1. Easements – Plan notes that “not all easements of record may not be shown”; final plat
                considered in the future should note all existing and proposed utility easements.
            2. Water and Sewer – Additional coordination with the city is needed to ensure adequate
                water pressure and avoid overloading the sewer system.
            3. SUDAS – Reference to SUDAS specifications shall be made on the cover sheet, Iowa DOT
                standards for street construction and related work is acceptable.
            4. Covenants – All covenants need to be incorporated in final plat (original submittal lacked
                summary, but covenants are now included as Dropbox File 004C)
            5. Street Extensions – Subdivision is isolated from adjacent residential neighborhoods.
                Code section 16.04.170.A.2 calls for “the arrangement of streets in a subdivision shall
                either provide for the continuation or appropriate projection of existing principal streets
                in surrounding areas.” The City may consider requiring street extensions as part of the
                subdivision approval process.
            6. Double Frontage Lots – Double frontage lots are shown along Locust Road and Clay Hill
                Road. 16.04.170.L.4 calls for “A planting screen easement of at least ten feet, across
                which there shall be no right of access, shall be provided along the line of lots abutting
                such a traffic artery or other disadvantageous use.” This should be included on the final
                plat as an easement.
            7. Sidewalks – Sidewalks are shown on one side of the street on sheet 10/22. The city may
                consider the need for sidewalks on both sides of the street or allowing single side
                sidewalks. The City may also consider the timing of sidewalk installation (i.e. during
                street construction or upon completion of house construction).
            8. Steet Construction Standard – City Engineer recommends use of 3” minus crushed rock or
                approved equal for paving base.
            9. Sidewalk Construction Standard – Sidewalk should be 5’ width minimum and have 6”
                thickness at driveways and street corner approaches to the sidewalk, including ADA
                landings.
            10. Street Signs – Signage per MUTCD shall be installed by developer.
            11. Stormwater Management Plan – Stormwater Management Plan needs to be provided to
                meet requirements of Chapter 13.44.

Consider Site Plan for Skyline Motorsports

Goedken gave the background notes
Action required – The City has received an application from Skyline Motor Sports for the construction of
an addition at 2333 Sweet Parkway Road. City Code Section 17.36.020 requires that the proposed
addition submit a site plan for review by the Planning and Zoning Commission and a recommendation
made to the City Council.

        The City Engineer has provided their review and the full letter is included in Dropbox as File 005F:
            1. Sidewalks – Sidewalks were not included in the Decorah Business Park Second
                Subdivision Preliminary Plats, although the paving planes include grade intended for
                future sidewalks. Sidewalks recently have been installed at developments along Sweet
                Parkway Road (i.e. Echo Development/Briar Grove Apartments and Viking Mountain
                Tools). Inclusion of a frontage sidewalk shall be considered by the City per Code Section
                12.28.030.
            2. Stormwater Management – No stormwater management plan was submitted, however,
                was included in the original submittal and addition is proposed on existing impervious
                surface area.
            3. Prohibited Storage of Motor Vehicles – City Code Section 17.16.100 prohibits the outdoor
                storage of motor vehicles not currently licensed, except motor vehicles held for sale by a
                licensed vehicle dealer and where permitted. This is meant to keep people from storing
                “junk” vehicles on property outside.
            4. Information Required – City Code Section 17.36.050 requires site plan to include names
                of all persons having an ownership interest. City Code Section 17.36.060 requires
                contour lines be shown on the site plan. Given the names are on applications submitted
                and the nature of the construction, this could be waived.

Staff recommends consideration of the Site Plan have no contingencies; except further discussion of the
Commission on item 1.
