C&C Auto’s Junkyard license was approved for six more months to provide additional time to dismantle the

On the motion of Supervisor Bell seconded by Supervisor Kensinger followed by 5-0 voice vote approval was
given for the proposal for garage door and closing the office on New Year’s Eve.

The manager reported on the completed fire study and the recommendation to establish one fire company
through merger/consolidation of Pinecroft & Excelsior Fire Companies. He further stressed that the
Township’s expectation is the fire companies work toward forming one fire company. We received a letter
from Excelsior formally notifying the township of their intent to work toward this goal. The Manager asked
what the intention is of the Pinecroft Volunteer Fire company. Joseph Addink commented Pinecroft could not
answer without additional information about the particulars of what a merged/consolidated fire company
would look like. Bill Muri responded that he could only commit to presenting a vote before Pinecroft members
to enter into discussions with Excelsior but not a commitment to forming one company at this time. Formal
notification will be provided to the Township on the outcome of the vote.

Supervisor Hornberger commented passionately about his feeling that both fire companies continue to serve
the township/borough and rejected the idea of a merged single fire company.
