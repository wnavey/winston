The April 3, 2025 Board of Supervisors meeting was called to order at 6:00 by Brian
Kustaborder followed by prayer and Pledge of Allegiance.

Public Comment Period

There were no public comments.

Meeting Minutes

The March 6, 2025 Board of Supervisor meeting minutes were approved on the motion of
Benjamin Hornberger, seconded by Susan Kensinger followed by a 5-0 voice vote.

Supervisors Reports

Sue Kensinger reported that the library is hosting a Phil MCCaully dance as a fundraiser.

Manager’s Report

The manager reported that we have initiated the process to place the General Fund into a
sweep account in order to maximize our internet earnings.

Ordinances regarding air pollution and source water protection are forthcoming.

Code Enforcement Report

Shawn Wolfe provided an update on the Kevin Taylor and Stoy properties. Staff was directed
to get quotes to have the Taylor property cleaned up and a lien placed against the property.


Engineer’s Report

The Twp Engineer had nothing to report.

Planning Commission

The Bickel’s Surplus Too Land Development(Property Code: 03.00-23.0021.05-000) was
tabled due to additional stormwater planning needed for a gravel lot that was not included on
the plans

Approval was given on the motion of Supervisor Hornberger, seconded by Supervisor
Winterstein followed by a 5-0 voice voter the Blair Frito Solar land development review period
was extended indefinitely.

On the motion of Supervisor Hornberger, seconded by Supervisor Kensinger followed by a 5-0
voice vote the DeGol Land Development was withdrawn.

Treasurer Reports

The March 2025 Treasurers Report and bills in the amount of $60,410.73 from the General
Fund, $10,816.72 from the Capital Reserve Fund, $500 from the Stormwater Improvement
were approved on the motion of Supervisor Winterstein, seconded by Supervisor Kensinger
followed by a 5-0 roll call vote.

Authorization was given on the motion of Supervisor Bell, seconded by Supervisor Winterstein
followed by a 5-0 voice vote to transfer $122,208.78 in first quarter EIT to the Capital reserve
Fund, transfer $20K from the General Fund to the Stormwater Improvement Fund and to
release $11,474.17 to the Conservation District for our contribution to the ISC.

Authorization was given to release half of our contribution to the library in the amount of
$18,375, on the motion of Supervisor Kensinger, seconded by Supervisor Winterstein followed
by a 5-0 voice vote.

New Business

The sealed bids for the aggregate material were opened at 1:00 on April 3rd with no bidders
present.

A motion to award the Aggregate Material Bid to New Enterprise Stone & Lime who submitted
the low bid of $74,327.50 was made by Supervisor Bell, seconded by Supervisor Kensinger
by a 5-0 vote. The bid tabulation is made part of these minutes.

The bid for the park/trail paving project was awarded to Laurel Asphalt on the motion of
Supervisor Winterstein seconded, by Supervisor Bell followed by a 5-0 voice vote.
Given that no bids were received for the recycling structure, authorization was given for a
second advertisement on the motion of Supervisor Winterstein seconded by Supervisor
Hornberger followed by a 5-0 voice vote.

On the motion of Supervisor Kensinger, seconded by Supervisor Bell followed by a 5-0 voice
vote authorization was given to naming a street located along Grandview Road Deer Valley
Lane as requested by Richard Himes.

On the motion of Supervisor Winterstein seconded by Supervisor Kensinger followed by a 5-0
voice vote, authorization was given to accept the repository bid submitted by Frank
Walendrewisz in the amount of $50 for property located along Kerbaugh Road.


PUBLIC SAFETY

The Fire Companies call list and financials were reviewed as well as Excelsior’s strategic plan
review. The Manager provided an overview of the contribution calculations. On the motion
of Supervisor Bell seconded by Supervisor Winterstein followed by a 5-0 voice vote,
authorization was given to release the contributions contingent upon a satisfactory review of
the financials and evidence that work is being done toward a merger. Supervisor Hornberger
noted that he is not in agreement with the contingencies.

Public Comment Period

There were no public comments.

The board adjourned at 8:04 PM.
