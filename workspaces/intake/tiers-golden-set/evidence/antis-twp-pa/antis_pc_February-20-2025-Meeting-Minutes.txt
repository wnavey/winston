4. Minor Subdivision/Lot Line Changes

Hess and Ryan Lot Line Relocation / Merger – This submission proposes to reconfigure the common lot
line between the referenced parcels. Area “A” containing 0.500 acres, is to be conveyed from the Ryan’s
and merged with the adjoining lands of Hess. It is also the intention of the Hess’s to merge Area “A” and
two tax parcels into one contiguous lot, eliminating the interior lot lines and associated building setback
lines. The combined Hess land will contain 1.165 acres and the remaining Ryan land will contain
approximately 9.44 acres.

Antis Township has received the Blair County Planning Commission approval letter. The outstanding
issues that needed to be addressed from the January 10, 2025 Engineers review letter were revised.
Signed plans were received by the Township.

            o   Action Taken- A recommendation was made for conditional approval of the Hess and
                Ryan Lot Line Relocation / Merger subject to all engineering comments being addressed
                in the Engineers Review Letter dated January 10, 2025. This motion was made by Todd
                Holes, seconded by Charles Taylor and a vote was called by Chairman Norman Saylor.
                The plan was recommended for approval by a 4-0 vote.
5. Land Development Proposals

Blair Frito Solar Land Development - The proposed Principal Solar Energy System (PSES), which consists
of three (3) separate solar arrays, is located on a privately owned parcel (Parcel ID 03.00-23..-026.00-
000) of land in Antis Township, Blair County, Pennsylvania. The existing land conditions are comprised of
low-density development and forest land. The Projects will consist of the construction of solar arrays
and associated infrastructure such as access roads, electrical collection lines, fencing, and signage. The
Projects will be developed in three (3) separate solar arrays with fencing enclosing each array and the
entirety of the development area.

Jay West and Emily Zapinski (representing Penn Renewables) were in attendance to discuss any
questions the planning commission may have on the proposed solar development. Charles Taylor had a
few questions regarding the Final Application that was submitted regarding the Financial Security box
which needs revised and a brief conversation was had on the Solar Ordinance that was recently
approved last year by the Board of Supervisors. Todd Holes provided a brief summary of the next steps
once the Township receives the first engineers review letter on the plans and also receipt of the county
review letter. Antis Township is waiting on the first engineers review letter and the Blair County
Planning Commission Review at this time.

            o   Action Taken- No action was taken on this proposal to allow for the Township Engineer
                to submit his first review on the plans and for the Blair County Planning Commission to
                submit their review along with all other outstanding issues to be addressed.

Bickel’s Surplus Too Warehouse Final Land Development - Bickel’s surplus is proposing their Final Land
Development Plans for the newly constructed warehouse on the existing property. The proposed plan
includes a warehouse that is 11,800 square feet with an access for trucks. The site is located off of Old
6th Avenue Road. The proposed building will include a water well and septic holding tank. Stormwater
management for the property include underground stormwater management facilities.

Antis Township is in receipt of the first Engineers review letter dated February 19, 2025. Antis Township
is waiting on a revised plan addressing the outstanding comments on the review letter. Charles Taylor
asked why there was no certification of all the improvements being built according to the plan
submitted per the Subdivision and Land Development (SALDO) requirement. Staff will ask Township
Engineer and Bickel’s Engineer this question to follow up.

            o   Action Taken- No action was taken on this proposal to allow for Bickel’s Engineer to
                address the outstanding issues from the Township’s engineer review letter dated
                February 19, 2025.
