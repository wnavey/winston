4. Minor & Major Subdivision/Lot Line Changes

Patton Minor Subdivision - Stephen L. & Deborah S. Patton of P.O. Box 282 Tipton, Pennsylvania, are
the owners of a 0.95-acre parcel of land located near the terminus of Township Road 708 (Spruce Street)
in the Tipton area of Antis Township, Blair County.
It is the intention of Patton’s at this time to subdivide their parcel of land into two lots, shown as Lots 1
& 2.
Proposed Lot 2 (0.303 acres) is intended to be conveyed to their daughter (Faith) who intends to
construct a single-family dwelling and connect to the public utilities.
Lot 1 the residual lands of Patton’s will contain 0.648 acres with an existing single-family dwelling
connected to the public utilities.
Antis Township’s Engineer has provided one review letter dated September 30, 2025. Antis Township
has received a revised plan that has satisfied the comments in the first engineers review letter. Antis
Township is still waiting on the Blair County Planning Commission review.
A brief discussion was had on the overall plan. A sewage planning exemption mailer was presented due
to public utilities being present for this parcel being proposed.
              o Action Taken – a recommendation was made for the Antis Township Supervisors to sign
                  the exemption mailer. This motion was made by Todd Holes, seconded by Seth Hunter
                  and a vote was called by Chairman Norman Saylor. The authorization was recommended
                  on a 5-0 vote.


Alfred and Mary Ingham Major Subdivision – Alfred E. & Mary E. Ingham of 156 Almary Lane, Dysart,
Pennsylvania are the respective owners of 86.7 acres of land located along State Route 1016 near the
Antis/Logan Township and Blair/Cambria County Line.
There have been five conveyances from the parent tract (Tax Parcel 3-18-12) since May of 1972.These
conveyances are shown as Lots 2 through 6 for catch up purposes with Lot 1 being the residual tract.
It is now the intention of the Ingham’s to create Proposed Lot 7. Proposed Lot 7 will contain 10.500
acres with an existing apartment building to be remodeled into a single-family home, utilizing an existing
on-lot sewage disposal system and well.

Antis Township’s Engineer has provided one review letter dated September 2, 2025. Antis Township has
received a revised plan that has satisfied the comments in the first engineers review letter. Antis
Township’s Solicitor has approved of the language that is in the shared use and maintenance
agreement. The Blair County Planning Commission has approved the plans and provided comments. One
non-build form has been presented to the Blair County Sewage Enforcement Officer and has been
approved. The non-build sewage waiver was submitted to DEP for review.

A brief discussion was had on the comments that were received through Blair Planning’s approval letter.
Mainly the comments about municipal cooperation as this tract of land falls on the Logan Township
Antis Township line. Township staff has communicated with Logan Township and they did not see any
reason for the plans to be presented to Logan for approval. Another discussion that was brought up was
the private road language and if all of the parcels had public road frontage. Lot 3’s road frontage was
questioned and a brief discussion was had of where the legal access was on the plans.

            o   Action Taken - a recommendation was made to approve the Alfred and Mary Ingham
                Major Subdivision contingent upon the non-build waiver being approved by DEP. This
                motion was made by Todd Holes, seconded by Thomas DelMastro and a vote was called
                by Chairman Norman Saylor. The Alfred and Mary Ingham Major Subdivision was
                recommended for approval contingent upon receipt of approval through DEP on the non-
                build waiver by a 5-0 vote.

5. Land Development Proposals

Fornari Dog Daycare Final Land Development - James Fornari is planning to construct a dog daycare
located along Sabbath Rest Road S.R. 4018 in Antis Township, Blair County, PA. The project will include
the construction of a new building, access road, parking lot, necessary grading, and the extension of
utilities. The proposed dog daycare will get water service and sanitary sewer service from the Altoona
Water Authority
The runoff from the project site flows west to a ditch that flows into Sandy Run. As a result of the
proposed construction and resulting surface cover condition changes, there is a net increase in the peak
rate of stormwater runoff. This peak rate increase will be mitigated with the installation of an infiltration
basin onsite.
The Limit of Disturbance is less than 1 acre; therefore, an Individual NPDES permit is not required.

A brief discussion was had on the Fornari Dog Daycare Final Land Development. Antis Township is
waiting on the First Township Engineer Review Letter. The Blair County Planning Commission has
received the plans to review as well.

            o     Action Taken – No action was taken on this proposal so the outstanding issues could be
                  addressed.
Riggles Gap Telecommunication Tower Land Development - The subject tract is located at 860 Riggles
Gap Road in Antis Township, Blair County. The site is approximately 4.02 acres in size and consists of an
existing accessory building, and a mix of lawn, woods, and agricultural fields. The proposed development
of this site includes the construction of a telecommunication tower on a 50’ x 50’ gravel pad and a
driveway to access the site from Riggle Gap Road.

A brief discussion was had on the Riggles Gap Telecommunication Project that was submitted. Antis
Township is waiting on the First Township Engineer Review Letter. The Blair County Planning
Commission has received the plans to review as well.

            o   Action Taken – No action was taken on this proposal so the outstanding issues could be
                addressed.

Blair Frito Solar Land Development - The proposed Principal Solar Energy System (PSES), which consists
of three (3) separate solar arrays, is located on a privately owned parcel (Parcel ID 03.00-23..-026.00-
000) of land in Antis Township, Blair County, Pennsylvania. The existing land conditions are comprised of
low-density development and forest land. The Projects will consist of the construction of solar arrays
and associated infrastructure such as access roads, electrical collection lines, fencing, and signage. The
Projects will be developed in three (3) separate solar arrays with fencing enclosing each array and the
entirety of the development area.

Antis Township is waiting on a review for the updated Blair County Conservation paperwork through
DEP. Antis Township is waiting on our engineer to review the revised plans submitted to address the
comments in the June 5, 2025 review letter. An NPDES permit also needs to be accepted by DEP.

            o   Taken- No action was taken on this proposal so the outstanding issues could be
                addressed.
