                                                                                     Application Checklist
                                                                                      Site Development Plan
This Checklist is intended to provide the information and data needed to constitute a complete application. A request for a
Site Development Plan requires review by staff and approval, in accordance with UDC Section 3.09 and Ordinance 2019-50.
For assistance, please contact the Planner of the Day at (512) 930-3575 or planning@georgetowntexas.gov

Note: See UDC 3.09.080.
Record Change/Record Drawing Change*: For a Record Change/Record Drawing Change to the approved Site Development
Plan, a new application, case number, and a submittal to MGO will NOT be required. For directions on submitting a Record
Change/Record Drawing Change, please see Record Drawing SOP guide.

Site Development Plan, Minor Revision*: For a Minor Revision to a Site Development Plan, a submission to MGO will be
needed, but a new application and new case number will NOT be required. Please submit to the existing application through
MGO. A fee will be assessed in accordance with the City’s adopted fee schedule.

Site Development Plan Amendment*: For an Amendment to an approved Site Development Plan, a new application, a new
case number, and new submittal to MGO WILL be required (please select Site Development Plan Amendment as the Sub-
Case Type). An Amendment will NOT be reviewed/considered under the approved Site Development Plan. Reviews of the
application will be completed in accordance with City procedures for new applications.

*For criteria on what constitutes a Record Change/Record Drawing Change, Minor Revision, or Amendment to a Site
Development Plan, please visit SDP Revisions Criteria guide.

                                            Digital Submission Requirements

New applications are only accepted digitally in accordance with our posted submittal dates within the Development
Manual. The application submittal calendar is available within the Development Manual on the home page of our website
here: https://www.georgetowntexas.gov/development_services/planning/information/development_manual/index.php

The City of Georgetown utilizes a digital review system called MGOConnect, which requires certain digital submittal
standards. For more detailed information and troubleshooting, please review the separate MGOConnect User Guides on
our website: www.georgetowntexas.gov/planning .
