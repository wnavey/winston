Welcome to the Community Development Office!
I’m Jessica Crouch, and I’m here to help make Paris a more vibrant, safe, and beautiful place to live, work, and visit. Through thoughtful planning and community partnerships, I work to support smart growth, preserve our unique charm, and plan for a bright future.
Whether you’re a resident, business owner, or developer, I’m here to support your vision and strengthen our shared community!
