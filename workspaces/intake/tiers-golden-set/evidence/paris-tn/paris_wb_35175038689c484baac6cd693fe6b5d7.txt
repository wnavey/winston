NEW BUSINESS
AGENDA ITEM NO. 1: Site Plan – Cornerstone Medical – Tyson and Cornerstone Drive

      DISCUSSION: Jennifer Morris, Community Development Director, stated that Cornerstone
      Medical submitted a site plan to construct a 1,601 sq. ft. addition to the surgery center
      building. Ms. Morris stated that this proposed addition will be utilized for storage-related
      purposes and will not be used for patient care or office space. Ms. Morris stated that due to
      the use of the proposed addition, it would not create additional parking requirements. Ms.
      Morris stated that this piece of property is less than half an acre and did not require an
      engineer to review. Ms. Morris stated that the addition will be replacing asphalt and concrete
      surfaces that it will not create additional impervious surfaces.

      ACTION: Motion made by Commissioner Gayle Griffith, seconded by Commissioner Kathy
      Ray to approve the site plan for Cornerstone Medical and Tyson and Cornerstone Drive.

      VOTE: Unanimous
NEW BUSINESS
AGENDA ITEM NO. 2: Site Plan – HCHS Wellness Center – South Wilson Street and Harding
Road

      DISCUSSION: Jennifer Morris, Community Development Director, stated that Henry County
      Board of Education submitted a site plan to construct a wellness center to be constructed at
      the southwest corner of the football field on the HCHS campus. Ms. Morris stated that all
      utilities have been reviewed. Ms. Morris also stated that the reviewing engineer had sent back
      the following comments to be addressed:

             1. Currently the proposed arch pipe shows an invert elevation out higher than the
                invert elevation in, this should be addressed on the revised site plan.
             2. There is less than 12” of cover on the proposed 18” CMP, verify the load capacity
                on the revised site plan.

      Bob Safin, TLM Engineer, stated that this proposed addition will be utilized by the school only
      and will have bathrooms on one end for use during football games. Mr. Safin stated that a
      detention pond will be added once the building is built.

      ACTION: Motion made by Commissioner Jackie Jones, seconded by Commissioner Tara
      Wilson to approve the site plan contingent upon the correction of the referenced above
      comments from the reviewing engineer.

      VOTE: Unanimous
NEW BUSINESS
AGENDA ITEM NO. 3: Site Plan – Patriot Mart – Hwy 641 and Hwy 218

      DISCUSSION: Jennifer Morris, Community Development Director, stated that Jay Sukhadia
      submitted a site plan to construct a 6,240 sq. ft. Patriot Mart (convenience store) at the corner
      of Highway 641 South and Highway 218. Ms. Morris stated that additionally a detention pond
      is proposed and that the only entrance is shown on Highway 218. Ms. Morris stated that the
      reviewing engineer, OHM, has reviewed the plans and LI Smith has submitted the corrections.
      Mr. Jay Sukhadia stated that he has spoken with TDOT, and he has a tentative permit upon
      payment of the bond and submitting insurance. Ms. Morris addressed the concern of trash
      going into the creek by placing a fence along the rear side of the property. Mr. Sukhadia stated
      that he would add a fence to the rear of the property.

      ACTION: Motion made by Commissioner Tara Wilson, seconded by Commissioner Kathy Ray
      to approve the site plan contingent upon the permit from TDOT.

      VOTE: Unanimous
NEW BUSINESS
AGENDA ITEM NO. 4: Minor Site Plan – Castle Car Wash – Mineral Wells Avenue

      DISCUSSION: Jennifer Morris, Community Development Director, stated that Castle Car
      Wash submitted a minor site plan at 1066 Mineral Wells Avenue to demolish a portion of the
      existing structure to install 8 vacuum bays. A representative for Castle Car Wash was present
      and showed the board a model of the proposed vacuum bays to be placed onsite. Ms. Morris
      stated this was placed on the agenda for review and approval since this will not create a
      requirement for more parking or alter the traffic flow.

      ACTION: Motion made by Commissioner Gayle Griffith, seconded by Commissioner Kathy
      Ray to approve the site plan contingent

      VOTE: Unanimous
NEW BUSINESS
AGENDA ITEM NO. 5: Planning and Zoning Zoom Training

       DISCUSSION: Jennifer Morris, Community Development Director, stated an Intro to Planning
       training by MTAS will be available via Zoom on May 3rd from 5:00 p.m. until 7:00 p.m. Ms.
       Morris stated that this was a great opportunity for Commissioners to obtain training for both
       Planning Commission and Board of Zoning Appeals. All commissioners stated that they would
       be available to attend the zoom training at the City Hall Courtroom.

       ACTION: None

       VOTE: None




Upon a motion by Commissioner Kathy Ray, second by Commissioner Tara Wilson, the meeting was
duly adjourned at 5:47 p.m.
