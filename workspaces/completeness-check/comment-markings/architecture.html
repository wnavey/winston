COMPLETENESS CHECK · COMMENT MARKINGS

Comment markings: how the review runbook does it, and porting it to the completeness check

Markings are points and small polygons, normalised 0–1 over a plan sheet, that show where a comment's problem is on the drawing. This page traces them through the review runbook, the database and the app, then proposes the same feature for the CC runbook. Companion to DESIGN-SPEC.md.

OverviewFive questions, short answers

The review runbook marks comments in a separate step that runs after the final record. Publish stores the marks inside each comment's JSON, and the app draws them only for review formats that route to its August adapter. The CC lacks all three of those pieces.

#QuestionAnswer today (review runbook)
1Plan-set sheets only?Yes. Every marker names a staged plan-set sheet ("03"). Publish fails the run if that label is missing from sheet_map. The app draws only over the sheet_version rasters of one plan set. A comment about a document is recorded as a sheetless nothing-to-mark. v2 adds document pages for CC (see the Documents tab).
2Needs pre-processing?Not for the geometry. Shapes are read off pixels of the staged sheet PDF and fingerprinted with sheet_sha256. The runbook around it does need a preprocessed submission, because staging lays out the transcriptions. The stager fetches block boxes but never writes them to disk. Display needs cityhall's own sheet rasters.
3Different output schema?Yes. Comment output_json gains annotations[] and annotation_disposition, and the review row is stamped 2026-09-annotated with a sheet_map. The app chooses its adapter by output_schema. The CC adapter hardcodes annotations: [].
4Same step as findings?No. 3.13-annotate runs after 3.11-final: a judgment sweep (one worker per discipline) writes sidecar files, then a script merges them into a copy of the record.
5Proposal for CC?A new 2.12-annotate family after 2.9-comments that never adjudicates. It marks every run that voted fail or warn, not only the winning one, and stores each run's markers on that run. The comment shows the winning run's markers. It reuses the review's scripts once they move to shared code, and seeds from the cited block's box. Lane B validates the markers and adds sheet_map while keeping the CC label, and the cityhall CC adapter reads the markers. Opt-in at first.
