**Deed Restrictions:** Not retrievable via web research. Early Austin subdivisions like Trigg Addition typically had deed restrictions that may have included use limitations, building setbacks, and (historically) racially restrictive covenants (the latter are unenforceable post-Shelley v. - The subsequent resubdivision (Doc. No. 201800004)
- The condominium declaration (Doc. No. 2018119069)
- Changes in zoning and land use over the decades
- Texas Property Code provisions on deed restriction enforcement

**Area Context:** The Trigg Addition area along Cameron Road has evolved from a semi-rural subdivision into a commercial/industrial corridor. The Cameron Road / Dessau Road Safety Project by the City of Austin indicates ongoing infrastructure investment in this corridor.

**Citation:** Concept plan reference to Volume 68, Page 79, P.R.T.C.T.; TCAD neighborhood designation; City of Austin Cameron Road / Dessau Road Safety Project.

**Development Implication:** Original Trigg Addition deed restrictions (if any survive) should be reviewed but are unlikely to materially constrain development given (a) the property's CS zoning, (b) the 2018 resubdivision and condominium declaration that would have been reviewed for compliance, and (c) the long history of commercial/industrial use. A title search should confirm whether any original restrictions remain of record and are enforceable.

**Confidence:** LOW. The actual content of the Trigg Addition plat and any associated deed restrictions was not accessible via web research. The Travis County Clerk's Digital Archives may contain the original plat given its historical nature.
