## 1. Neighborhood Plan

### Finding

The parcel at 9101 Cameron Rd falls within the **St. John/Coronado Hills Combined Neighborhood Planning Area (SJCHCNPA)**. The plan was adopted by Austin City Council on **April 26, 2012**, via Ordinance No. 201204026-100 (case NP-2011-0029). It is an amendment to the Austin Tomorrow Comprehensive Plan (now superseded in part by Imagine Austin).

Cameron Road serves as the approximate boundary between the St. John neighborhood (to the west) and the Coronado Hills neighborhood (to the east). The plan addresses land use, community life, recreation, transportation, and implementation across both sub-areas.

The plan's land use chapter supports maintaining existing commercial uses along major corridors while encouraging a balanced mix of uses. The plan emphasizes preserving housing affordability and creating commercial activity nodes.

Note: The parcel is **not** within the North Lamar Combined Neighborhood Planning Area (Georgian Acres), which lies further north and west, bounded by US-183, I-35, Braker Lane, and North Lamar Blvd. That plan was adopted June 24, 2010 and does not cover the 9101 Cameron Rd location.

### Citation

- [St. John/Coronado Hills Combined Neighborhood Plan (PDF)](https://www.austintexas.gov/sites/default/files/files/Housing_&_Planning/Adopted%20Neighborhood%20Planning%20Areas/28_StJohn-CoronadoHillsCombined/sjch-np.pdf)
- [St. John/Coronado Hills FLUM (PDF)](https://www.austintexas.gov/sites/default/files/files/Housing/FLUM/St_John_Coronado_Hills_Combined_FLUM.pdf)

- Planning Commission staff report for NPA-2022-0029.01 (7601 Cameron Road) confirming SJCHCNPA boundaries

### Development Implication

The parcel is governed by an adopted neighborhood plan. Any zoning change that conflicts with the plan's Future Land Use Map requires a Neighborhood Plan Amendment (NPA), which must go through the Planning Commission and City Council. However, for a use that conforms to the existing CS base zoning and FLUM designation, no NPA should be required.

### Confidence Level

**High.** The SJCHCNPA plan area is confirmed through multiple City of Austin staff reports referencing properties on Cameron Road in this vicinity (7601 Cameron Rd zoning case, C14-2022-0094). The plan adoption date and ordinance number are documented in public records.

## 2. Future Land Use Map (FLUM)

### Finding

The FLUM for the St. John/Coronado Hills Combined Neighborhood Plan designates the Cameron Road corridor in this vicinity as **"Commercial."**

The City of Austin's FLUM categories map to specific zoning districts. The "Commercial" FLUM designation supports zoning districts including **CS (General Commercial Services)** and **GR (Community Commercial)**. The parcel's existing CS zoning is consistent with the Commercial FLUM designation.

A 2022 staff report for the property at 7601 Cameron Road (approximately 1,500 feet south of the subject parcel) confirmed that parcels along Cameron Road in the SJCHCNPA carry a "Commercial" FLUM designation. That case sought to change the FLUM from Commercial to Mixed Use, requiring an NPA -- further evidence that the default designation for the corridor is Commercial.

An AutoZone retail store (automotive parts sales) is a permitted use within the CS zoning district. A retail use on CS-zoned land with a Commercial FLUM designation is **conforming**.

### Citation

- [St. John/Coronado Hills Combined FLUM (PDF)](https://www.austintexas.gov/sites/default/files/files/Housing/FLUM/St_John_Coronado_Hills_Combined_FLUM.pdf)
- Planning Commission case NPA-2022-0029.01 staff report: "Current FLUM: Commercial Base District; Proposed: Mixed Use"
- [Austin Land Use and Zoning Matrix (PDF)](https://www.austintexas.gov/sites/default/files/files/Planning/LandUse_Zoning-Combined.pdf)

### Development Implication

The proposed AutoZone retail use on CS-zoned land aligns with the Commercial FLUM designation. No Neighborhood Plan Amendment is anticipated to be required for this project. This removes a significant procedural hurdle and potential source of delay.

### Confidence Level

**High.** The Commercial FLUM designation for Cameron Road parcels is confirmed by City staff reports in recent (2022) zoning cases on the same corridor within the SJCHCNPA. CS zoning is an accepted zoning district under the Commercial FLUM category.

## 3. Imagine Austin Comprehensive Plan

### Finding

Cameron Road is designated as an **Imagine Austin Activity Corridor** on the Growth Concept Map. City staff reports further identify Cameron Road as a **"future core transit corridor."**

The parcel at 9101 Cameron Rd is located less than 1/4 mile from the **Cameron & 183 Job Center**, an Imagine Austin-designated center that provides employment opportunities and commercial service options. This Job Center designation reinforces the commercial character of the immediate area.

Activity corridors under Imagine Austin are described as "connections that link activity centers and other key destinations to one another and are characterized by a variety of activities and types of buildings located along the roadway -- shopping, restaurants and cafes, parks, schools, single-family houses, apartments, public buildings, houses of worship, mixed-use buildings, and offices."

City staff have consistently found that commercial and mixed-use development along Cameron Road is consistent with the Imagine Austin activity corridor designation. In the 7601 Cameron Road case, staff stated: "the property fronts on Cameron Road, an activity corridor where mixed use is appropriate."

- [Imagine Austin Growth Concept Map App (ArcGIS)](https://www.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=3c602527fd7146129d14aad9db223959)

### Development Implication

The Imagine Austin activity corridor designation supports commercial development along Cameron Road. A retail auto-parts store is consistent with the corridor's commercial character, though the comprehensive plan's long-term vision leans toward walkable, mixed-use intensification. The single-use, auto-oriented character of an AutoZone store (large parking lot, drive-up layout) is less aligned with the aspirational mixed-use vision but is not inconsistent with the current corridor reality. Staff have approved similar-intensity commercial uses along this corridor.

### Confidence Level

**High.** Multiple City staff reports explicitly confirm Cameron Road as an Imagine Austin activity corridor and future core transit corridor. The Cameron & 183 Job Center is documented in official Imagine Austin datasets.

## 4. Station-Area Plan / Transit-Oriented Development

### Finding

The **North Lamar Transit Center (NLTC)** is located at 8001 US-183, approximately **1.3 miles west-northwest** of 9101 Cameron Rd. The NLTC Station Area Vision Plan was adopted by City Council on **May 22, 2025** (CPA-2025-0001). The vision plan encompasses a half-mile radius around the transit center and envisions equitable transit-oriented development including affordable housing, enhanced walkability, and multimodal connectivity.

The half-mile radius of the NLTC Station Area Vision Plan does **not extend to 9101 Cameron Rd**, which is well outside the plan boundary. The subject parcel is approximately 0.8 miles beyond the eastern edge of the NLTC half-mile radius.

There is **no TOD overlay zoning** applied to the subject parcel. Austin's existing TOD overlay districts were adopted in anticipation of MetroRail Red Line service and are concentrated along that alignment (northwest Austin to downtown), not along Cameron Road.

Cameron Road is served by **CapMetro Route 325 (Metric/Rundberg)** and is identified as a future core transit corridor, but no Project Connect light rail or MetroRapid stations are currently planned at or near 9101 Cameron Rd.

CapMetro is developing a **North Base operations facility** at 10805 Cameron Rd (approximately 1.7 miles north of the subject parcel), which will include Cameron Road intersection improvements but is not a passenger station.

### Citation

- [North Lamar Transit Center Station Area Vision Plan -- CPA-2025-0001](https://bandc.crccheck.com/planning-commission/447737-32-cpa-2025-0001-north-lamar-transit-center-station-area-vision-plan-part-1/)
- [City Council approves NLTC and South Congress Station Area Vision Plans (May 2025)](https://www.austintexas.gov/news/city-envisions-equitable-transformation-two-key-mobility-hubs)
- [CapMetro TOD Overview](https://www.capmetro.org/plans-development/tod)
- [North Lamar Transit Center -- Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Lamar_Transit_Center)

### Development Implication

The subject parcel is not within any adopted station-area plan or TOD overlay. No additional design standards, density requirements, or affordable housing mandates arising from transit-oriented development plans apply. However, the "future core transit corridor" designation for Cameron Road could eventually lead to transit improvements that trigger new planning requirements. For now, this is not a constraint.

### Confidence Level

**High** for the absence of TOD overlay at the subject parcel. **Medium** for future transit planning, as Project Connect is still evolving and additional MetroRapid or bus rapid transit routes could be added along Cameron Road.

## 5. Neighborhood Association(s)

### Finding

The primary registered neighborhood association covering the subject parcel area is the **Coronado Hills/Creekside Neighborhood Association (CH/CNA)**.

- **Boundaries:** US-183, US-290 East, and Cameron Road
- **Mailing address:** PO Box 16384, Austin, TX 78761
- **Website:** [www.chcrna.com](http://www.chcrna.com/)
- **Meetings:** Monthly, third Tuesday at 6:30 PM, at Windsor Park Library (and via Google Meet)

The **St. John Neighborhood Association** also covers portions of the SJCHCNPA west of Cameron Road and may claim notification interest for properties fronting Cameron Road.

The CH/CNA has been actively engaged in Cameron Road development cases. In the 2022 rezoning case for 7601 Cameron Road, the association:
- Recommended uses that "provide the community the most benefit AND protect the valuable natural spaces that are dwindling in their neighborhood"
- Emphasized that Cameron Road development should "include options that would make traffic along Cameron Road safer for all"
- Advocated for preservation of "permeable land and urban forest"
- Raised concerns about traffic safety, environmental impacts, and loss of open space

The association's posture toward commercial development is **conditionally supportive** -- they do not oppose commercial uses outright but advocate for site design that addresses traffic safety, stormwater/permeability, and tree preservation. An auto-parts retail store with a conventional big-box layout and large parking lot could draw scrutiny on impervious cover and tree removal.

### Citation

- [Coronado Hills/Creekside Neighborhood Association](http://www.chcrna.com/)
- [Austin Community Registry Search](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/community-registry-search)
- Planning Commission case C14-2022-0094 (7601 Cameron Road) -- CH/CNA comments

### Development Implication

The applicant should expect notification to the CH/CNA (and potentially the St. John NA) under Austin's neighborhood notification requirements. Early engagement with the CH/CNA is advisable. Key concerns to address proactively: traffic impacts on Cameron Road, impervious cover, tree preservation, and site drainage. The association is organized, active, and familiar with the development review process.

### Confidence Level

**High** for CH/CNA as the relevant association. **Medium** for predicting their specific response to this project -- their documented positions suggest conditional engagement rather than blanket opposition, but their response will depend on project specifics.

## 6. Cameron Road Corridor Plans

### Finding

There is **no separately adopted corridor plan or special district overlay** specific to Cameron Road. Cameron Road does not have a Corridor Development Program (CDP) designation, a Vertical Mixed Use (VMU) overlay by default, or a Capitol View Corridor overlay.

However, Cameron Road is the subject of the **Cameron Road / Dessau Road Safety Project**, a City of Austin transportation project proposing safety and mobility improvements along the 6.5-mile corridor from 51st Street to Parmer Lane. The project originated from a 2019 community listening session and public survey. Construction was expected to begin in spring 2025. Improvements include:
- Intersection safety enhancements
- Turn lane widening
- New traffic control devices
- Pedestrian and bicycle safety features

Individual properties along Cameron Road carry **Conditional Overlay (CO)** restrictions in some cases, which modify permitted uses and impose site-specific requirements (e.g., vegetative buffers, traffic studies). Whether the subject parcel has a CO would need to be verified against the zoning map for Parcel 928312 specifically.

Some Cameron Road properties have been rezoned to add **VMU (Vertical Mixed Use)** overlays through individual zoning cases (e.g., 7601 Cameron Road). These are property-specific, not corridor-wide.

### Citation

- [Cameron Road / Dessau Road Safety Project (StoryMap)](https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/b8bec12a854d45c9b8b4721ff73f5915)
- [Cameron/Dessau Road project moves forward -- Austin Monitor (Oct 2024)](https://austinmonitor.com/stories/2024/10/cameron-dessau-road-project-moves-forward-refined/)

### Development Implication

No corridor-wide plan creates additional requirements beyond base zoning. However, the Cameron Road/Dessau Road Safety Project may affect right-of-way, access points, or frontage conditions at the subject parcel. The applicant should coordinate with Austin Transportation to confirm whether improvements are planned at or near 9101 Cameron Road that could affect site access, driveway placement, or required dedications.

### Confidence Level

**High** for the absence of a formal corridor plan overlay. **Medium** for the transportation project impacts -- the safety project covers a long corridor and specific impacts at 9101 Cameron Rd would depend on the detailed engineering plans.

## 7. Historic District

### Finding

There is **no local historic district, National Register historic district, or individual historic landmark designation** affecting the subject parcel at 9101 Cameron Rd or the immediate Cameron Road corridor in the 78753/78754 area.

Austin's locally designated historic districts and National Register historic districts are concentrated in central Austin (downtown, Old West Austin, Hyde Park, Travis Heights, Wilshire Wood, etc.). The Cameron Road corridor in north-central Austin is a post-war commercial/suburban area with no historic district designations.

The City of Austin Historic Preservation Office maintains a [Historic Property Viewer](https://www.austintexas.gov/planning/historic-preservation) that can confirm the absence of any individual landmark designation on the specific parcel.

### Citation

- [Austin Historic Districts -- AustinTexas.gov](https://www.austintexas.gov/planning/historic-districts)
- [Properties in National Register Historic Districts (PDF)](https://www.austintexas.gov/sites/default/files/files/Planning/Historic_Preservation/nhrd_property_list.pdf)
- [Historic Preservation -- AustinTexas.gov](https://www.austintexas.gov/planning/historic-preservation)

### Development Implication

No historic preservation review, certificate of appropriateness, or demolition delay applies. This removes a potential source of delay and design constraint. Standard demolition permits (if applicable for existing improvements) should proceed through normal channels.

### Confidence Level

**High.** No historic districts exist in this part of Austin, and the property's 1985 construction date and commercial character make individual landmark designation highly unlikely.
