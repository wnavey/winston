## 1. Water Service

**Provider:** Austin Water (city-owned utility)

9101 Cameron Rd is within the Austin Water full-purpose service area. The concept plan confirms substantial existing water infrastructure on-site: a 36-inch transmission main, a 14-inch distribution main, and an 8-inch line. These sizes are well in excess of what a 7,375 SF retail store would require; a typical commercial retail connection ties into an 8-inch or larger main.

**Pressure Zone:** Austin Water operates nine pressure zones corresponding to the city's topography. The Cameron Rd / 78753 corridor in north Austin is in the lower-to-mid elevation range, generally served by the Central North (CN) pressure zone. The exact zone designation should be confirmed via Austin Water's interactive ArcGIS pressure zone map or by contacting Austin Water directly.

**Tap/Connection Fees (FY 2025-26):**
- Water impact fee (capital recovery fee) for lots platted after October 1, 2023: **$4,800 per equivalent service unit** (water portion)
- Wastewater impact fee: **$2,900 per equivalent service unit**
- Combined water + wastewater impact fee: **$7,700** per service unit
- A typical commercial retail store with a 1-inch or 1.5-inch meter would likely represent 1 equivalent service unit. Larger meters (2-inch) may represent multiple service units, increasing the fee proportionally.
- Additional meter and inspection fees apply but are not published on the website; contact Austin Water at 512-972-0000 ext. 4 for exact amounts.

**Development Implication:** The presence of a 36-inch transmission main and multiple distribution lines directly on-site means water supply is robust. Tapping into an existing 8-inch line for a retail store is straightforward. No off-site water main extensions should be required.

**Confidence:** HIGH for service area and infrastructure; MODERATE for fee amounts (published city data but exact meter size and service-unit count depend on design).

## 2. Wastewater Service

**Provider:** Austin Water (gravity sewer)

The concept plan shows an 8-inch and 10-inch wastewater line on or adjacent to the site, with manhole inverts provided. This confirms gravity sewer is available and the site can connect by gravity (no lift station required).

**Capacity:** Austin is currently expanding its South Austin Regional Wastewater Treatment Plant at a cost that has increased to approximately $1.8 billion (up $600M from prior estimates, per February 2026 reporting). - A gravity connection to the existing 8-inch or 10-inch main will require a standard tap and manhole connection per Austin Water Utility Criteria Manual standards

**Development Implication:** Gravity sewer is immediately available with adequate pipe sizes. Connection is routine for a retail use. No off-site wastewater extensions anticipated.

## 10. Drainage Utility Fee

**Current Rate (FY 2025-26):** $0.00593 per square foot of impervious cover per month

**Formula:** Monthly Drainage Charge = Impervious Cover (SF) x Base Rate x Adjustment Factor
- Adjustment Factor = (1.5425 x % Impervious Cover) + 0.1933

**Estimated Fee for Subject Property:**
- Assuming approximately 80% impervious cover after development (building footprint + parking + drives on a 44,150 SF lot): ~35,320 SF impervious
- Adjustment Factor at 80%: (1.5425 x 0.80) + 0.1933 = 1.4273
- Monthly charge estimate: 35,320 x $0.00593 x 1.4273 = **~$299/month (~$3,588/year)**

**Development Implication:** The drainage utility fee is an ongoing monthly charge billed on the utility bill. Higher impervious cover percentages increase the fee. Implementing stormwater management features (bioswales, permeable pavement, detention) may qualify for the Stormwater Management Discount, potentially reducing the fee.

**Confidence:** HIGH for rate; MODERATE for estimated amount (depends on actual impervious cover after development).

## 11. Impact Fees

### Water & Wastewater Impact Fees (Capital Recovery Fees)
- **Water:** $4,800 per equivalent service unit
- **Wastewater:** $2,900 per equivalent service unit
- **Combined:** $7,700 per service unit
- Applies to lots platted after October 1, 2023. Earlier plat dates may have lower fees ($1,100-$7,600 depending on zone and plat date tier).
- Fees are assessed per connection at time of tap, based on meter size / equivalent service units.

### Street Impact Fees
- Austin's Street Impact Fee Program (adopted December 10, 2020) applies to new development generating 10+ PM peak-hour trips.
- Fees vary by **Service Area** (17 areas defined) and are calculated based on zoning entitlements and trip generation.
- A 7,375 SF retail store generates approximately 30-40 PM peak-hour trips (per ITE Trip Generation Manual), exceeding the 10-trip threshold.
- Specific fee rates require identification of the Service Area for the Cameron Rd location and use of the city's calculation worksheet. Estimated range: **$5,000-$25,000** depending on service area.
- Contact: StreetImpactFee@AustinTexas.gov

### Parkland Dedication Fees
- **Not applicable to commercial development.** Texas House Bill 1526 (effective January 1, 2024) prohibits cities from imposing parkland dedication requirements on commercial development.

### Transportation Impact Fees (TUF)
- The Transportation User Fee (TUF) is a monthly operational fee, not a one-time impact fee. It is assessed on utility bills based on land use and trip generation.

**Development Implication:** Total one-time impact fees are estimated at **$12,700-$32,700** (water/wastewater capital recovery + street impact fee). The exemption from parkland fees for commercial development is a cost savings. Exact street impact fee requires service area determination.

**Confidence:** HIGH for water/wastewater fees; LOW-MODERATE for street impact fee (range estimate; service area and trip generation must be confirmed).
