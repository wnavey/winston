## Finding

Parkland dedication does **not** apply to this project.

Under the City of Austin Land Development Code (LDC 25-1-601 et seq.), parkland dedication requirements are triggered at **site plan** for **residential (multifamily) and hotel/motel** uses only. Effective January 1, 2024, the prior commercial parkland dedication requirement was **repealed** by Ordinance No. 20231130-087.

The proposed development is a single-story AutoZone retail store (7,375 SF) — a purely commercial use. Commercial and retail uses are expressly exempt from parkland dedication under the current ordinance framework. Civic and industrial uses are likewise exempt.

## Requirements

None. No parkland dedication fee, land dedication, or fee-in-lieu payment is required for this project.

## Conditions / Next Steps

No parkland-related conditions, submittals, or reviews are anticipated at site plan or building permit stage for this commercial retail development.
