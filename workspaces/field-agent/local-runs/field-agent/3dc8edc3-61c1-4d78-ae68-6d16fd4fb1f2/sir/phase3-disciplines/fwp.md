# Floodplain Findings --- 12713 Cinchring Ln

**Property:** 12713 Cinchring Ln, Austin, TX 78727 --- Lot 12, Block M, Scofield Subdivision
**Proposed use:** Demolish existing SFR; build ground-up duplex (2 units)
**Lot:** ~7,840 sq ft (0.18 acres), SF-2 zoning
**Watershed:** Walnut Creek (Suburban)
**Date:** 2026-06-03

---

## Summary

- **No floodplain encumbrance.** The property is in FEMA Zone X (FIRM Panel 48453C0115E, effective 06/16/1993), outside both the 100-year and 500-year floodplain. The survey confirms this determination. No floodplain development permit, elevation certificate, CLOMR/LOMR, or ASCE 24 certification is required.
- **No CWQZ, EHZ, or CEF constraints identified.** The lot is an upland site at approximately 850--870 ft elevation, well above the Walnut Creek floodplain corridor. No classified waterway is on or immediately adjacent to the parcel, and no Critical Water Quality Zone, Erosion Hazard Zone, or Critical Environmental Feature buffers encroach on the site.
- **No floodway (COA 25-yr) or COA fully-developed 100-yr floodplain impact.** The upland position places the site outside both the FEMA SFHA and the City of Austin's fully-developed-conditions floodplain, which can be broader than FEMA in Atlas 14-updated watersheds. Neither boundary reaches this lot.
- **No drainage easement encumbrance for floodplain purposes.** The recorded easements (5-ft PUE, blanket electrical easement, ingress/egress utility easement) are utility-related; no drainage easement is recorded on the plat, and none is required because the regulatory 100-yr floodplain does not cross the parcel.
- **This discipline presents no material constraint to the proposed duplex development.** The floodplain assessment is complete and favorable. Remaining environmental obligations (impervious cover, water quality controls, tree survey, BCCP fee) are addressed in the Environmental Landscape and Site Drainage disciplines.

## Findings

### 1. FEMA Flood Zone Determination

- **Severity:** note
- **Citation:** FEMA FIRM Panel 48453C0115E, effective 06/16/1993; property survey; DCM 1.2.6.B
- **Finding:** The property is mapped in FEMA Zone X --- areas determined to be outside both the 1% annual chance (100-year) and 0.2% annual chance (500-year) floodplains. The survey explicitly states the property does not lie within the 100-year flood prone area. Zone X designation means no Special Flood Hazard Area (SFHA) applies.
- **Implication for the developer:** No FEMA-related floodplain obligations attach to this site. No flood insurance purchase requirement under NFIP. No elevation certificate, floodplain development permit, or FEMA map amendment (CLOMR/LOMR) is needed. No ASCE 24-14 structural compliance is required.
- **Recommended next step:** None. Retain the FIRM panel citation and survey flood zone note in the project file for the building permit application.

### 2. City of Austin Fully-Developed 100-Year Floodplain

- **Severity:** note
- **Citation:** LDC Ch. 25-7; LDC Ch. 25-12; DCM 1.2.6.A-B; Rule R161-20.01 (Atlas 14)
- **Finding:** The COA fully-developed 100-year floodplain, derived from City regulatory hydraulic models with Atlas 14 rainfall data (where applicable), does not extend to this upland lot. The Walnut Creek floodplain corridor lies at materially lower elevations. This is consistent with the site's 850--870 ft elevation and its position within a fully built-out subdivision on higher ground. No COA floodplain boundary encroaches on the parcel.
- **Implication for the developer:** No COA floodplain development permit is required. No compensatory storage, no-rise analysis, or freeboard calculations are needed. The project is not subject to the COA 25-year floodway zero-rise standard (LDC 25-12-54).
- **Recommended next step:** Confirm the COA GIS floodplain layer at site plan submission as a procedural step. No engineering analysis required.

### 3. CWQZ / WQTZ Applicability

- **Severity:** note
- **Citation:** LDC 25-8-261; ECM 1.5.2; DCM 1.2.6.A.1 (64-acre classified waterway threshold)
- **Finding:** Critical Water Quality Zone buffers in Suburban watersheds apply to classified waterways with 64 or more acres of contributing drainage area. No such waterway is on or immediately adjacent to this lot. The lot is an interior subdivision parcel surrounded by other residential lots and local streets, with no surface water feature or classified drainage channel present. The CWQZ does not encroach on the site.
- **Implication for the developer:** No CWQZ use restrictions apply. No buffer averaging analysis or LUC variance under the enhanced LDC 25-8-41(B) standard is needed. Proposed building and impervious cover placement is unconstrained by CWQZ.
- **Recommended next step:** None.

### 4. Erosion Hazard Zone (EHZ) Applicability

- **Severity:** note
- **Citation:** LDC 25-7-32(C); DCM Appendix E
- **Finding:** EHZ analysis is triggered when development is within 100 ft of a classified waterway centerline (64 or more acres drainage area). No classified waterway is within 100 ft of this lot. No EHZ has been identified or is expected.
- **Implication for the developer:** No EHZ setback analysis, meander belt delineation, or WPD/ERM concurrence on protective works is required.
- **Recommended next step:** None.

### 5. Critical Environmental Features (CEFs)

- **Severity:** note
- **Citation:** LDC 25-8-281; ECM 1.10.0; City of Austin Watershed Protection karst database
- **Finding:** No known CEFs (springs, caves, sinkholes, point recharge features, rimrock, bluffs) have been identified at or immediately adjacent to the site. The Scofield subdivision in north Austin is developed on Cretaceous-age formations not typically associated with significant karst features. The Edwards Aquifer Recharge Zone is well south of this property.
- **Implication for the developer:** No 150-ft CEF protective buffer applies. No administrative or LUC variance for CEF setback reduction is needed.
- **Recommended next step:** The Environmental Resource Inventory (ERI) form, required at site plan application, will provide formal City confirmation of CEF status. This is a procedural requirement, not a risk item.

### 6. FIRM Panel Age

- **Severity:** note
- **Citation:** FIRM Panel 48453C0115E, effective 06/16/1993
- **Finding:** The effective FIRM panel dates to 1993. While older panels can sometimes be superseded by preliminary FIRMs from watershed restudies, a Zone X determination on an upland lot at 850--870 ft elevation is extremely unlikely to be affected by any FEMA restudy of the Walnut Creek watershed. No pending preliminary FIRM affecting this panel has been identified.
- **Implication for the developer:** The 1993 effective date does not create risk for this parcel. The Zone X designation is stable.
- **Recommended next step:** As a best practice, confirm at permit application that no preliminary FIRM has been published for this panel. This is low-effort and low-risk.

### 7. Drainage Easement Status

- **Severity:** note
- **Citation:** LDC 25-7-152; plat records (Cabinet 91, Slide 264-265)
- **Finding:** The recorded easements on this lot (5-ft PUE, blanket electrical easement, ingress/egress utility easement) are utility-related. No drainage easement is recorded, and none is required because the regulatory 100-year floodplain does not cross the parcel. The drainage easement dedication requirement (LDC 25-7-152) applies only where the regulatory floodplain exists on a lot.
- **Implication for the developer:** No drainage easement dedication or variance is needed.
- **Recommended next step:** None.

## Open Questions for the Engineer

1. **Atlas 14 watershed amendment status for Walnut Creek:** Has the Walnut Creek watershed been updated to Atlas 14 rainfall per Rule R161-20.01 (effective 2019-11-25)? This does not affect the floodplain determination for this upland lot, but should be documented for completeness and in case any drainage study is required for site plan review.

2. **Preliminary FIRM check:** Has FEMA published any preliminary FIRM panels for the Walnut Creek watershed that would supersede Panel 48453C0115E? Again, unlikely to affect a Zone X upland lot, but confirm for the file.

3. **Contributing drainage area to the lot:** Does any concentrated flow path with 64 or more acres of contributing drainage area cross or abut the lot? The answer is almost certainly no given the interior subdivision location, but the engineer should confirm this at the site plan stage per DCM 1.2.6.A.1.
