# Fire Findings — 12713 Cinchring Ln

**Property:** 12713 Cinchring Ln, Austin, TX 78727 — Lot 12, Block M, Scofield Subdivision
**Proposed use:** Demolish existing SFR; build ground-up duplex (2 units)
**Lot:** ~7,840 sq ft (0.18 acres), SF-2 zoning
**Jurisdiction:** City of Austin, Full Purpose — Austin Fire Department
**Applicable code:** 2024 International Fire Code (adopted via LDC § 25-12-171, Ord. 20250410-039, effective July 10, 2025); building construction governed by IRC (International Residential Code) for duplex (Group R-3 occupancy)

---

## Summary

- **Low overall fire-code risk.** A two-unit duplex built under the IRC on an existing platted lot within a developed suburban subdivision presents a straightforward fire profile with no high-rise, standpipe, NFPA 13, or commercial fire-flow triggers.
- **WUI status is very likely non-applicable.** The site is in a flat, fully urbanized 1990s subdivision with no adjacent wildland; web research and wildfire risk tools indicate low risk. Formal confirmation from AFD Wildfire Division is still required — self-declaration is not accepted.
- **Fire apparatus access is satisfied by existing public street.** Cinchring Ln is a public residential street providing frontage; a two-story duplex footprint well within 150 ft of the street requires no private fire lane, turnaround, or AMOC.
- **Hydrant coverage benefits from the 600-ft R-3 allowance.** Group R-3 occupancies receive the most generous hose-lay distance (600 ft) regardless of sprinkler status. Existing subdivision hydrant infrastructure almost certainly satisfies this, but the nearest hydrant location should be field-verified.
- **Sprinklers are not required** for an IRC duplex unless the building exceeds specific IRC thresholds (e.g., fire area, access limitations). No NFPA 13/13R/13D system is anticipated; no FDC, fire pump, or standpipe applies.

### 2. Fire Apparatus Access

### 3. Hydrant Coverage

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | data-gap |
| **Citation** | LDC § 25-12-173 § 507.5.1; UCM § 2.9.2.D |
| **Finding** | Group R-3 and Group U occupancies receive a 600-ft hose-lay allowance to all exterior walls, regardless of sprinkler status. Hydrant spacing in non-commercial/non-high-density areas (SF-2 qualifies) is 600 ft maximum interval. Scofield Subdivision, developed in the 1990s, has existing hydrant infrastructure. However, the exact location of the nearest hydrant and its distance to the proposed building have not been field-verified. |
| **Implication** | If an existing hydrant is within 600 ft hose-lay of all exterior walls (highly likely in a developed subdivision), no new hydrant or tap is required. No fire flow test is anticipated for an IRC duplex that does not trigger sprinkler or commercial fire-flow requirements. |
| **Recommended next step** | Field-verify or confirm via Austin Water GIS the location of the nearest fire hydrant and measure hose-lay distance to the farthest exterior wall of the proposed duplex. |

### 4. Fire-Flow Demand

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | note |
| **Citation** | IRC; IFC Appendix B Table B105.1(2) (informational for R-3) |
| **Finding** | An IRC duplex (Group R-3, two-family dwelling) does not trigger the commercial fire-flow analysis pathway under the IFC. Fire flow for one- and two-family dwellings is governed by the IRC and the local jurisdiction's residential water supply standards. Austin Water's existing mains in Scofield Subdivision serve the existing SFR and are expected to be adequate for a replacement duplex of comparable scale. |
| **Implication** | No AFD fire flow test, no AWWA available-flow calculation, and no fire-flow demand table are required for permit. No AMOC for fire-flow reduction is needed. |
| **Recommended next step** | No action required beyond standard Austin Water tap plan review for the new duplex service connections. |

### 5. Sprinkler / Fire Suppression System

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | note |
| **Citation** | IRC R313; IFC § 903 |
| **Finding** | Austin has not adopted a residential sprinkler mandate for IRC one- and two-family dwellings beyond the IRC's own thresholds. A standard two-story duplex does not trigger NFPA 13, 13R, or 13D requirements. No standpipe is required (building height well below 30 ft above lowest FD access). No FDC is required. |
| **Implication** | No sprinkler design, FDC siting, fire pump, or riser room is needed. This eliminates significant cost and design complexity. |
| **Recommended next step** | None. If the developer voluntarily installs sprinklers (e.g., for insurance benefits), NFPA 13D would be the applicable standard for a two-family dwelling. |

### 6. Building Separation / Fire-Resistance Between Units

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | moderate |
| **Citation** | IRC R302.1, R302.2, R302.3 |
| **Finding** | The zoning pathway confirms that HOME Phase 1 imposes no zoning-based building separation requirement for duplex units. However, the IRC requires fire-separation construction between dwelling units in a duplex: a 1-hour fire-resistance-rated wall assembly from foundation to underside of roof sheathing (IRC R302.2), with no plumbing or mechanical penetrations that compromise the rating. Exterior wall fire-resistance and opening protection depend on fire-separation distance from the property line (IRC R302.1 Table R302.1). |
| **Implication** | The architect/designer must detail the party wall (or floor/ceiling assembly if stacked units) to meet IRC fire-separation requirements. This is standard duplex construction but must be explicitly shown on plans. Exterior walls within 3 ft of a property line require 1-hour rating with no openings; walls 3-5 ft require 1-hour rating with limited openings. The 5-ft side setback on this lot will govern the exterior wall treatment. |
| **Recommended next step** | During design, confirm fire-separation distance from each side property line and detail the party wall and any exterior walls within 5 ft of a property line for IRC R302.1 compliance. |

## Open Questions for the Engineer

1. **Nearest hydrant location and distance.** What is the location of the nearest fire hydrant to the proposed duplex, and does the hose-lay path-of-travel to the farthest exterior wall fall within 600 ft? (Field verification or Austin Water GIS confirmation needed.)

2. **WUI map status.** Has the 2024 WUI Code Map been checked for this parcel? If the property appears on the map, has AFD Wildfire Division been contacted for a formal zone determination?

3. **Fire-separation distance at side yards.** With 5-ft side setbacks, will any exterior wall be closer than 3 ft to the property line (e.g., eaves, projections)? This determines whether IRC R302.1 requires a 1-hour-rated exterior wall with no openings versus limited openings.

4. **Party wall construction type.** Has the designer specified the party wall assembly (IRC R302.2 — 1-hour fire-resistance-rated, foundation to underside of roof sheathing, no through-penetrations)? Will the duplex be side-by-side or stacked?

5. **Voluntary sprinklers.** Is the developer considering voluntary NFPA 13D sprinklers for insurance premium reduction or marketability? If so, this would require coordination with Austin Water for a dedicated fire service line and backflow preventer.
