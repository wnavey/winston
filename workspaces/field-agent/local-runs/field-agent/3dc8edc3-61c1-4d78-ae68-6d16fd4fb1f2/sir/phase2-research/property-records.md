## Recorded Easements & Encumbrances (from Survey)

The following easements and encumbrances were identified from the property survey:

1. **Blanket-type electrical easement** — recorded at Vol. 660, Pg. 968, Travis County Deed Records. This easement grants the electric utility (Austin Energy) access across the entire lot for installation and maintenance of electrical infrastructure.

2. **Ingress/egress easement for utilities** — recorded at Vol. 11863, Pg. 1147, Travis County Deed Records. Provides utility access for ingress and egress to the property.

3. **5-foot Public Utility Easement (PUE)** — per the recorded plat (Cabinet 91, Slide 264-265). Standard subdivision PUE along lot boundaries, typically along the front and/or side property lines. Structures, fences, and permanent improvements are generally prohibited within the PUE.

4. **25-foot Building Line** — per plat, Vol. 11863, Pg. 1147. This platted building line restricts construction within 25 feet of the front property line. See Zoning Pathway analysis for interaction with HOME Phase 1 reduced setbacks.

5. **Declarant's reserved ROW/easement rights** — per Vol. 11863, Pg. 1147. The subdivision declarant reserved the right to grant or dedicate rights-of-way and easements for public utility purposes, not to exceed 10 feet in width on each side of any lot line. This is a standard subdivision development provision; verify whether any additional easements have been granted under this reservation.
