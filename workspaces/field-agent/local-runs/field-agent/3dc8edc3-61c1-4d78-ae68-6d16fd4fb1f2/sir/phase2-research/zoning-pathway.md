## Base District

The property is zoned **SF-2 (Single-Family Residential -- Standard Lot)** per the Austin Land Development Code. SF-2 is the standard single-family district with a minimum lot size of 5,750 sq ft. The lot is approximately 0.18 acres (~7,840 sq ft), which exceeds the SF-2 minimum and the 7,000 sq ft minimum lot area historically required for duplex use.

Under pre-HOME zoning, SF-2 did **not** permit duplex or two-family residential use. That changed with HOME Phase 1 (see Recent Ordinances below).

**Citation:** Austin LDC Title 25, Ch. 25-2, Subchapter C, Art. 2, Div. 1, Section 25-2-492 (Site Development Regulations Table); [Municode -- LDC Ch. 25-2](https://library.municode.com/tx/austin/codes/land_development_code?nodeId=TIT25LADE_CH25-2ZO)

## Duplex Permissibility Under HOME Phase 1

**HOME Phase 1** (Ordinance No. 20231207-001, effective February 5, 2024) amended the LDC to allow up to three dwelling units -- including duplexes, two-unit residential, and three-unit residential uses -- on any lot zoned SF-1, SF-2, or SF-3.

Key provisions:

- **Unit count:** Up to 3 dwelling units permitted on SF-1/SF-2/SF-3 lots. A duplex (2 units) is permitted by right.
- **Minimum lot area for duplex/two-unit:** 5,750 sq ft (the lot at ~7,840 sq ft qualifies).
- **Minimum lot width:** 50 ft (per LDC duplex standards; verify against plat).
- **Removes primary/secondary distinction:** All dwelling units are treated equally; no unit is an "accessory."
- **Removes unrelated-adult occupancy limits.**
- **Each dwelling unit requires a unique address.**

**Citation:** [HOME Amendments -- Austin Development Services](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/home-amendments); Ordinance 20231207-001

## 25-Foot Platted Building Line

The recorded plat for Scofield Subdivision shows a **25-foot building line** on the front of Lot 12.

**Interaction with HOME Phase 1 setbacks:**

- HOME Phase 1 reduces the zoning front setback to 15 ft for two-unit/duplex uses.
- However, a platted building line is a deed restriction recorded against the land. Under Austin practice, **the more restrictive requirement controls** -- meaning the 25 ft platted building line governs over the 15 ft zoning setback.
- To use the 15 ft front setback, the owner would need to **replat or amend the plat** to remove or reduce the building line, which requires subdivision review.

**Recommendation:** Confirm whether Austin treats the platted building line as enforceable through the permitting process or only as a private covenant. If enforceable, factor a 25 ft front setback into the building envelope. If a variance or replat is viable, the 15 ft setback could unlock additional depth.

**Citation:** [Global Land Surveying -- Building Setbacks: Plats, Zoning, and Covenants](https://gls-inc.com/3-building-setbacks-plats-zoning-and-covenants/); Austin LDC subdivision standards

## Procedural Pathway

### Site Plan Exemption

Under Austin LDC, **residential uses of four or fewer units are exempt from site plan requirements**. A duplex (2 units) qualifies for this exemption. No administrative site plan is required.

### Building Permit

The duplex can proceed via a **residential building permit** application through Austin Development Services (DSD). Under HOME Phase 1, applications have been accepted since February 5, 2024.

**Permit review path:**

1. **Residential Plan Review** -- submit building plans through Austin DSD
2. Plans reviewed for compliance with LDC dimensional standards, IRC/IBC building codes, and applicable environmental/drainage requirements
3. No zoning change, conditional use permit, or Board of Adjustment variance is needed (assuming the lot meets dimensional standards)

### Site Plan Lite (HOME Phase 2 -- March 2025)

Site Plan Lite is a streamlined review process adopted March 6, 2025, primarily for HOME Phase 2 subdivision and small-lot projects. It is **not required** for a duplex on an existing lot that already meets SF-2 minimum lot size. However, if the owner later pursues subdivision of the lot (e.g., into two small lots), Site Plan Lite would apply.

### Demolition Permit

A separate **demolition permit** is required to remove the existing house. Austin DSD processes demo permits administratively; typical turnaround is 2-4 weeks. Asbestos survey may be required for pre-2000 construction (this house was built 1993).

### Tree Permits

Austin's tree ordinance (LDC Ch. 25-8, Subchapter B) protects trees with trunk diameter >= 19 inches (heritage trees >= 24 inches). A tree survey may be required, and mitigation fees or replacement may apply if protected trees must be removed.

**Citation:** [Austin DSD -- Residential Plan Review](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/residential-plan-review); [Austin DSD -- Site Plans and Exemptions](https://www.austintexas.gov/siteplans); [HOME Amendments Page](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/home-amendments)
