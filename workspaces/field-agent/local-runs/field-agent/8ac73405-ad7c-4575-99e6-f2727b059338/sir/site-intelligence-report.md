3. **Tree Survey Needed:** The lot is 30+ years old and likely has mature trees. Protected or heritage trees could significantly constrain the buildable envelope. An RPLS-certified tree survey is required before design.

4. **Dimensional Constraints are Tight:** At 8,054 SF with a 0.40:1 FAR cap, maximum GFA is approximately 3,222 SF for the duplex (both units combined). Building coverage is capped at 40% (3,222 SF footprint). These are workable for a duplex but require careful design.

### Deed Restriction Risk

**This is the single highest risk factor for the proposed development.**

The City of Austin explicitly states that private restrictive covenants and city zoning laws are separate regulatory systems:
- The City issues building permits based on compliance with the Land Development Code, regardless of deed restrictions
- Deed restrictions are enforceable only through private civil action by the HOA or individual property owners
- The applicant may receive a building permit but face an injunction from the HOA

Scofield Farms was developed in the early 1990s as a single-family residential community. The original deed restrictions almost certainly contemplate single-family residential use. Whether duplexes are prohibited, or whether the HOME amendments affect the enforceability of single-family-only covenants, is a legal question that requires:

1. Full review of the Declaration and all amendments
2. Legal opinion on duplex permissibility under the covenants
3. Assessment of HOA enforcement posture
