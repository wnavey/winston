# Stormwater & Drainage Findings — 12713 Cinchring Ln

**Property:** 12713 Cinchring Ln, Austin, TX 78727 — Lot 12, Block M, Scofield Subdivision
**Proposed use:** Demolish existing SFR; build ground-up duplex (2 units)
**Lot area:** ~7,840 sq ft (0.18 acres), SF-2 zoning
**Watershed:** Walnut Creek — Suburban Watershed classification
**Floodplain:** Zone X (not in 100-year or 500-year floodplain)
**EARZ:** Not in Edwards Aquifer Recharge Zone or Contributing Zone
**Date:** 2026-06-03

---

## Summary

- **Site Plan Lite drainage reforms exempt this lot from engineered drainage review and on-site detention.** At ~7,840 sq ft the lot is well under the 11,500 sq ft threshold; no drainage study, detention pond, or formal drainage plan is required, saving significant cost and schedule.
- **Impervious cover is capped at 45% (~3,528 sq ft) by the controlling SF-2 zoning limit**, which aligns with the Walnut Creek Suburban Watershed IC framework. The building footprint (40% / ~3,136 sq ft) plus driveways, sidewalks, and patios must stay within this envelope.
- **Water quality controls are likely satisfied by the existing Scofield subdivision regional facilities**, but this must be confirmed with the Watershed Protection Department since demolition-and-rebuild constitutes redevelopment subject to current WQ standards.
- **RSMP fee-in-lieu is a fallback option if detention is ever triggered**, but the Site Plan Lite exemption likely makes it unnecessary for this project. Eligibility in Walnut Creek watershed should be verified regardless.
- **No floodplain, EARZ, CEF, or erosion hazard zone constraints apply.** The site is in FEMA Zone X on upland terrain with no waterway within 100 ft, eliminating an entire class of drainage complications.

## Findings

### 1. Site Plan Lite Drainage Exemption

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | opportunity |
| **Citation** | Site Plan Lite Phase 2 (Ordinance adopted Mar 6, 2025, applications accepted Jun 16, 2025); LDC residential infill provisions |
| **Finding** | The Site Plan Lite drainage reforms exempt lots of 11,500 sq ft or less from drainage review. Lots up to 1 acre draining to an existing street or drainage system do not require on-site detention or an engineered drainage plan. This lot at ~7,840 sq ft qualifies on both counts. |
| **Implication** | The developer avoids the cost of an engineered drainage study, detention facility design, and associated review cycles. This removes what would otherwise be a $15,000-$40,000 engineering expense and 2-4 months of review time for a project of this scale. |
| **Recommended next step** | Confirm the lot drains to the existing street/storm system (expected for a platted Scofield lot on a local street). Verify with DSD that the exemption applies to demo-and-rebuild redevelopment, not just new construction on vacant lots. |

### 2. Impervious Cover Limit — 45%

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | moderate |
| **Citation** | LDC Sec. 25-2-492(D) (SF-2 site development regulations); LDC Sec. 25-8-392 (Suburban Watershed uplands IC); HOME Phase 1 Ordinance 20231207-001 |
| **Finding** | The SF-2 zoning impervious cover limit is 45%, yielding a maximum of ~3,528 sq ft of IC on this ~7,840 sq ft lot. The Walnut Creek Suburban Watershed IC limit for residential use is in the 45-50% range; the more restrictive of zoning or watershed controls. At 45%, the zoning limit is the binding constraint. Building coverage is separately capped at 40% (~3,136 sq ft). |
| **Implication** | After accounting for the building footprint (~3,136 sq ft maximum), only ~392 sq ft remains for driveways, sidewalks, patios, and other hardscape before hitting the IC ceiling. A two-story duplex with a smaller footprint would provide more IC headroom for site improvements. The developer must track IC carefully during design — exceeding 45% is a hard stop at permitting. |
| **Recommended next step** | Size the building footprint to leave adequate IC budget for driveways and walks. Consider pervious pavers or permeable materials for non-building hardscape to stay within the 45% limit. Cross-reference with the zoning/land-use (zlu) discipline for FAR and building coverage coordination. |

### 3. Water Quality — Redevelopment Compliance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | moderate |
| **Citation** | LDC Sec. 25-8-211; ECM Sec. 1.6.0; LDC Ch. 25-8 Subchapter A (Watershed Protection Ordinance — demolition/redevelopment) |
| **Finding** | Demolition of the existing SFR and construction of a new duplex constitutes redevelopment under Austin's Watershed Protection Ordinance. Redevelopment must comply with current water quality standards, including providing WQ controls for the developed impervious area. The WQ trigger threshold is 8,000 sq ft of new + redeveloped IC outside the BSZ. This lot's maximum IC (~3,528 sq ft) falls below the 8,000 sq ft trigger, which may exempt the project from on-site WQ controls entirely. |
| **Implication** | If the 8,000 sq ft WQ trigger is confirmed as not met, no structural water quality controls (rain garden, bioswale, sand filter) are required — a significant simplification. If WPD interprets the trigger differently for redevelopment (e.g., counting existing IC being replaced), the Scofield subdivision's existing regional WQ facilities may provide coverage, avoiding on-site treatment. |
| **Recommended next step** | Confirm with Watershed Protection Department (WPD) whether (a) the 8,000 sq ft IC threshold applies to this redevelopment, and (b) if triggered, whether existing Scofield regional facilities satisfy the WQ obligation for this lot. |

### 4. No On-Site Detention Required

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | note |
| **Citation** | DCM Sec. 1.2.2.D (no-increase rule); Site Plan Lite Phase 2 drainage reforms; DCM Sec. 1.2.2.G (Simplified RSMP for sites up to 1 acre) |
| **Finding** | Under the Site Plan Lite drainage reforms, lots up to 1 acre draining to an existing system are exempt from on-site detention requirements. This lot at 0.18 acres qualifies. Independently, the DCM Simplified RSMP pathway (Sec. 1.2.2.G) would also cover SF lots up to 1 acre if detention were otherwise triggered. The no-increase rule (2-, 10-, 25-, 100-year storms) does not apply when the exemption is in effect. |
| **Implication** | No detention pond, underground vault, or other flood-control structure is needed on site. This preserves the full buildable area for the duplex and site improvements. |
| **Recommended next step** | No action required beyond confirming the Site Plan Lite exemption applies (see Finding 1). |

## Open Questions for the Engineer

1. **Site Plan Lite applicability to demo-and-rebuild redevelopment.** Does the drainage review exemption (lots <=11,500 sq ft) apply to demolition-and-new-construction on an existing platted lot, or only to greenfield construction? Confirm with DSD before relying on the exemption.

2. **WQ trigger for this IC level.** The site's maximum IC (~3,528 sq ft) is below the 8,000 sq ft WQ trigger threshold. Confirm with WPD that this threshold applies to redevelopment IC (replaced + new) and that the project is exempt from structural water quality controls.

3. **Scofield regional WQ facilities.** If WQ controls are triggered, do existing regional stormwater facilities serving the Scofield subdivision satisfy the water quality obligation for this lot? The subdivision was developed in the late 1980s/early 1990s and may have regional ponds. WPD confirmation is needed.

4. **Existing drainage pattern.** Does the lot currently drain entirely to Cinchring Ln (front), or does any portion drain to the rear or side toward adjacent lots? Positive drainage to the street is assumed but should be confirmed by a topographic survey or site visit before design.

5. **RSMP watershed eligibility.** Is Walnut Creek confirmed as a participating RSMP watershed on the Feasibility Viewer? This is a contingency question — only matters if the project scope changes beyond the Site Plan Lite exemption threshold.

6. **IC accounting method.** For the 45% IC limit, does WPD use gross lot area (~7,840 sq ft) or net site area (excluding easements/ROW dedications) as the denominator? The 5-ft PUE along the lot line and any ROW dedication could reduce the effective denominator and tighten the IC budget.

7. **Interbasin diversion.** Confirm that the lot drains entirely within the Walnut Creek watershed and that no grading changes would divert runoff across a watershed boundary (unlikely given the suburban setting, but required for due diligence under LDC Sec. 25-8-365).
