## Findings

### F-1: Duplex Permitted by Right Under HOME Phase 1

- **Severity:** Opportunity
- **Citation:** Ord. 20231207-001 (HOME Phase 1), eff. Feb 5, 2024; LDC Sec. 25-2-492 as amended
- **Finding:** HOME Phase 1 amended the SF-2 use table to permit duplex, two-unit, and three-unit residential uses by right on all SF-1, SF-2, and SF-3 lots citywide. The lot at ~7,840 sf exceeds the 5,750 sf minimum. No rezoning, conditional use permit, variance, or density bonus program enrollment is required.
- **Implication:** The developer has a clear by-right entitlement path. No discretionary approval body (Planning Commission, Land Use Commission, Board of Adjustment, City Council) is involved in the use determination.
- **Recommended next step:** Proceed with residential building permit application. No zoning action needed.

### F-2: 25-Foot Platted Building Line Controls Front Setback

- **Severity:** Moderate
- **Citation:** Plat of Scofield Subdivision, Cabinet 91, Slides 264-265, Vol. 11863, Pg. 1147; LDC Sec. 25-2-492(B) (more restrictive standard governs)
- **Finding:** HOME Phase 1 reduces the zoning front setback to 15 ft for duplex/two-unit uses. However, the recorded plat for Scofield Subdivision establishes a 25-foot building line on the front of Lot 12. Under Austin practice, platted building lines are enforceable through the permitting process, and the more restrictive requirement controls. The 25 ft front building line governs over the 15 ft HOME setback, reducing the buildable depth by 10 feet compared to what HOME would otherwise allow.
- **Implication:** The building envelope loses approximately 10 ft of depth at the front. On a lot of this size, this is a material constraint on total buildable area, particularly given the tight FAR cap (0.40:1). Two-story massing must fit within the reduced depth.
- **Recommended next step:** (1) Confirm with DSD whether the platted building line is enforced at building permit review or treated as a private covenant only. (2) If enforced, evaluate whether a replat to reduce the building line is feasible and cost-justified. (3) Design the duplex assuming 25 ft front setback unless a replat is pursued.

### F-3: FAR Cap Constrains Total Floor Area

- **Severity:** Moderate
- **Citation:** LDC Sec. 25-2-492(D) (SF-2: 0.40:1 FAR)
- **Finding:** The 0.40:1 FAR limit yields a maximum gross floor area of approximately 3,136 sf on the ~7,840 sf lot. For a duplex, this translates to roughly 1,568 sf per unit. This is a meaningful constraint for a two-story duplex targeting market-rate finishes in a suburban location where competing product (single-family homes in Scofield) averages 1,800-2,400 sf. GFA under LDC Sec. 25-1-21(47) includes all enclosed floor area except parking, driveways, enclosed loading berths, and atria airspace.
- **Implication:** Unit sizes will be capped around 1,500 sf each. Garages and covered parking are excluded from GFA and do not count against FAR, which provides some design flexibility. The developer should verify whether HOME Phase 1 applies the alternative FAR formula (55% of lot area or 3,200 sf, whichever is greater, for two-unit uses) -- the programs research references this alternative, which would yield ~4,312 sf (55% x 7,840) or 3,200 sf, potentially more generous than the 0.40:1 base.
- **Recommended next step:** Confirm the applicable FAR standard with DSD -- specifically whether HOME Phase 1 substitutes the "55% or 3,200 sf" formula for the base 0.40:1 ratio for two-unit uses. Design GFA accordingly.

### F-4: No Parking Minimums

- **Severity:** Opportunity
- **Citation:** Ord. 20231102-028 (eff. Nov 13, 2023); LDC Sec. 25-6-471(A)
- **Finding:** Austin eliminated all minimum off-street parking requirements citywide in November 2023. No minimum spaces are required for a duplex. ADA accessible spaces are required only if a common parking facility is provided (not applicable to a typical duplex with individual driveways). No PUD parking exhibit or UNO maximum applies.
- **Implication:** Parking is a free design variable. The developer can provide as much or as little as the market demands. In suburban north Austin, market expectations typically call for 2 spaces per unit (4 total), but no code minimum compels this.
- **Recommended next step:** Size parking to market demand. A single shared driveway or two individual driveways serving both units is the typical configuration.

### F-5: No Overlays, Combining Districts, or Compatibility Triggers

- **Severity:** Note
- **Citation:** Austin GIS overlay layers (CVC, Capitol Dominance, HCRC, AO, WO, NCCD, ETOD, DB90, VMU, NP, CO, historic); LDC Sec. 25-2-1051 (Compatibility Standards applicability)
- **Finding:** No overlay or combining district applies to this parcel. The property is not within any NCCD, CO, NP, historic district, TOD regulating plan, ETOD subdistrict, CVC, Capitol Dominance, HCRC, AO, or WO boundary. Article 10 Compatibility Standards (post-July 2024) do not apply because the site is zoned SF-2 (less restrictive than MF-4 threshold for applicability) and the proposed use is duplex (exempt per Sec. 25-2-1052).
- **Implication:** The dimensional envelope is governed solely by the SF-2 base district standards as modified by HOME Phase 1. No overlay-driven height caps, buffer requirements, design review boards, or additional setbacks apply.
- **Recommended next step:** None required. This is a clean regulatory position.

### F-6: Site Plan Exempt -- Building Permit Only

- **Severity:** Opportunity
- **Citation:** LDC Sec. 25-5 (site plan requirements); HOME Phase 1 residential infill provisions
- **Finding:** Residential uses of four or fewer units on an existing platted lot are exempt from site plan requirements. The duplex proceeds via residential building permit application only. No Consolidated Site Plan, Site Plan Lite, or F1/F2 staged plan is required. The lot also qualifies for the Site Plan Lite Phase 2 drainage review exemption (lots of 11,500 sf or less), eliminating the need for an engineered drainage study or on-site detention.
- **Implication:** The permitting path is streamlined -- no site plan review, no Watershed Protection Department drainage plan review, no site plan fees. Timeline from application to permit issuance is typically 4-8 weeks for residential plan review (subject to DSD workload).
- **Recommended next step:** Submit residential building permit application through Austin DSD. Include demolition permit application for the existing house (separate process, 2-4 weeks).

### F-7: HOA CC&Rs -- Critical Non-Zoning Feasibility Risk

- **Severity:** Significant
- **Citation:** Declaration of Covenants, Conditions and Deed Restrictions, Scofield Subdivision, Vol. 11863, Pg. 1147, Travis County Deed Records (plus 8 recorded amendments)
- **Finding:** Scofield Farms is governed by an HOA (Scofield Residential Owners Association, managed by Inframark) with recorded CC&Rs and an active Architectural Control Committee. The full CC&R text was not obtained during Phase 2 research. Based on subdivision era (1990s) and character, the CC&Rs almost certainly restrict lots to single-family residential use. HOME Phase 1 does not preempt private deed restrictions under Texas law (Texas Property Code Ch. 202). The city will issue a building permit for a code-compliant duplex, but the permit does not immunize the owner from HOA enforcement. The HOA or individual lot owners could seek injunctive relief in court to block or reverse construction.
- **Implication:** This is the single largest feasibility risk for the project. Even with a valid building permit, the developer faces potential litigation, injunction, and forced reversal if the CC&Rs prohibit duplex use. CC&R amendment requires a supermajority vote of ~574 homeowners, which is practically infeasible for a single-lot project.
- **Recommended next step:** (1) Obtain the full Declaration of CC&Rs and all 8 amendments from Inframark or Travis County Clerk. (2) Have a Texas real estate attorney review the specific use-restriction language. (3) Assess available defenses (laches, waiver, changed conditions). (4) Contact the HOA Board informally before committing to design. (5) Do not proceed to permit application until the CC&R risk is resolved or accepted.

### F-8: Walnut Creek Suburban Watershed -- 45% Impervious Cover

- **Severity:** Note
- **Citation:** LDC Ch. 25-8, Sec. 25-8-392 (Suburban Watershed Uplands Zone); LDC Sec. 25-2-492(D) (SF-2 IC: 45%)
- **Finding:** The property is in the Walnut Creek watershed, classified as a suburban watershed under LDC Ch. 25-8. The SF-2 zoning impervious cover limit of 45% is the likely controlling standard (the watershed limit for residential in suburban uplands is 45-50%). The more restrictive of zoning or watershed limits applies. At 45%, maximum IC is approximately 3,528 sf on the ~7,840 sf lot. IC includes all building footprint, driveways, sidewalks, patios, and other impervious surfaces.
- **Implication:** After accounting for the building footprint (~3,136 sf at 40% coverage), approximately 392 sf of additional impervious surface is available for driveway, walkways, and patios. This is tight. Permeable paving for driveways or walkways may be needed to stay within the IC limit while providing adequate parking and circulation.
- **Recommended next step:** Coordinate site layout to fit within the 45% IC cap. Consider permeable driveway materials. The 2-ft projection exemption (ECM Sec. 1.8.1(C)) may apply to eaves at the residential building permit level but should not be relied upon without confirming current DSD practice.

### F-9: Lot Width Verification Needed

- **Severity:** Data-gap
- **Citation:** LDC Sec. 25-2-492(D) (SF-2: 50 ft minimum lot width for duplex)
- **Finding:** HOME Phase 1 requires a minimum lot width of 50 ft for duplex use. The lot area (~7,840 sf) is adequate, but the actual lot width has not been confirmed from the plat. If the lot is narrower than 50 ft, the duplex use would not qualify under HOME standards without a variance.
- **Implication:** If the lot width is below 50 ft, a Board of Adjustment variance would be required, adding 3-6 months and introducing discretionary risk.
- **Recommended next step:** Obtain the Scofield Subdivision plat (Cabinet 91, Slides 264-265) from Travis County and confirm the lot width of Lot 12, Block M.

### F-10: HOME Phase 1 FAR Formula May Be More Generous

- **Severity:** Data-gap
- **Citation:** Ord. 20231207-001 (HOME Phase 1); programs research referencing "55% or 3,200 sf, whichever is greater" for two-unit uses
- **Finding:** Phase 2 research references a HOME Phase 1 FAR standard of "55% of lot area or 3,200 sf, whichever is greater" for two-unit uses, which would yield ~4,312 sf (55% x 7,840) rather than ~3,136 sf (0.40:1). This discrepancy between the base SF-2 FAR (0.40:1) and the HOME-amended standard has not been definitively resolved. The programs research also references a 30 ft / 2-story height limit under HOME, versus the 35 ft base SF-2 limit.
- **Implication:** If the 55% / 3,200 sf formula applies, the duplex can be approximately 1,176 sf larger in total GFA (~2,156 sf per unit instead of ~1,568 sf). This materially changes the project's market viability and design. The height limit question (30 ft vs. 35 ft) must also be resolved.
- **Recommended next step:** Confirm with DSD the exact FAR and height standards that apply to a two-unit development on an SF-2 lot under HOME Phase 1. Request written confirmation or cite the specific code section.

## Open Questions for the Engineer

1. **Lot width:** Confirm from the recorded plat (Cabinet 91, Slides 264-265) that Lot 12, Block M has a minimum width of 50 ft at the front building line. If narrower, a BOA variance is required.

2. **HOME Phase 1 FAR formula:** Confirm whether the applicable FAR for a two-unit use on SF-2 is the base 0.40:1 ratio (~3,136 sf) or the HOME-amended "55% of lot area or 3,200 sf, whichever is greater" (~4,312 sf). This is a ~1,176 sf difference in allowable GFA.

3. **HOME Phase 1 height limit:** Confirm whether the duplex is subject to 35 ft (base SF-2) or 30 ft / 2 stories (HOME Phase 1 modified standard). Research sources conflict on this point.

4. **Platted building line enforceability:** Confirm with DSD whether the 25 ft platted building line (Vol. 11863, Pg. 1147) is enforced through the building permit process or is treated as a private covenant enforceable only by adjacent owners. If enforced, evaluate replat feasibility to reduce to 15 ft.

5. **CC&R review:** Obtain and review the full Scofield Farms Declaration of CC&Rs (Vol. 11863, Pg. 1147) and all 8 amendments. Identify the specific single-family use restriction language and assess legal risk of proceeding with a duplex. This is the gating item for project feasibility.

6. **Impervious cover budget:** With 40% building coverage and 45% IC, only ~392 sf of non-building impervious surface is available for driveway, walkways, and patios. Confirm whether this is sufficient for the proposed site layout. Evaluate permeable paving options.

7. **5-ft PUE location:** Confirm from the plat where the 5-ft Public Utility Easement runs (front, side, or both). Structures and permanent improvements are prohibited within the PUE, which may further constrain the buildable area.

8. **Rear easement encumbrance:** Confirm whether the declarant's reserved 10-ft utility easement (Vol. 11863, Pg. 1147) has been exercised along the rear lot line. If so, the effective rear setback increases from 5 ft to 15 ft.

9. **Tree survey:** Determine whether any protected (19"+) or heritage (24"+) trees exist on the lot. Removal requires mitigation and may constrain building placement.

10. **Asbestos survey:** The existing house was built in 1993. Under TCEQ Asbestos NESHAP regulations, an asbestos survey is required before demolition of structures built before 2000. Coordinate survey before demolition permit application.
