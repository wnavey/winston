# Water & Wastewater Findings -- 12713 Cinchring Ln

**Property:** 12713 Cinchring Ln, Austin, TX 78727 -- Lot 12, Block M, Scofield Subdivision
**Proposed use:** Demolish existing SFR; build ground-up duplex (2 units)
**Lot:** ~7,840 sq ft (0.18 acres), SF-2 zoning
**Assessment date:** 2026-06-03

---

## Summary

- **Austin Water is the retail provider for both water and wastewater** at this address. The parcel is within Austin's full-purpose city limits with no MUD, PID, or private-utility overlay. Standard AW connection procedures apply.
- **Capital recovery (impact) fees are estimated at up to ~$15,400** ($7,700 per service unit x 2 units at the highest published tier). The actual amount may be lower because the Scofield plat dates to the early 1990s and a pre-2018 fee tier may apply; Austin Water makes the final determination.
- **A PE-prepared Utility Tap Plan is required**, with separate water meters and separate wastewater clean-outs for each unit. A ROW permit from Austin Transportation and Public Works is required for service connection installation.
- **An existing concrete sanitary sewer vault on site must be evaluated during demolition planning.** Disposition (removal, capping, or reuse) requires coordination with Austin Water before demolition proceeds.
- **No SER (Service Extension Request) is expected to be required** for a duplex on an existing platted lot with existing utility service, provided the nearest accessible mains are within 100 ft and no capacity concern area applies. However, this must be confirmed with AW correspondence, as Pressure Concern Areas and Wastewater Capacity Concern Areas are administrative designations not visible in public data.

## Findings

### 1. Capital Recovery (Impact) Fees

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | moderate |
| **Citation** | LDC SS 25-9-321; AWU Water Impact Fee Ordinance No. 20111215-115 |
| **Finding** | Each duplex unit constitutes one service unit. Current published capital recovery fees for lots platted after Oct 1, 2023 are $4,800 (water) + $2,900 (wastewater) = $7,700 per service unit. Two units yield a combined estimate of ~$15,400. The Scofield plat dates to the early 1990s, so a lower pre-2018 tier may apply, but Austin Water determines the applicable tier at tap-permit application. Tap installation fees, meter fees, and DSD inspection fees are additional and separate from capital recovery fees. |
| **Implication** | Budget at least $15,400 for capital recovery fees in the pro forma, with potential downside if the pre-2018 tier applies. Tap and inspection fees will add several hundred to a few thousand dollars on top. Fees are assessed at the schedule in effect when the tap permit is pulled -- there is no fee lock at SER or other earlier stage. |
| **Recommended next step** | Contact Austin Water to confirm the applicable fee tier based on the Scofield plat recording date (Cabinet 91, Slides 264-265). Request a written fee determination before finalizing the pro forma. |

### 3. Separate Meters and Clean-outs Per Unit

### 6. SER (Service Extension Request) Applicability

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | data-gap |
| **Citation** | LDC SS 25-9-33(A); UCM 2.2.3 |
| **Finding** | An SER is required when the nearest accessible main is >100 ft from the property boundary, the connection is to a transmission main (>=24") or interceptor (>=18"), or the parcel is in a Pressure Concern Area or Wastewater Capacity Concern Area. For a duplex on an existing platted lot with prior SFR service, an SER is unlikely but cannot be ruled out without Austin Water confirmation. Pressure and capacity concern areas are administrative AW designations that are not publicly mapped. |
| **Implication** | If an SER is triggered, it adds 3-9 months to the entitlement timeline and does not reserve capacity (UCM 2.2.3(E)). This would be a significant schedule impact for a duplex project. |
| **Recommended next step** | Request written confirmation from Austin Water that no SER is required for this address. Specifically ask whether the parcel is in any Pressure Concern Area or Wastewater Capacity Concern Area. |

### 9. Wastewater Main Size and Gravity Service

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Severity** | data-gap |
| **Citation** | UCM 2.9.4.G.3 (direct tap prohibition >15"); UCM 2.9.4.J (gravity requirement) |
| **Finding** | The size of the wastewater main serving Cinchring Ln was not confirmed during Phase 2 research. If the main exceeds 15 inches, direct service taps are prohibited (UCM 2.9.4.G.3). If the main is a force main rather than gravity, a grinder station at the property line would be required. Given this is a 1990s single-family subdivision, a standard 8-inch gravity main is most likely, but this has not been verified. |
| **Implication** | If the main is oversized or non-gravity, additional infrastructure and an SER would be required, adding cost and schedule. |
| **Recommended next step** | Verify wastewater main size and type (gravity vs. force) via AW GIS or Austin Water correspondence during tap plan preparation. |

## Open Questions for the Engineer

1. **SER status confirmation:** Has Austin Water confirmed in writing that no SER is required for a duplex at this address? Is the parcel in any Pressure Concern Area or Wastewater Capacity Concern Area?

2. **Wastewater main size and type:** What is the diameter and type (gravity vs. force main) of the wastewater main in Cinchring Ln? Is a direct service tap permitted, or is the main >15 inches?

3. **Water main size and pressure zone:** What is the diameter of the water main serving the lot, and what pressure zone is the parcel in? Is the main a distribution main (<24 inches) allowing standard meter taps?

4. **Capital recovery fee tier:** What fee tier does Austin Water assign to this lot based on the Scofield plat recording date? Is the pre-2018 schedule applicable?

5. **Sewer vault disposition:** What is the function and condition of the existing concrete sanitary sewer vault? Does Austin Water require removal, capping, or can it be incorporated into the new service design?

6. **Distance to mains:** What is the distance from the property boundary to the nearest accessible water and wastewater mains? Is either >100 ft (SER trigger)?

7. **Yard-line routing:** Given the 5-ft PUE, blanket electrical easement, and declarant's reserved easement rights, are there any conflicts that would constrain the routing of new water and wastewater yard lines or meter placement?

8. **Demolition sequencing:** What is the required sequence for disconnecting existing water and wastewater service, demolishing the existing house and sewer vault, and establishing new service connections? Does Austin Water require a pre-demolition inspection?
