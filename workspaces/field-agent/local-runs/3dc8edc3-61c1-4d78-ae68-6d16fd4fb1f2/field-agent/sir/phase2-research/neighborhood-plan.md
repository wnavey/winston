# Neighborhood Plan & Comprehensive Plan Context

**Property:** 12713 Cinchring Ln, Austin, TX 78727 (Lot 12, Block M, Scofield Subdivision, Travis County)
**Proposed project:** Demolish existing SFR; construct duplex (2 units, ground-up)

> **Legal note:** Per TX LGC Section 213.005, comprehensive plans (including the Future Land Use Map) are policy documents, not regulatory instruments. They inform but do not independently control entitlements.

---

## 1. Adopted Neighborhood Plan

**Finding: No adopted neighborhood plan covers this parcel.**

The City of Austin has adopted 31 neighborhood plans, primarily in central and south Austin. The Scofield Farms area in far north Austin (Parmer/MoPac) is not within any adopted neighborhood planning area. The nearest adopted plans are North Shoal Creek, North Lamar Combined, and North Burnet/Gateway, all of which are several miles south of this parcel.

Because there is no adopted neighborhood plan, there is no Neighborhood Plan Contact Team (NPCT) with standing to review zoning or land-use cases for this area under the neighborhood plan framework.

- [City of Austin - Neighborhood Plans and Resources](https://www.austintexas.gov/planning/neighborhood-plans-and-resources)
- [WGI - Neighborhood Planning Areas in Austin](https://wginc.com/neighborhood-planning-areas-in-austin/)

## 2. NP Combining District

**Finding: No NP combining district applies.**

The NP (Neighborhood Plan) combining district is applied only within adopted neighborhood planning areas and is appended to the base zoning string (e.g., SF-3-NP). Since Scofield is outside any adopted neighborhood plan area, no NP combining district appears in the zoning for parcels in this subdivision. The property's zoning string does not carry an -NP suffix.

- [Austin LDC Section 25-2, Subchapter A, Art. 2, Div. 6 - Combining and Overlay Districts](https://library.municode.com/tx/austin/codes/code_of_ordinances?nodeId=TIT25LADE_CH25-2ZO_SUBCHAPTER_A._ZONING_USES_DISTRICTS_MAP_DISTRICT_DESIGNATIONS_ART2ZODI_DIV6COOVDI)

## 3. Future Land Use Map (FLUM)

**Finding: The FLUM designation for this area is consistent with single-family / suburban residential land use.**

Areas without an adopted neighborhood plan do not have a parcel-specific FLUM adopted by City Council. The Imagine Austin Comprehensive Plan's Growth Concept Map provides the applicable policy-level land-use guidance for this parcel. The Scofield Farms subdivision is a developed single-family residential area, and the FLUM Composite dataset maintained by the City reflects this existing character.

Per TX LGC Section 213.005, the FLUM is advisory policy -- it does not regulate lot-level entitlements or prohibit by-right uses allowed under the base zoning district.

- [City of Austin - Future Land Use Map Composite (Open Data)](https://data.austintexas.gov/Locations-and-Maps/Future-Land-Use-Map-Composite/acz6-tpe2)
- [City of Austin - FLUM / Growth Concept Map Comparison](https://www.austintexas.gov/sites/default/files/files/iacp_flum-gcm-compare2.pdf)

## 4. Imagine Austin Growth Concept Map

**Finding: The parcel is near the Parmer Lane Activity Corridor but is not within an Activity Center.**

The Imagine Austin Growth Concept Map designates Parmer Lane as an Activity Corridor, reflecting the roadway's role as a major employment and commercial spine (Apple, Samsung, GM, and 3M campuses). 12713 Cinchring Ln is located approximately 0.5 miles south of Parmer Lane, placing it in the corridor's influence area but not directly on the corridor itself.

No Activity Center is mapped at the Parmer/MoPac intersection. The nearest mapped Activity Center is the Tech Ridge center at I-35 and Parmer, roughly 3.5 miles east.

Imagine Austin policy supports incremental density (including "missing middle" housing) near activity corridors, but this is policy guidance, not a regulatory mandate.

## 5. Station-Area / Transit-Oriented Plans

**Finding: No TOD overlay applies. No Project Connect station is planned near this parcel.**

The Project Connect Orange Line runs along the North Lamar/Guadalupe corridor, roughly 2 miles east of MoPac. The nearest planned station in the initial investment is the North Lamar Transit Center (at Lamar and US-183), approximately 4 miles south of this property. Future extension stations at Parmer Lane and Tech Ridge are along the I-35/North Lamar alignment, not MoPac, and are not included in the initial investment phase -- they will operate as MetroRapid bus stops until light rail is extended (estimated 2040s).

No Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) overlay district has been adopted for any area near this parcel. The City of Austin's equitable TOD planning efforts have focused on the Blue Line (east-west) and the initial Orange Line corridor segments, none of which reach the Scofield area.

## 6. Active Neighborhood Associations

**Finding: The Scofield Farms ROA is the primary community organization. No public posture on density/duplex development was identified.**

The **Scofield Residential Owners Association (ROA)** governs approximately 574 homes in the Scofield Farms community. It is managed by Inframark and led by a 5-member Board of Directors elected annually. The ROA maintains an Architectural Control Committee (ACC) that reviews proposed changes to homes and landscaping.

A separate organization, **The Ridge at Scofield Farms**, covers a distinct section of the broader Scofield development.

No public statements, meeting minutes, or advocacy positions regarding infill density, duplex construction, or zoning changes were identified on the ROA's website or in public records. The ACC's purview is limited to aesthetic and architectural standards within the existing HOA covenants, not municipal zoning or land-use entitlements.

Applicants should check the [City of Austin Community Registry](https://www.austintexas.gov/development-services/community-registry) to confirm which registered organizations receive notice for development applications at this address.
