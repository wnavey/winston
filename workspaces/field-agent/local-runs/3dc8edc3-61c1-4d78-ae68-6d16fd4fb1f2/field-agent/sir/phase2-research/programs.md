# Programs & Opportunities

**Site:** 12713 Cinchring Ln, Austin, TX 78727 (Lot 12, Block M, Scofield Subdivision, Travis County)
**Zoning:** SF-2 (Single-Family Residential, Standard Lot)
**Proposed use:** Demolish existing SFR; build ground-up duplex (2 units)

---

## 1. HOME Initiative Phase 1

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | Likely eligible |
| **Status** | Adopted Dec 7, 2023 (Ord. 20231207-001); effective for new applications Feb 5, 2024 |
| **What it does** | Allows duplex, two-unit, and three-unit residential uses on SF-1, SF-2, and SF-3 lots citywide. Eliminates the prior conditional-use-permit requirement for duplexes on SF-2/SF-3 lots. |
| **Key standards (SF-2/SF-3)** | Building coverage: 40%; impervious cover: 45%; FAR: 55% or 3,200 sf (whichever is greater) for two-unit; height: 30 ft (two stories); minimum lot size: 5,750 sf. Subchapter F setback advantages are incorporated for all properties. No minimum parking (citywide parking reform adopted Nov 2, 2023). |
| **Benefit** | A duplex is permitted by right on this SF-2 lot. No rezoning, no CUP, no conditional overlay needed. Simplified design standards -- no sidewall articulation or tent requirement. |
| **Obligation** | Must comply with tree ordinance and modified McMansion standards. No affordability set-aside required. |
| **Citation** | LDC Sec. 25-2-773 (repealed and replaced); Ordinance No. 20231207-001 |

## 3. Affordability Unlocked

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | ? Requires verification -- likely impractical for a 2-unit project |
| **Status** | Active (Ord. 20190509-027) |
| **What it does** | Grants density bonuses, height increases, parking waivers, and compatibility exemptions in exchange for deep affordability commitments. Two tiers: Type 1 and Type 2. |
| **Benefit** | Increased height and density, reduced parking, lot-size reductions, compatibility waivers. |
| **Obligation** | 50% of total units must be affordable (rental: avg 60% MFI, with 20% at 50% MFI or below; ownership: avg 80% MFI). 25% of affordable units must be 2BR+. 99-year affordability period. Requires Housing Dept certification before site plan or building permit. |
| **Assessment** | For a 2-unit duplex, this means 1 of 2 units at deep affordability for 99 years. The trade-off is unlikely to pencil for a small-scale market-rate project. May be relevant if the developer pursues an affordable housing mission or seeks LIHTC/other subsidy. |
| **Citation** | Ordinance No. 20190509-027; LDC Sec. 25-2-Subchapter E, Art. 4 |

## 7. RSMP (Regional Stormwater Management Program)

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | ? Requires verification |
| **Status** | Active (est. 1980s; updated Oct 1, 2020) |
| **What it does** | Allows qualifying developments to make a fee-in-lieu payment instead of constructing on-site stormwater detention, provided no adverse flooding impacts to neighboring properties result from increased runoff. |
| **Benefit** | Avoids cost and space burden of on-site detention pond. Fee is calculated based on appraised land value, proposed impervious cover, and estimated detention facility construction cost. |
| **Obligation** | Payment-in-lieu calculated via RSMP Payment Calculator. Funds are spent in the same watershed on flood-risk-reduction projects. |
| **Assessment** | For a duplex on a ~7,200 sf lot, on-site detention may not be required at all under Site Plan Lite / HOME drainage exemptions (see item 8). If detention is triggered, RSMP fee-in-lieu is a potential alternative. Eligibility depends on watershed and discharge conditions. Verify with Watershed Protection Dept (RSMP@austintexas.gov). |
| **Citation** | Drainage Criteria Manual Sec. 1.2.2.G and Sec. 8; LDC Ch. 25-7 |

## 8. Site Plan Lite

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Eligibility** | Likely eligible |
| **Status** | Phase 1 adopted Jul 2023; Phase 2 adopted Mar 6, 2025 (applications accepted Jun 16, 2025) |
| **What it does** | Streamlines the permitting process for small residential projects. Phase 1 exempts 3-4 unit projects from site plan requirements entirely. Phase 2 creates a simplified "Small Project Site Plan" for 5-16 unit projects. |
| **Benefit for a duplex** | A 2-unit duplex is below the 3-4 unit threshold, so it was already exempt from site plan under prior rules (building permit only). However, the associated drainage reforms are significant: lots of 11,500 sf or less do not require drainage review; lots up to 1 acre do not require on-site detention or an engineered drainage plan if the site drains to the street or existing drainage system. |
| **Obligation** | Submit commercial or residential building permit application. Must meet basic drainage discharge requirements. |
| **Assessment** | This lot (~7,200 sf) qualifies for the drainage review exemption. This is a meaningful cost and timeline savings -- no engineered drainage study, no detention pond required. |
| **Citation** | Ordinance adopted Mar 6, 2025 (Site Plan Lite Phase 2 & Infill Plat); LDC Sec. 25-2, residential infill provisions |

## Key Takeaways for This Project

1. **HOME Phase 1 is the foundational entitlement.** It permits a duplex by right on this SF-2 lot with no rezoning, no CUP, and no affordability requirements. This is the primary enabling program.

2. **Site Plan Lite drainage reforms provide meaningful savings.** At ~7,200 sf, the lot qualifies for drainage review exemption, eliminating the need for an engineered drainage study or on-site detention.

3. **No parking minimums apply.** Austin's citywide parking reform (Nov 2023) removed minimum off-street parking requirements. The developer can size parking to market demand.

4. **Affordability-linked programs (Affordability Unlocked, S.M.A.R.T.) are available but impractical at this scale** unless the developer is pursuing a mission-driven or subsidized affordable project.

5. **ETOD, VMU, DB90, and QOZ are inapplicable** due to the parcel's suburban SF-2 zoning and north Austin location.
