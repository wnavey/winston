## 1. Water -- Austin Water

| Component   | Per Service Unit |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Water       | $4,800           |
| Wastewater  | $2,900           |
| **Combined**| **$7,700**       |

A duplex = 2 service units, so the combined capital recovery fees could be approximately **$15,400** (2 x $7,700) at the highest tier. Actual amount may be lower depending on plat date. Meter and inspection fees are additional.

## 9. Soil Conditions

The 78727 area of north Austin lies in the **Blackland Prairie** ecological region, characterized by deep, dark, clay-rich soils (Vertisols). Likely soil series in this area include **Houston Black clay** and related series (Altoga, Branyon, Heiden).

**Key characteristics:**
- **Shrink-swell potential:** HIGH. These "cracking clays" expand when wet and contract when dry, forming deep cracks. This is the dominant foundation risk in Austin.
- **Foundation recommendation:** Engineered slab-on-grade with post-tensioned cables or drilled piers. A geotechnical report is strongly recommended before design.
- **Septic suitability:** Poor (high-clay, low-permeability), but not relevant here as city sewer is available.

A site-specific geotechnical investigation should be conducted prior to foundation design.

**Source:** [USDA Web Soil Survey](https://websoilsurvey.nrcs.usda.gov/app/) | [Travis County Soils](https://travis-tx.tamu.edu/about-2/horticulture/soils-and-composting-for-austin/the-real-dirt-on-austin-area-soils/)

## 11. HOA -- Scofield Farms Residential Owners Association (ROA)

**THIS IS A CRITICAL FEASIBILITY ISSUE FOR THE DUPLEX.**

**HOA:** Scofield Residential Owners Association, Inc.
**Management:** Inframark (formerly Certified Management of Austin)
**Contact:** communitymanagement@inframark.com | 512-287-8000
**Dues:** Reported as under $40/month (~$480/year); includes pool access keycard.
**Website:** [scofieldfarms.org](https://scofieldfarms.org/)

**Architectural Control Committee (ACC):**
- 5-member committee reviews proposed additions and changes.
- ACC approval is required for exterior modifications; contact modifications@inframark.com.

**Deed Restrictions -- Duplex Feasibility Risk:**
Scofield Farms has a Declaration of Covenants, Conditions and Deed Restrictions (with at least 8 amendments). The actual deed restriction documents are hosted on the HOA website but their full text was not publicly viewable during this research. Typical 1980s-90s subdivision CCRs in Texas **restrict lots to single-family residential use**, which would prohibit a duplex regardless of what city zoning (HOME initiative) allows.

**Key legal principle:** Under Texas law, city zoning is the floor and deed restrictions are the ceiling. The HOME initiative explicitly does not override private deed restrictions. If the Scofield Farms CCRs restrict the property to single-family use, a duplex cannot be built without either (a) obtaining a variance/waiver from the HOA, or (b) the HOA choosing not to enforce the restriction.

**Action Required:** Before proceeding with duplex design, the owner must obtain and review the Scofield Farms Declaration of Covenants, Conditions and Deed Restrictions (and all amendments) to determine whether multi-family or duplex use is prohibited. This document can be requested from Inframark or found in the Travis County deed records.

**Source:** [Scofield Farms HOA](https://scofieldfarms.org/) | [HOA Rules & Regs Documents](https://scofieldfarms.org/scofield-farms-roa-rules-and-regulations-documents/) | [Community Not Commodity -- HOA & HOME](https://communitynotcommodity.com/2023/11/17/if-you-have-deed-restrictions-or-live-in-an-hoa-city-halls-h-o-m-e-rezoning-plan-impacts-you/)
