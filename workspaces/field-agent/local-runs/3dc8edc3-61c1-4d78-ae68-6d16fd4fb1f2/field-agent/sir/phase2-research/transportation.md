## 3. ROW Dedication

Under Austin's Transportation Criteria Manual (TCM) and ASMP, ROW dedication is required when the existing ROW is narrower than the ASMP future ROW standard. For Cinchring Lane (Level 1, 50-ft standard), the existing platted ROW in the Scofield Subdivision is expected to already meet the 50-ft standard. Therefore, **no additional ROW dedication is anticipated** for this project.

If any dedication delta were identified during site plan review, the city would require a dedication along the property frontage as a condition of the building permit or site plan approval. The City of Austin's Residential Review Guidelines (rev. November 2025) govern ROW review for residential projects.

## 4. Traffic Impact Analysis (TIA)

Austin requires a TIA when a proposed development generates **2,000 or more net new vehicular trips per day**. The TIA Determination Worksheet must be submitted to the Transportation Development Services (TDS) Division.

| Metric | Value |

**Conclusion: No TIA is required.** The net new trip generation (~10 trips/day) is far below the 2,000-trip threshold. No TIA Determination Worksheet filing is necessary for a project of this scale.

## 5. Access Management -- Driveway Requirements

Austin's TCM Section 7 (Driveways) governs driveway design and spacing. For a duplex on a Level 1 local street:

- **Maximum driveways**: Typically 1-2, depending on lot frontage.
- **Driveway width**: Residential driveway maximum 24 ft (two-way) per TCM.
- **Spacing**: Minimum 35 ft between driveways (edge to edge) if semicircular layout used.
- **Setback from intersection**: Minimum 30 ft from the nearest cross-street curb return.
- **Construction**: Driveway must be constructed within the street frontage of the subject property; no overlap onto adjacent property frontage without written approval.

A single shared driveway serving both duplex units is the most common configuration and satisfies all spacing requirements.

## 7. Bicycle and Pedestrian Infrastructure

### Sidewalks
Austin's Land Development Code and Residential Review Guidelines require sidewalks as part of residential development. For a duplex redevelopment on a local street:

- **Sidewalk requirement**: A minimum 5-ft sidewalk is typically required along the property frontage within the ROW.
- **Fee-in-lieu**: If physical constraints prevent sidewalk construction, a fee-in-lieu payment may be accepted per LDE Residential Review Guidelines.
- **Existing conditions**: Cinchring Lane in the Scofield Subdivision may have existing sidewalks on one or both sides from the original subdivision plat; the project must maintain or improve frontage sidewalk to current standards.

### Bicycle Facilities
No dedicated bike lanes exist on Cinchring Lane. As a low-speed, low-volume Level 1 residential street, shared-lane (bicycle-with-traffic) operation is the applicable standard. No bike lane construction is required as part of this project.

The nearest designated bicycle infrastructure is along Parmer Lane and MoPac frontage roads, per the Austin Bicycle Plan and ASMP Active Transportation component.

## 8. Scenic Roadway Overlay

The Austin scenic roadway overlay applies to specific designated corridors including Loop 360, RM 2222 (west of MoPac), Barton Springs Road, Lake Austin Boulevard, and others. **Cinchring Lane is not a designated scenic roadway.** Neither Scofield Ridge Parkway nor Parmer Lane in this segment carries a scenic roadway overlay designation.

**No scenic roadway overlay requirements apply to this project.**
