R4Detention-governed buildable footprint — the canonical example significant · flagship SIR p. 6

The report's flagship geo-spatial finding, and the one the user pointed at. It fuses two artifacts — the operator's concept plan / marketing graphic (which markets a net ~1.56 ac) and a county-clerk-pulled recorded plat carrying the image of the 20-ft sewer/drainage easement — against the county GIS gross area, then subtracts on the ground and detects that the wash's own basin wants the same footprint the easement already occupies. That overlap is where and when the agent concludes buildable land is reduced.
