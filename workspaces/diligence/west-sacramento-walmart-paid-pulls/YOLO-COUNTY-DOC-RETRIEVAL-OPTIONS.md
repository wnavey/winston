## Bottom line (read this first)

- Everything we need is recorded and identifiable in Yolo County. The question is **how much to spend and how**.
- The load‑bearing document — the center's **REA / Declaration of CC&Rs** — is **heavily amended**: 1 master declaration + a second master declaration + **27 amendments** (≈29 restriction instruments total). Buying and reading all of them piecemeal runs **~$800** and leaves you to reconcile 27 amendments by hand.

- **Two realistic choices for your money:**
  1. **County self‑service copies (DIY):** cheapest for a *targeted* set. A minimal "read the operative frame" set (deed + both master declarations + both maps) is **≈ $207**. The complete restriction history (with all 27 amendments) is **≈ $807**.

This is the standard diligence move and removes the 27‑amendment reconciliation burden.

**Recommendation:** order a **preliminary title report** (option 2). If you'd rather stay in‑house, do the **targeted DIY set (~$207)**, starting with the deed, and expand only if the REA points to something unresolved.

## Clarifying the "online vs. phone" question — it's one order, one price

There are **not** two differently‑priced retrieval paths. There is **one** path:

1. **You (or we) find and select the documents online** in the county's free self‑service portal and add them to a cart. County staff **will not search for you** ("Clerk‑Recorder personnel do not perform searches").
2. **You place the order online** — no account/login required, just a name.
3. **You pay by phone.** The portal **does not take payment online**; after placing the order you call **(530) 666‑8130** and give a credit card. (Payment can alternatively be made in person or by mailed/faxed application.)
4. **Delivery:** a *plain* copy ("Official Record Copy") arrives **by email**; a *certified* copy arrives **by mail only**.

So "online order" and "phone order" are the **same order** — built online, paid by phone. **The price is identical no matter how you pay.** The only things that change the price are:

- **Page count** — every copy is **$5.00 for the first page + $2.00 each additional page**.
- **Plain vs. certified** — certification adds **$3.00 per document** (and forces mail delivery). For reading and analysis, **plain email copies are sufficient**; certified copies are only needed to rely on a document in a binding legal proceeding.

> Page counts are shown in the free index, so **the exact price of every document is known before you buy**. There is no per‑document surprise and no online "convenience" surcharge — the online fee equals the county's standard copy fee.

**Fee formula:** `price = $5 + $2 × (pages − 1)` for a plain copy; add `$3` per document if certified.

## Practical caveats

- **Index lag:** the online index updates nightly (M–F) and lags recording by ~3 business days. Fine for these historical documents.
- **Which map governs our lot:** both maps are cheap ($25 + $13); pull both rather than guess. The 2008 Parcel Map No. 4918 is the likely lot‑creating map (map year 08/09), but the 2006 Subdivision No. 4797 is the master.
- **Certified vs. plain:** plain (email) copies are sufficient to read and analyze. Only certify if a document must be relied on in a binding/legal context.
- **No payment happens without a person.** We can identify, price, and stage the exact order to the "Place your Order" screen; a human then calls to pay. Anthropic/Claude never handles the card.
