### The gap

This report identifies **52 checklist items** across guides 1, 2, and 13 that would benefit from distance measurement ("Yes" classification). The experiment's agents invoked the tool against only **2 of the 3 guide files** (guides 2 and 13), and **0 invocations** targeted guide 1 despite it containing 25 "Yes" items.

**Specific items the tool was used for vs. this report's "Yes" items:**

For **guide 2**, the experiment's successful measurement (run-3, sheet 31: mitigation trees to OHE line) maps most directly to:
- **EL-2.1** (non-UC trees within 15 feet of distribution conductor) — the tool measured tree-to-conductor distance

This report classifies 9 of 15 guide-2 items as "Yes." The experiment attempted 2 calls and got 1 successful measurement, meaning most distance-measurable items went unmeasured.

For **guide 13**, the experiment's 2 successful measurements (run-1 sheet 21: pad to building wall; run-2 sheet 19: pad to driveway) map to:
- **EL-13.1** (pad to building, 5-foot threshold) — pad-to-Bldg-2 measurement
- **EL-13.38** (pad within 4 feet of parking/vehicle routes, bollard trigger) — the only Tier A citation in the entire experiment

This report classifies 18 of 39 guide-13 items as "Yes." The experiment got 2 successful measurements against 2 items, leaving 16 distance-measurable items without tool data.

For **guide 1**, no tool calls were made at all. All 25 "Yes" items went completely unmeasured.

### Key findings

1. **Massive coverage gap in guide 1.** The largest guide (47 items, 25 requiring measurement) saw zero tool invocations. This guide covers the core overhead-line-to-site-feature clearances that are the most natural use case for horizontal distance measurement (building-to-conductor, fence-to-conductor, pole-to-driveway, etc.).

2. **Low invocation rate even where the tool was used.** Guides 2 and 13 together have 27 "Yes" items but saw only 7 invocations (3 successful). The agents are not systematically using the tool for every distance-relevant item.

3. **Reliability bottleneck limits effective coverage.** With a 68.4% failure rate (mostly timeouts from option-a vector analysis), even items where the agent attempts the tool often get no usable data back. Of 19 total invocations, only 6 produced results.

4. **Vertical-clearance items correctly avoided.** The tool was never invoked for items this report classifies as "Conditional" due to vertical clearance requirements (EL-1.18 through EL-1.21, EL-1.43, EL-1.44, EL-13.29), which is appropriate — the tool cannot measure vertical distances.

5. **52 items could benefit; 3 actually did.** Only 3 of 52 distance-measurable items received successful tool measurements in the experiment (EL-2.1, EL-13.1, EL-13.38). This represents a 5.8% effective coverage rate of the opportunity space.
