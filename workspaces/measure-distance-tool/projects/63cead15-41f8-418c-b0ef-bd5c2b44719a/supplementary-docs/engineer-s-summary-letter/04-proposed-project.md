# Proposed Project

*Outlines the proposed construction of 39 townhomes, water quality controls, and a reduction in impervious cover.*

**Proposed Project**
This project consists of the construction of 8 buildings consisting of thirty-nine townhomes, a water quality and detention pond, and associated parking and driveways. The proposed impervious cover is 60%.

Water quality and detention for the improvements will be treated by a proposed stacked biofiltration and detention pond. Calculations provided in the drainage report show proposed flows generated from the site are below existing conditions.
