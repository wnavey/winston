# Completeness Check Results

City of Austin site plan completeness check results detailing reviewer statuses and applicant responses to deficiencies.

## Sections

1. **Project Information and Review Status** (p. 1)
   Provides general project details, tracking numbers, and a summary table of completeness statuses for various city reviewers.
   File: `01-project-information-and-review-status.md`
2. **Drainage and Water Quality Review** (pp. 2-3)
   Comments from the drainage reviewer regarding storm drain connectivity and off-site runoff, along with the applicant's responses and information-only notes.
   File: `02-drainage-and-water-quality-review.md`
3. **City Arborist Review** (p. 3)
   Informational comments from the City Arborist regarding the proposed removal of a 25-inch Heritage Live Oak tree and variance requirements.
   File: `03-city-arborist-review.md`
4. **Land Development Engineering Review** (pp. 3-4)
   Informational requirements for License Agreements, Encroachment Agreements, Easement Releases, and ROW Vacations.
   File: `04-land-development-engineering-review.md`
5. **ATD Review** (p. 4)
   Comments from the transportation department requesting dimensions and details for driveways and behind-the-curb improvements, along with applicant responses.
   File: `05-atd-review.md`
6. **Austin Energy Review** (p. 5)
   Confirmation from Austin Energy that the completeness check is complete.
   File: `06-austin-energy-review.md`
