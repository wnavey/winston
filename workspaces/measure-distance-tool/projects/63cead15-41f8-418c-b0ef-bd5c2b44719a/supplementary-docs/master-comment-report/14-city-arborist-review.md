CA2 – Current Status: Pending
* U1: Not addressed. A heritage American elm is shown to be removed on landscape sheets. 
* U0: Administrative Variance
An administrative variance may be granted for a heritage tree that does not have at least one stem that is 30 inches in diameter or larger to be removed only after determining by the City Arborist that the tree is dead, diseased, or an imminent hazard. If not dead, diseased or an imminent hazard, clearly show that the tree prevents reasonable use or access of the property and that all design options have been exhausted.
Reference: LDC Section 25-8-642

The following items are required to review an administrative variance request to remove a Heritage tree:
1. Please provide a variance request memo on letterhead (any letterhead works) that states tree id, species and DBH of heritage tree(s) proposed for removal. Reference LDC 25-8-642 to prepare the memo.
2. Letter shall include applicant findings per the LDC and provide alternative layouts demonstrating preservation is not feasible. 
3. Please pay the administrative variance fee. This fee is added once the memo has been received. 
4. If removal is condition related, provide a third-party arborist report with clear photos of the tree’s condition. Please provide a ISA basic tree risk assessment report if trying to claim DDI status. 
5. If a variance, waiver, exemption, modification, or alternative compliance from another City Code provision would eliminate the need for a variance from LDC Section 25-8-641 before requesting a variance to allow removal of a heritage tree. Please demonstrate that a variance, waiver, exemption, modification, or alternative compliance from another City Code provision that would eliminate the need for a variance has been denied.

* U0: Existing Impacts & Meeting 50%
Proposed impacts in the Half or Quarter CRZ are allowed within the footprint of existing impacts if they do not exceed the depth or width of those impacts. Additional depth of impact may be allowed if the applicant demonstrates the absence of significant roots. 
To determine whether roots are likely to have extended under an existing impact, please contact a private arborist to air-excavate in the critical root zone adjacent to the edge of the impact (slab foundation, asphalt drive, etc)The arborist needs to air-excavate a 1ft width along the entire span of the critical root zone that has been impacted. 
The air-excavation should be at a 24 inch depth. 
The arborist needs to take pictures that clearly show whether or not roots exist along the area of the trench, extending into the impacted area.
Please provide pictures showing the root system prior to it being backfilled with soil. Ensure that the pictures are well-lit, and the view is not obstructed.

Excessive pruning constitutes removal of a regulated tree per Land Development Code 25-8-603. Pruning shall conform to the most recent ANSI A300 pruning standard. CA10 – Current Status: Pending
* U1: comment pending
* U0: Grading
Modify the proposed grading to meet preservation criteria.
A grade change of a few inches can be detrimental to a tree, yet most sites require extensive cut and fill in order to manage drainage flow. Some design alternatives which can be used to preserve significant trees are as follows:
  o Use berms or retaining walls instead of cutting to provide detention.
  o Provide tree well and/or aeration systems for trees in fill areas (see City of Austin Standard Specifications and City of Austin Standards).
  o Provide retaining walls to mitigate cuts and fills (see Figure 3-10 in Appendix V of this manual).
Reference: ECM 3.5.2 .A.3

No less than 75% of the total caliper inches of mitigation trees required must be represented by significant shade trees located in the ECM Appendix F. The remaining 25% of total caliper inches may be represented by a mixture of the ECM Appendix F tree species.
All trees selected must be suitable for the environment of the immediate planting site (see ECM Appendix F for specific categorization such as tree, soil, site, or regulatory qualities). Eligible replacement tree species are listed in Environmental Criteria Manual Appendix F. The City Arborist may approve species not listed in Appendix F for mitigation credit.
Reference: ECM 3.5.4 2. B.1.b

  1. When twenty or more inches of replacement trees are planted on a site they must be selected from at least two species.
  2. When one hundred or more inches of replacement trees are planted on a site they must be selected from at least five species, with no single species representing more than fifty percent of the total caliper of the required mitigation.
