# Sheet 28 — Construction Details 7

Construction details for storm drain manholes and catch basins.

## Reading Guide

This sheet provides construction details and standard specifications for storm drainage infrastructure, specifically focusing on City of Austin standards for manholes and ParkUSA catch basins. It includes detailed cross-sections, plan views, and dimensional tables to guide the installation of pre-cast and cast-in-place drainage structures.

**Key values:**
*   **Catch Basin Concrete Specification:** 4500 PSI design strength at 28 days
*   **Catch Basin Reinforcement:** Grade 60 steel rebar conforming to ASTM A615
*   **Catch Basin Castings:** Grey cast iron conforming to ASTM A48-76 Class 30
*   **Catch Basin Sizes:** Models range from CB-12 (12"x12"x24", 180 lbs) to CB-84 (84"x84"x72", 19,500 lbs)
*   **Manhole Frame & Cover Size:** 800 mm (32") or 813 mm (32")
*   **Manhole Lid Weights:** 325 N (73 lbs) for cast iron; 778 N (175 lbs) for ductile iron
*   **Manhole Lid Load Test Requirement:** 20,000-pound proof load test (AASHTO M-306)
*   **Minimum Manhole Diameter to Pipe Size Ratios:**
    *   1.2 m (4') dia. for 450 to 600 mm (18" to 24") pipes
    *   1.5 m (5') dia. for 750 to 1.05 m (30" to 42") pipes
    *   1.8 m (6') dia. for 1.2 to 1.6 m (48" to 64") pipes
    *   2.1 m (7') dia. for 1.5 to 1.7 m (60" to 66") pipes
*   **Junction Manhole Intersecting Pipes:** Diagram shows 460 mm (18"), 610 mm (24"), and 760 mm (30") I.D. pipes
*   **Box Manhole Requirement:** Used for 760 mm (30") and larger pipes, requiring Class "A" Concrete and Item 406 reinforcing steel
