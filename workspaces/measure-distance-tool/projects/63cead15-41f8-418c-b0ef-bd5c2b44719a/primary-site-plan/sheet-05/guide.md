# Sheet 5 — Erosion and Sedimentation Control Plan

Site layout detailing erosion and sedimentation control measures.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains the Erosion and Sedimentation Control Plan for the Valley View Townhomes project located in Austin, Texas. Its primary purpose is to detail the site's protective environmental measures during construction, illustrating the spatial layout of silt fences, mulch socks, inlet protections, and construction staging areas relative to proposed building footprints and existing topography.

*   **Benchmark 2:** Mag Nail in Asphalt, Elevation = 681.08' NAVD 88
*   **Topsoil Requirement:** Minimum 6 inches of topsoil over subgrade loosened to at least 2 inches depth.
*   **Idle Disturbed Area Limit:** Must be stabilized if not worked on for more than 14 days.
*   **Right-of-Way:** Valley View Road 60' R.O.W.
