# Sheet 2 — Plat

Subdivision plat map showing legal boundaries, easements, and signatures.

## Reading Guide

This sheet displays the historical recorded plat for the Mecey Subdivision (Travis County, 1975), which serves as the base property boundary framework for the current Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to establish the legal boundary dimensions, right-of-way dedications, existing easements, and historical surveyor certifications for Lots 1 and 2 between Valley View Road and Clawson Road.

**Key values:**
*   **Property Recording:** Book 73, Page 76 (Filed December 11, 1975, at 2:10 PM)
*   **Original Survey Date:** August 20, 1975
*   **Base Property Reference:** Lot A, Trico Subdivision (Book 69, Page 14) / Volume 4916, Page 2385
*   **Scale:** 1" = 100 Feet
*   **Curve Data:** Delta (Δ) = 31°25', Tangent (T) = 20.84', Radius (R) = 74.10', Long Chord (L.C.) = 39.12', Arc = 40.63'
*   **Surveyor:** Forrest B. Scott, Reg. Public Surveyor #399 (Austin, Texas)
*   **Current Project Address:** 3759 Valley View Road, Austin, Texas 78704

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

*   **Block 1: drawing — Mecey Subdivision Plat**
    The main drawing area containing the historical plat map, legal descriptions, owner's dedication, surveyor's certification, and approval signatures. 
    *Use VISION to examine* the spatial layout of Lot 1 and Lot 2, their orientation between Valley View Road and Clawson Road, and the location of drainage easements. 
    *READ the block content for* precise boundary bearings/dimensions, curve data details, historical recording information, and standard legal declarations.

*   **Blocks 7, 18: text_block — Project Title**

    Contains the project tracking details.
    *READ the block content for* the Project No. (RJ3 70496) and the initials for Design (CS, JR), CAD (JW, CS), and Review (JKR).

*   **Blocks 2, 13: text_block — Sheet Indexing**
    *READ the block content for* the sheet number (Sheet 2 of 39) and tracking number (SP-2025-0126C).

*   **Blocks 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17: various — Boilerplate & Administrative**
