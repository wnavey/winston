# Sheet 20 — Utility Tap Public Plan & Profile

Utility tap public plan and profile for townhomes project.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains the public plan and profile for a proposed utility tap (Water Line A) for the Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to detail the connection to an existing AC water main, the transition to new DI pipe, and the installation of a new fire demand meter, vault, and fire hydrant.

**Key values:**
* Proposed Pipe Segments: 47.0 lf of 8" DI (@ 0.00% slope), 5.0 lf of 6" DI (@ -0.00% slope), 12.0 lf of 6" DI (@ 0.00% slope), 3.0 lf of 6" C900, 5.0 lf of 6" C900
* Flowline Elevations: FL 8" = 674.33', FL In 6" = 674.42', FL Out 6" = 674.42'
* Restraints: 52' of restraints required from Sta 0+00 to Sta 0+52
* Benchmarks: BM #1 = 678.52' (NAVD 88), BM #2 = 681.08' (NAVD 88)
* Pressure Reducing Valve (PRV) Threshold: 65 psi
* Geologist Inspection Trench Depth Threshold: > 5 feet

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

* **Block 1**: drawing — Profile View of Water Line A (Wtr-Ln-A). Shows the vertical alignment of the proposed water line from station 0+00 to 0+75, including the transition from AC to DI pipe, reducers, and the meter vault. Use VISION to examine the pipe slopes and relationship to the existing/proposed ground surfaces, and READ for exact flowline elevations, lengths, and the 52' restraint dimension.
* **Block 2**: drawing — Plan View of Utility Tap. Top-down layout showing the proposed connection to the existing 6" water line, the new fire hydrant, vault, and various utility crossings. Use VISION to examine the spatial layout of the proposed infrastructure and intersections with existing easements and lines.
* **Block 3**: notes — Restrained Joints Specification. Details the requirement to restrain all bends, tees, and valves using specific SPL standards (WW-27F or WW-27A). READ the block content for the approved mechanical joint restraint standards.
* **Block 4**: notes — Benchmarks. Provides the elevation reference points for the site. READ the block content for exact elevations and datum (NAVD 88).
* **Block 5**: notes — Water Tap Note. Contains instructions for replacing the existing AC pipe with DI pipe. READ the block content for strict asbestos handling and disposal requirements.
* **Block 6**: notes — Legend. Defines linetypes and symbols used in the plan view. READ the block content to accurately interpret the utility lines (Water, Wastewater, Storm, Electric) in Block 2.
* **Block 7**: notes — PRV Note. READ the block content for the specific pressure threshold (65 psi) that requires the installation of a pressure reducing valve.
* **Block 9**: notes — Void and Water Flow Mitigation. READ the block content for the trenching depth threshold (> 5 feet) that triggers a mandatory inspection by a licensed geologist.
* **Blocks 8, 10, 11, 12, 13**: various — Boilerplate & Administrative. Includes the legal description, standard disclaimers regarding existing utility locations, general verification notes, the PE Seal for Jason K. Rodgers, and the Texas 811 logo. READ the block content only if verifying administrative details, legal descriptions, or standard utility coordination notes.
