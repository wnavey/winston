# Sheet 35 — Landscape Calculations

Provides landscape calculations, plant lists, and tree preservation notes.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains landscape and tree preservation calculations, plant schedules, and standard municipal notes for the Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to demonstrate compliance with City of Austin landscape, buffering, and tree mitigation requirements, detailing exact plant species, quantities, and associated fees for tree removal.

**Key values:**
*   **Site & Landscape Areas:** Total Site Area: 94,203 SF; Total Street-Yard Area: 6,314 SF; Street-Yard Landscape Provided: 5,206 SF (Required: 1,263 SF)
*   **Open Space & Buffering:** Private Common Open Space Provided: 5,855 SF (Required: 4,715 SF); Buffer Points Provided: 57 (Required: 40)
*   **Parking Lot Landscape:** Non-Street-Yard Landscape Provided: 816 SF (Required: 60 SF)
*   **Tree Preservation & Mitigation:** Total App F Tree Inches Surveyed: 606 inches; Total App F Tree Inches Removed: 240 inches; Total Invasive Tree Inches Removed: 137 inches
*   **Mitigation Requirements:** Total Mitigation Inches Planted on Site: 55 inches; Remaining Mitigation Inches Required: 162 inches; Amount to be Paid into Fund: $26,900
*   **Key Plant Quantities (Minimum specs):** 10 Cedar Elm (100 gal.), 2 Live Oak (100 gal.), 6 Mexican Redbud (45 gal.), 2 Texas Mountain Laurel (45 gal.)
*   **Groundcover Quantities:** 92 Obedient Plant (4" flat), 72 Frogfruit (4" flat)

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

**Calculations & Mitigation Data**
*   **Block 14:** City of Austin Landscape Calculations. READ for required versus provided area metrics for street yards, parking lots, buffering points, and private common open space.
*   **Blocks 11 & 17:** Tree Preservation Calculations (Heading and Table). READ for an exact breakdown of existing trees, trees removed by size/category, replacement inches required (including 300% multipliers for heritage trees), and final fee-in-lieu calculations. 
*   **Block 15:** COA Required Trees Chart. READ for street yard, buffer, and parking lot tree inch requirements (Total required: 13 inches) versus proposed inches. 
*   **Block 3:** Tree preservation notes. READ for exemptions (Chinaberry, Bradford Pear, and Ash trees require no mitigation).

**Plant List Schedules**
*   **Blocks 6 & 16:** Plant List heading and Shade Trees schedule. READ for exact shade tree species, quantities (10 Cedar Elm, 2 Live Oak), and minimum planting specifications (100 Gal., 4" Cal.). Use VISION to see the associated plan symbols.
*   **Block 12:** Ornamental Trees schedule. READ for species, quantities, and container specs. Use VISION to map symbols to the landscape plan.
*   **Block 18:** Shrubs schedule. READ for shrub species, quantities, container sizes, and maximum spacing requirements. 
*   **Block 13:** Groundcovers schedule. READ for groundcover species, quantities, and spacing specifications.
*   **Block 5:** Plant list notes. READ for contractor instructions regarding unit cost formatting, substitutions, and plant sourcing responsibilities.

**Municipal Notes & Specifications**
*   **Block 1:** Remedial Tree Care Notes. READ for aeration and supplemental nutrient requirements for trees within construction areas.
*   **Block 2:** Special Construction Techniques (ECM 3.5.4 D). READ for specifications on rock sawing, mulch applications, and grading near critical root zones.
*   **Block 4:** Modified & Natural Area Protection Notes. READ for fencing, wrapping, and pruning rules before, during, and after construction.
*   **Block 7:** Site Development Permit Landscape notes. READ for requirements on protective barriers, vegetation buffering, and temporary seeding during specific dates.
*   **Block 8:** Site Development Permit Irrigation notes. READ for TCEQ Chapter 344 compliance, water conservation rules, and final inspection deliverable requirements.

**Boilerplate & Administrative**
*   **Blocks 9, 10, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24:** Project and sheet administrative data. READ for project name (Valley View Townhomes), owner (WM 41 Austin RE, LLC), location (3759 Valley View Road), engineering firm (Blu Fish Collaborative), landscape architect seal, and sheet number (32 of 35).
