# Sheet 24 — Construction Details 3

Standard construction details for water services and trench repairs.

## Reading Guide

This sheet provides standard City of Austin construction details for water infrastructure and pavement repair. Its primary purpose is to specify the installation and abandonment requirements for water services and meters, as well as the structural layering and dimensional requirements for trench repair in existing asphaltic surfaces.

**Key values:**
*   **Trench Repair HMAC Surface:** Minimum 2" (50 mm) Type "D" for local/residential streets; Minimum 3" (75 mm) Type "C" for collector/arterial streets.
*   **Trench Repair Flexible Base:** Replace in kind plus 3" (75 mm), but no less than 12" (300 mm) total thickness. Maximum layer thickness is 12" (300 mm).
*   **Trench Repair Saw Cut:** Minimum 12" (300 mm) wider than undisturbed trench sides, with a minimum 6" (150 mm) cut back on both sides of the trench.
*   **Trench Repair Minimum Cover:** 30" (750 mm) over the utility line.
*   **Damaged Pavement (Outside Trench):** Replace with a base thickness of 10" (250 mm) or match existing, whichever is greater.
*   **Tracer Tape Placement:** 12" (300 mm) below subgrade over the centerline of the pipe, or 18" (450 mm) below finished grade outside pavement limits.
*   **Water Tubing Cover:** Minimum 2' depth of cover behind curb and gutter.
*   **Water Tubing Bend Radii:** Minimum 3' radius for 1" tubing; minimum 5' radius for 2" tubing.
*   **Meter Box Placement:** Top of box should be 4" above ground, located behind the curb next to the property line/easement, and no more than 36" below final grade.
*   **Abandonment Removal Depth:** Remove all materials to 36" below grade if a ball valve coupling does not exist.

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

*   **Blocks 1 & 5:** diagram — Trench Repair Cross-sections. Use VISION to examine the structural layers (HMAC, base, subgrade, bedding), cutback locations, and minimum dimensional callouts for asphalt trench repair.
*   **Block 14:** notes — Trench Repair Notes. READ the block content for specific pavement and base replacement thicknesses, asphalt material types based on street classification, concrete substitution allowances, and tracer tape placement requirements.
*   **Blocks 3 & 4:** diagram — Water Service Tap Details (Plan & Profile). Use VISION to examine the spatial layout, hardware orientation, and required clearances (e.g., 2' min, 4'-1/4" min) relative to the right-of-way/easement lines.
*   **Block 13:** notes — Water Service Installation Notes. READ the block content for critical installation rules including wrapping requirements, minimum bend radii, meter box placement, and specific requirements for reclaimed water components (purple parts/paint).
*   **Block 16:** notes — Water Service Materials List. READ the block content for exact material specifications (splices, stops, valves, tubing) and the explicit division of items to be installed by the plumber versus those purchased from Austin Water.
*   **Block 2:** diagram — Abandonment of Services at the Main (Multiple Meters). Use VISION to observe the required locations for physical separation and the limits of material removal.
*   **Blocks 12 & 17:** notes — Abandonment of Services (Multiple Meters) Notes & Materials. READ the block content for the step-by-step procedural requirements, required removal depths (36" below grade), polyethylene wrapping, and the required materials list (A-P).
*   **Blocks 15 & 18:** notes — Abandonment of Services (Single Meter) Notes & Materials. READ the block content for single-meter abandonment steps and materials, which largely mirror the multiple-meter requirements but exclude the tee/wye component.
*   **Blocks 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 19, 20:** various — Boilerplate & Administrative. These blocks contain standard City of Austin detail title blocks, the primary sheet title block (Bleyl Engineering, Valley View Townhomes), and the engineer's seal.
