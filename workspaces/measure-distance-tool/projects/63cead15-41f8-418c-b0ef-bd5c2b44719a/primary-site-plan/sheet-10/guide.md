# Sheet 10 — Fire Protection Plan

Site plan illustrating fire lanes, hydrants, and fire zone details.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains the Fire Protection Plan for the Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to detail the site's fire access routing, fire lane markings, fire hydrant locations, and necessary suppression system requirements to ensure compliance with the City of Austin Fire Code and NFPA standards. 

**Key values**:
* Building 1: 3,290 sf, FFE = 687.50'
* Building 2: 4,830 sf, FFE = 685.50'
* Building 3: 4,144 sf, FFE = 685.50'
* Building 4: 4,144 sf, FFE = 685.50'
* Building 5: 3,290 sf, FFE = 683.0'
* Building 6: 3,290 sf, FFE = 683.0'
* Building 7: 4,144 sf, FFE = 685.0'
* Building 8: 4,997 sf, FFE = 685.50'
* Total private fire hydrants on site: 1
* Required fire suppression system: NFPA 13D for all buildings
* Fire Zone curb stenciling: 4-inch high white letters at 30-foot intervals on painted red curbs
* Fire Zone signage dimensions: 305 mm x 457 mm overall, with 38 mm to 76.2 mm lettering heights
* Benchmark 1: Mag Nail in Asphalt, Elevation = 678.52' NAVD 88 (GEOID 18)
* Benchmark 2: Mag Nail in Asphalt, Elevation = 681.08' NAVD 88 (GEOID 18)

**Content blocks on this sheet**:

* **Block 2: drawing — Main Site Plan**
  Contains the detailed site layout showing buildings 1-8, fire lanes, fire hydrants, walls, fences, and utility easements.
  Use VISION for spatial layout of fire access routing, hydrant placement, and building footprints.

* **Block 11: notes — Fire Zone Notes**
  Contains rules for fire zone demarcation, including standard detail references and curb painting instructions.
  READ for exact specifications on signage placement, red curb painting with 4-inch stenciling, and the requirement that all private hydrants must be painted red.

* **Block 9: notes — General System Notes**
  Contains requirements for underground main installation and testing for sprinkler systems.
  READ for exact testing specifications (NFPA 13 and NFPA 24) and the required Knox-box location at the exterior fire riser room doors.

* **Block 7: notes — Private Hydrant Note**
  Details the City of Austin Water and Wastewater Utility requirements regarding the site's hydrants.
  READ to confirm the presence of 1 private hydrant and the owner's legal obligation for annual service and flow testing.

* **Block 3: notes — Fire Riser Room Note**
  Specifies the required internal fire suppression system.
  READ to confirm the NFPA 13D system requirement for all proposed buildings.

* **Block 1: diagram — Fire Zone Signage Detail**
  Contains City of Austin Standard Detail 901S-6 for "NO PARKING FIRE ZONE TOW-AWAY ZONE" signs.
  READ for exact values of sign dimensions, letter heights, and material colors (red transparent over silver reflective background), and use VISION for the visual layout of the sign text.

* **Block 4: notes — Benchmarks**
  Provides surveyor reference points.
  READ for exact elevation values and locations of Benchmarks 1 and 2.

* **Blocks 6, 8: notes — Sheet Cross-References**
  Brief notes pointing the reviewer to other sheets for additional data.
  READ to locate the Tree List (Sheet 32) and the Fire Flow Test results (Sheet 18).

* **Blocks 5, 10, 12, 13: various — Boilerplate & Administrative**
  Contains the title block, legal description (Lot 1, Mecey Subdivision), Texas 811 callout, and standard disclaimers regarding existing utilities and engineer responsibility.
  READ for general project information and administrative records.
