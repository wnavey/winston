## Block 4: notes — The block contains a numbered list of Fire Zone Notes, specifying requirements for signage, painting, underground mains, and private hydrants.

Fire Zone Notes
1. Restricted Zone signage to be placed on front of lockers and along boat slip retaining walls as necessary or
required.
2. Stencil the words "FIRE ZONE/ TOW-AWAY ZONE" in white letters at least 4" high at 30 foot intervals along the
curb and the entire curb shall be painted red to serve as the background for the white letters.
3. Signs stating "FIRE ZONE/ TOW-AWAY ZONE" shall be posted at both ends of a fire zone and at each entry
and exit point which constitutes a portion of the fire zone or fire lane. Signs shall comply with Standard City of
Austin Detail 901S-6.
4. Underground mains feeding private hydrants must be installed and tested in accordance with NFPA-24, and the
Fire Code, by a licensed contractor with a plumbing permit. The entire main must be hydrostatically tested at
one time, unless isolation valves are provided between tested sections.
5. All private hydrants shall be painted red.

## Block 6: notes — The block contains a numbered list of general Notes regarding the installation and testing of underground mains for sprinkler systems and private hydrants, as well as the location of a Knox-box.

Notes
1. Underground mains feeding NFPAR 13 sprinkler systems must be installed and tested in accordance with
NFPAR 13, and the Fire Code, by a licensed sprinkler contractor with a plumbing permit. The entire main must
be hydrostatically tested at one time, unless isolation valves are provided between tested sections.
2. Underground mains feeding private hydrants must be installed and tested in accordance with NFPA 24, and
the Fire Code, by a licensed contractor with a plumbing permit. The entire main must be hydrostatically tested
at one time, unless isolation valves are provided between tested sections.
3. The Knox-box is to be located at exterior fire riser room door (non-hinged side).
