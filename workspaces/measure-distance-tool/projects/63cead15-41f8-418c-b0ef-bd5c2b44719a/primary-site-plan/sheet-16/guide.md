# Sheet 16 — Biofiltration-Detention Pond Profiles

Biofiltration pond profiles, outfall details, and comprehensive maintenance requirements.

## Reading Guide

This sheet provides cross-sectional profiles and outfall construction details for a biofiltration-detention pond at the Valley View Townhomes project. It also contains extensive textual specifications detailing the routine maintenance, major maintenance, and construction requirements for Stormwater Control Measures (SCMs).

**Key values**:
*   **Water Surface Elevations (WSEL)**: 100-yr (676.75'), 25-yr (676.57'), 10-yr (676.45'), 2-yr (676.25')
*   **Biofiltration Media Elevation**: 672.00'
*   **Retaining Wall Top Elevations**: 681.00', 677.25', and 677.50' (outfall)
*   **Outfall Weir**: 4.5' wide at elevation 675.65'
*   **Storm Pipe**: 5.00 linear feet of 24" HDPE laid at a 1.35% slope
*   **Rock Flow Spreader**: 6"-12" (H) x 3' (W) using 5-8" diameter rock
*   **Rock Rip-Rap**: 5-8" diameter rock
*   **Drawdown Time Limits (Maintenance)**: Maximum 96 hours for water quality volume; 24 hours for detention ponds
