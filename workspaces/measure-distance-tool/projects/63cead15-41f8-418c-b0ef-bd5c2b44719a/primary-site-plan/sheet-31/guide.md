# Sheet 31 — Landscape Plan

Landscape design plan featuring planting details and irrigation notes.

## Reading Guide

This sheet presents the Landscape Plan for the Valley View Townhomes project, detailing the layout of plantings, open spaces, hardscape features, and site contours. It serves as the primary reference for landscape grading, planting mitigation requirements, and automatic irrigation system specifications.

**Key values:**
*   **Drawing Scale:** 1" = 20'-0"
*   **Topsoil Placement Depth:** Finished elevation for parking lot islands and similar areas must be at least 6 inches below the finished parking lot/curb elevation.
*   **Irrigation Spray Limits:** No spray irrigation is permitted on areas less than 10 feet wide (e.g., medians, buffer strips).
*   **Irrigation Setbacks:** Above-ground irrigation emission devices must be set back at least 6 inches from impervious surfaces.
*   **Rain Shut-off Threshold:** Automatic rain shut-off devices must trigger after more than 1/2 inch (0.5") of rainfall.
*   **Tree Mitigation Size:** Street yard and parking lot trees exceeding 1.5 caliper inches count towards mitigation.
*   **Site Elevations:** Topographic contour lines indicate site elevations ranging generally from approximately 660 to 690 feet.
