## Block 2: notes — Notes regarding the installation of fences near trees and a disclaimer about the accuracy of existing utility locations shown on the plans.

**Fence Note**
Post holes that are to be installed near a trees Critical Root Zone (CRZ) will be dug by hand. No machinery is allowed in the CRZ and any work done in this area shall be done by hand. Add a 3-inch layer of hardwood mulch in the CRZ prior to commencing construction activities.

Release of this application does not constitute a verification of all data, information and calculations supplied by the applicant. The engineer of record is solely responsible for the completeness, accuracy and adequacy of his/her submittal, whether or not the application is reviewed for Code compliance by City engineers.

The location of all existing utilities shown on these plans has been based upon record information only and may not match locations as constructed. The contractor shall contact Texas 811 for assistance in determining existing utility locations prior to beginning construction. Contractor shall field verify locations of utility crossings prior to beginning construction.
