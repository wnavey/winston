# Sheet 38 — Wall Plan

Plan view, section details, and notes for retaining walls.

## Reading Guide

This sheet provides the structural details, plan view, and specifications for retaining walls, including a 14'-0" maximum height retaining wall section. Its primary purpose is to guide the construction, reinforcement, and inspection of the site's retaining walls, footings, and associated slab tie-ins.

**Key values**:
* Maximum retaining wall height: 14'-0"
* Concrete compressive strength: 3,000 PSI (minimum 28-day)
* Allowable soil bearing: 4,000 PSF
* Active soil pressure (heel): 45.5 PSF
* Passive pressure: 290 PSF
* Soil weight: 130 PSF
* Reinforcing steel grades: Grade 60 (for #4 rebar and larger), Grade 40 (for #3 rebar)
* Wall vertical reinforcement: #7 @ 10" O.C.
* Wall horizontal reinforcement: #3 @ 12" O.C. (each face)
* Minimum concrete clear cover: 3" for surfaces in contact with earth; 1 1/2" for walls/footings adjacent to exterior/interior surfaces; 1 1/2" above sub-grade for slabs
* Dowel embedment: 6" embedment for #6 rebar dowels into existing concrete

**Content blocks on this sheet**:

* **Block 2**: diagram — 14'-0" Max Retaining Wall cross-section showing rebar spacing, gravel backfill, weep pipes, and geotextile placement. READ for exact rebar sizing and material specifications, VISION for structural layout and dimensions.
* **Block 4**: drawing — Plan View showing the layout of the new and existing retaining walls, pond walls, and specific doweling instructions for an existing buttress. Use VISION to understand the spatial relationship, alignment, and construction phasing between new and existing walls.
* **Block 1**: diagram — Dowel Detail showing a cross-section for tying a new slab to an existing slab with #6 rebar. READ for drill bit sizing (7/8" for #4), epoxy installation instructions, and hand-installation notes.
* **Block 3**: diagram — Typical Step in Footing elevation view showing standard 2'-0" horizontal and 12" vertical step dimensions. Use VISION for step geometry and reinforcement continuity.
* **Block 10**: table — Design Criteria outlining IBC 2015 soil bearing and lateral pressure requirements. READ for the exact engineering design values.
* **Block 6**: notes — General and structural notes detailing concrete specifications, clear cover minimums, and reinforcement rules. READ for material grades, cover depths, and corner bar requirements (24"x24").
* **Block 9**: table — Required Inspections list. READ to confirm the three key inspection milestones (footings, walls, backfill/drainage).
* **Blocks 5, 7, 8**: various — Boilerplate & Administrative. Contains a general schematic disclaimer (Block 5), title block data for project 3759 Valley View (Block 7), and engineer seals for MLAW Engineers (Block 8).
