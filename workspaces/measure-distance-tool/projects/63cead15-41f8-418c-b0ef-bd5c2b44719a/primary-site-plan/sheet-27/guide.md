# Sheet 27 — Construction Details 6

Construction details for wastewater manholes and duplex grinder pumps.

## Reading Guide

This sheet provides construction specifications and standard details for the Valley View Townhomes project, focusing specifically on precast concrete wastewater manholes per Austin Water standards. It also features detailed plan and profile views of a duplex grinder pump station.

**Key values:**
*   **Manhole Inside Diameter:** 48" minimum to 72" maximum
*   **Manhole Cover Size:** 32" bolted cover
*   **Manhole Foundation Extension:** 6" thick layer (1" min. course aggregate) extending 12" min. all around the manhole base
*   **Manhole Invert/Base Slope:** 1:12 slope to drain to invert
*   **Manhole Lining:** 80 mil. lining of SPL WW-511 or 1/4 to 1/2 inch of SPL WW-511B product
*   **Chimney Adjustment Limit:** 24" maximum for adjustments before cone must be removed; non-shrink grout shall not exceed 1" in any dimension
*   **Internal Drop Requirement:** Elevation changes of >24" require an internal drop structure
*   **End Manhole Fall:** Minimum 2% fall
*   **Duplex Grinder Wet Well:** 60" diameter, 10' minimum depth
*   **Grinder Pump Base:** 12" thick concrete fill base
*   **Grinder Pump Piping:** 3" vent piping; 2" brass gate valves, isolation valves, and air release valves
