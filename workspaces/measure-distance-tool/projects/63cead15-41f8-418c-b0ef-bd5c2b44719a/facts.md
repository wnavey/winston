- **10' Wastewater Easement (Vol 73/76, C8S-75-160):** COA GIS shows this plat-dedicated easement running across the parcel. Not confirmed on the submitted site plan. Site plan notes "must verify no conflicts."
- **25' Drainage Easement and 7.5' PUE (Vol 64/13, C8-71-079):** GIS shows these easements (from the Cortez Heights plat) running across the NE boundary of Lot 1. Not verified on site plan. Precise location relative to proposed improvements must be confirmed.
- **FIRM Panel Number:** Site plan cites Panel #48453C0585H (effective September 26, 2008). FloodPro returns null for both FIRM_PANEL and EFFECTIVE_DATE. Must be independently verified on FEMA Flood Map Service Center.
- **Demolition permit number:** Site plan cites "2022-101266 BP" as the demolition permit. Amanda shows 2022-101266 as a Plumbing Permit (Repair); the actual demolition building permit is 2022-096813 BP (Final). Actual demolition is confirmed complete.

### Recorded Document Provisions

**Instrument 2010070731 — Greenbelt Access & Use Easement:**
- Entire 0.31-acre NE interior area is a strict no-build zone; no structures, fences, pavement, or permanent improvements permitted
- Easement is exclusive to the adjacent condo project beneficiaries and is freely assignable to the project's HOA
- Runs with the land; binds all successors and assigns of the grantor
- No time limit; perpetual duration
- Current property owner (WM 41) took title subject to this easement per the 2019 acquisition deed

**Instrument 2011021137 — City of Austin Drainage Easement:**
- City of Austin (Watershed Protection Department) holds unlimited, irrevocable right of access to the 0.130-acre NE corner area
- City may construct, reconstruct, operate, inspect, maintain, repair, replace, and upgrade a drainage channel within the easement
- Owner may not obstruct City access; any improvements within or adjacent to the easement require City coordination
- Perpetual; binds all successors and assigns
