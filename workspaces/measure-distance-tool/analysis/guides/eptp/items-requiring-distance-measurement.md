## Key Observations

1. **EPTP is dominated by non-distance checks.** At 87.2% not-applicable, the EPTP department is heavily oriented toward documentation, species verification, arborist credentials, labeling, and plan consistency rather than spatial measurements. This contrasts with infrastructure-oriented departments like Electrical (EL) where distance checks are more prevalent.

2. **CRZ-based items are the largest distance measurement cluster.** Guides 3, 4, 5, and 26 collectively contribute 66 horizontal items, nearly all involving Critical Root Zone radius calculations (radius in feet = trunk diameter in inches). These require measuring the distance from a tree trunk to proposed construction, then comparing against the CRZ threshold derived from the tree's diameter.

3. **Guide 8 (Street Tree/Street Yard) has the highest distance-only density.** With 10 distance-only items out of 39 total (25.6%), this guide is the strongest candidate for tool automation. Most items involve straightforward distance thresholds (50-foot tree proximity, 8-foot island width, 25-foot parking lot median proximity).

4. **Guide 4 has the most total horizontal items (30) but most are distance-plus.** The Construction Activities Near Trees guide requires identifying whether staging areas, spoils, dumpsters, construction access, and other temporary facilities fall within CRZ zones. The distance measurement is needed but cannot alone resolve the verdict because the activity type and compliance method must also be verified.

5. **The distance-plus pattern is distinctive to EPTP.** Unlike EL where distance-only items are common, EPTP's distance-plus items typically require: (a) computing a CRZ radius from a tree's DBH, (b) measuring the distance from tree to feature, and (c) verifying additional information like species, condition, or compliance method. This suggests the measure-distance tool should be paired with tree attribute lookup capabilities.

## Methodology

- All 26 EPTP guide files were read from `/Users/winston/workspace/bureau/jurisdictions/austin/review-guides/eptp/`
- Each checklist item (rows matching `| EPTP-N.N | deficiency text |` in markdown tables) was extracted and classified
- Items in guide 9 use simple numeric IDs (1-76 in the source); these were mapped to `EPTP-9.N` for consistency
- Classification criteria:
  - **horizontal**: The deficiency involves measuring a plan-view distance, separation, setback, clearance, or spatial relationship between features on a site plan
  - **vertical-or-mixed**: The deficiency requires measuring depth below grade, elevation difference, vertical clearance, or profile/section view information
  - **not-applicable**: The deficiency is about documentation completeness, labeling, species identification, credentials, calculations, notes, or other non-spatial checks
- Sub-classification for horizontal items:
  - **distance-only**: A single distance measurement compared to a numeric threshold resolves the verdict
  - **distance-plus**: Distance measurement is necessary but insufficient; additional information must be verified (species, material, method, approval, etc.)
- Thresholds were extracted from deficiency text where explicit numeric values were stated
- The JSON schema matches the EL department classification at `/Users/winston/workspace/winston/workspaces/measure-distance-tool/analysis/guides/el/item-classification.json`
