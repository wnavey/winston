# SDE Items Requiring Distance Measurement

Analysis of 2438 checklist items across 40 SDE (Site Development and Environmental) review guides.

## Summary

| Category | Count | Percentage |
|----------|-------|------------|
| **Horizontal (plan-view distance)** | **99** | **4.1%** |
| - Distance-only | 89 | 3.7% |
| - Distance-plus | 10 | 0.4% |
| Vertical or mixed | 521 | 21.4% |
| Not applicable | 1818 | 74.6% |
| **Total** | **2438** | **100%** |

## Key Findings

- **99 items (4.1%)** require plan-view distance measurement that the ruler tool can directly measure.
- **89 items** are pure distance checks where measurement alone resolves the verdict.
- **10 items** need distance measurement plus additional verification (materials, approvals, conditions).
- **521 items (21.4%)** involve vertical/3D measurements (slopes, elevations, depths, profiles) -- the ruler tool cannot fully resolve these yet.
- **1818 items (74.6%)** are non-distance checks (documentation, calculations, specifications, labeling, administrative compliance).
- SDE is heavily documentation-oriented: nearly 3/4 of items are about report content, plan labeling, calculations, and administrative requirements.
- The vertical/mixed category is significant at 21.4% due to SDE's focus on grading, slopes, pond elevations, cross-sections, and hydraulic profiles.
