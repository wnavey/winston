# Sheet 15 — Biofiltration-Detention Pond Plan & Calculations

Biofiltration and detention pond plan, details, and engineering calculations.

## Reading Guide

This sheet provides the detailed site plan, schematic cross-sections, and comprehensive engineering calculations for a partial biofiltration and onsite detention pond associated with Unit 501. Its primary purpose is to outline the spatial layout, stage-storage volumes, flow rates, and material specifications required to ensure proper stormwater management and water quality control for the development.

**Key values:**
*   Drainage Area to Control: 1.48 ac (86.09% Impervious Cover, 1.27 ac)
*   Water Quality Volume (WQV): 6,237 cf required, 6,388 cf provided
*   Water Quality Elevation Provided: 675.65 ft msl
*   Top of Biofiltration Media Elevation: 672.00 ft
*   Maximum Ponding Depth above Sand Bed: 3.65 ft
*   Filtration Pond Area Provided: 831 sf
*   Peak Flow Rates (Q100): 18.83 cfs (Entrance), 15.60 cfs (Outfall)
*   D50 Rock Rip-Rap: 0.25 in (Entrance), 0.39 in (Outfall)
*   Required Orifice Diameter: 1.39 in (Area: 0.011 sq. ft.)
