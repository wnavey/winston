conducted by a UST Contractor that is registered with the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ).
Contact UST@austintexas.gov if you have any questions.[COA Title 6]

## Block 33: text_block — This block is a note requiring all potable water system components installed after January 4, 2014, to be "lead free" according to the US Safe Drinking Water Act. It lists examples of exempt and non-exempt components.

All potable water system components installed after January 4, 2014, shall be essentially "lead free" according to the
US Safe Drinking Water Act. Examples are valves (corporation stop, curb stop, and pressure reducing), nipples,
bushings, pipe, fittings and backflow preventers. Fire hydrants, tapping saddles and 2 inch and larger gate valves are
the only components exempt from this requirement. Components that are not clearly identified by the manufacturer as
meeting this requirement either by markings on the component or on the packaging shall not be installed.
