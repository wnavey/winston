# Sheet 6 — Erosion Control Notes

Contains erosion control, tree protection, and special construction notes.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains comprehensive text notes, tables, and specifications for erosion control, tree protection, and vegetative stabilization for the Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to provide the contractor with regulatory guidelines, material application rates, and procedural requirements for managing environmental impacts and protecting existing natural features during site construction.

**Key values:**
*   Temporary Seeding (Sept 15 - Mar 1): Western Wheatgrass at 5.6 lbs/acre, Oats at 4.0 lbs/acre, Cereal Rye Grain at 45 lbs/acre
*   Permanent/Warm Season Seeding (Mar 2 - Sept 14): Hulled Bermuda at 45 lbs/acre (95% purity, 0.83 PLS minimum)
*   Temporary Hydromulching Rate: 1,500 to 2,000 lbs/acre (0-3 months longevity)
*   Permanent Hydromulching Rates: 2,500 to 4,000 lbs/acre for Bonded Fiber Matrix (BFM); 3,000 to 4,500 lbs/acre for Fiber Reinforced Matrix (FRM)
*   Tree Protection Fencing: Chain-link mesh with a minimum height of 5 feet
*   Root Zone Mulch Depth: Minimum 8 inches, maximum 12 inches
*   Topsoil Requirement for Revegetation: Minimum 6 inches; requires loosening subgrade to a depth of at least 2 inches prior to placement
*   Silt Removal Threshold: When accumulation reaches 6 inches or 1/3 of the installed height of the control
*   Rainfall Inspection Threshold: After 0.5 inches or greater of rainfall
*   Spoils Notification: Must notify Development Services Dept 48 hours prior to disposing of excavated material

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

*   **Block 8**: General Erosion Control Notes. READ for the main compliance requirements, BMP sequencing, inspector certifications, void mitigation, and temporary vegetative stabilization seeding rates/dates.
*   **Block 1**: Permanent Vegetative Stabilization notes. READ for seeding schedules, fertilizer requirements, and watering compliance necessary for final site stabilization.
*   **Block 6**: Table 2: Hydromulching for Permanent Vegetative Stabilization. READ for exact material compositions, longevity expectations, and application rates for BFM and FRM.
*   **Block 9**: Table 1: Hydromulching for Temporary Vegetative Stabilization. READ for the material specs and application rates for short-term (0-3 months) slopes.
*   **Block 2**: Tree Protection Notes (ECM 3.6.2). READ for specific requirements regarding pre-construction fencing, root zone mulching, and construction-phase limitations around preserved trees.
*   **Block 4**: Remedial Tree Care Notes. READ for soil aeration, fertilization, and post-construction compaction mitigation instructions directed by the City Arborist.
*   **Block 5**: Special Construction Techniques. READ for procedures to minimize root damage during grading and excavation, including using rock saws and plastic vapor barriers near tree roots.
*   **Block 10**: Unnumbered site restoration notes. READ for topsoil depths, decompaction requirements (per SSM 661S), and elevation offsets for parking lot islands/medians.
*   **Block 3**: Environmental Inspector Note. READ for a brief note authorizing the inspector to mandate additional controls.
*   **Block 7**: Spoils Control Note. READ for daily cleanup requirements on impervious covers.
*   **Block 11**: Dust Control Note. READ for directives on using irrigation trucks and mulching to control site dust.
*   **Block 15**: Developer Information. READ for the owner (WM 41 Austin RE LLC), representative (Bleyl Engineering), and contact phone numbers.
*   **Blocks 12, 13, 14, 16, 17**: Various — Boilerplate & Administrative. READ Block 16 for project title, sheet number (6 of 39), and engineering firm details. Use VISION to verify the PE seal (Block 14) and review standard disclaimers regarding utility locations and engineer liability (Blocks 12, 17). Block 13 is an empty layout element.
