# Sheet 21 — Preliminary Electrical Design Plan

Layout of preliminary electrical design, routing, transformers, and meters.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains the Preliminary Electrical Design Plan for the Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to detail the proposed electrical infrastructure, mapping the layout of underground electric lines, electrical meters, transformers, and pull boxes across the eight proposed buildings on the site.

**Key values:**
*   Scale: 1" = 20'
*   Benchmark #1 Elevation: 678.52' NAVD 88
*   Benchmark #2 Elevation: 681.08' NAVD 88
*   Bldg. 1: 3,290 sf, FFE = 687.50
*   Bldg. 2: 4,830 sf, FFE = 685.50
*   Bldg. 3: 4,144 sf, FFE = 685.50
*   Bldg. 4: 4,144 sf, FFE = 685.50
*   Bldg. 5: 3,290 sf, FFE = 683.0
*   Bldg. 6: 3,290 sf, FFE = 683.0
*   Bldg. 7: 4,144 sf, FFE = 685.0
*   Bldg. 8: 4,997 sf, FFE = 685.50
*   Private Domestic Water Branches: 2" HDPE
*   Private Wastewater Branches: 6" PVC
*   Geologist inspection trench depth threshold: > 5 feet

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

*   **Block 3: drawing — Main Electrical Site Plan**
    The central site plan detailing the layout of Buildings 1 through 8, including their individual units, square footages, and Finished Floor Elevations (FFEs). The drawing illustrates the routing of underground electric lines, and the specific placement of electrical transformer pads, pull boxes, and meters. Use VISION to examine the spatial layout, routing of electrical lines, and proximity of utility boxes to the building units; READ for specific unit numbers and adjacent easement dimensions.

*   **Block 4: notes — Benchmarks**
    Details for two survey control points (mag nails set in asphalt) used for elevation reference. READ the block content for exact benchmark descriptions and elevations (678.52' and 681.08').

*   **Block 6: notes — Utility Notes**
    Provides contractor directives regarding the verification of existing utilities and specific material sizing for private domestic water (2" HDPE) and wastewater (6" PVC) branches. READ the block content for these specifications and liability disclaimers.

*   **Block 7: notes — Tree List & Geologist Mitigation Rule**
    Contains a cross-reference to landscape sheet 32 for the tree list, and a critical construction constraint regarding the Void and Water Flow Mitigation Rule. READ for the specific requirement that all trenching greater than 5 feet deep must be inspected by a geologist.

*   **Block 5: notes — Cross Reference**
    A small note directing the reader to another sheet for profile information. READ to note the reference to Sheet 19.

*   **Block 11: text_block — Legal Description**
    Identifies the legal plat details of the subject property. READ for the subdivision name and recording details (Lot 1, Mecey Subdivision Volume 73, Page 76).

*   **Block 12: text_block — Title Block**
    Specifies the sheet name and project location. READ to confirm the sheet is the Preliminary Electrical Design Plan for Valley View Townhomes.

*   **Block 2: diagram — Scale and North Arrow**
    Provides the graphic scale and orientation for the main drawing. VISION to understand the page orientation (North arrow pointing up-right) and confirm the 1" = 20' scale.

*   **Blocks 1, 8, 9, 10: various — Boilerplate & Administrative**
    Contains standard project tracking and professional compliance elements, including the Texas 811 "Call Before You Dig" logo (Block 1), Bleyl Engineering firm contact info (Block 8), Jason K. Rodgers' professional engineer seal (Block 9), and sheet tracking information (Block 10). READ Block 10 for the specific sheet number (21 of 39).
