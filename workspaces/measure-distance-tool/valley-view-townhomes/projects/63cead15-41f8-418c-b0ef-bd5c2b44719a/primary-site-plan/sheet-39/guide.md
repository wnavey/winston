# Sheet 39 — Wall Plan Details And Notes

Details and cross-sections for multiple pond retaining walls.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains structural cross-sections and reinforcement details for pond retaining walls of varying maximum heights. Its primary purpose is to provide the exact dimensional, material, and reinforcement specifications required for constructing these walls, alongside soil bearing data and a post-tensioning stressing chart.

**Key values:**
* Max Pond Wall Heights detailed: 4'-0", 6'-0", 8'-0", and 13'-0"
* Soil Bearing Pressure: 2000 PSF
* Geotextile specification: Mirafi 140N Geotextil (or approved equal)
* Topsoil depth requirement: 12"
* Drainage specification (13' wall): 3" PVC pipe to daylight @ .5% slope
* 13'-0" Wall Footing width: 8'-0" total (2'-9" heel, 1'-0" wall, 4'-3" toe)
* 13'-0" Wall Vertical Reinforcement: #7 @ 10" O.C. Vert. X (minimum 36" lap for dowels)
* 4'-0" Wall Footing width: 3'-1" total (1'-2 1/2" heel, 8" wall, 1'-2 1/2" toe)
* Wall Opening Reinforcement: (4) #5 x 4'-0" bars each face at opening

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

* **Block 5:** diagram — Cross-section detail of a 13'-0" Max Pond Wall. Use VISION for the spatial layout of the 8'-0" footing and drainage pipe location, and READ for heavy reinforcement specifications like the #7 @ 10" O.C. vertical bars and chamfer notes.
* **Block 2:** diagram — Cross-section detail of an 8'-0" Max Pond Wall. Use VISION for footing dimensions and structural geometry, and READ for reinforcement schedules (#3 and #4 bars) and gravel backfill placement.
* **Block 4:** diagram — Cross-section detail of a 6'-0" Max Pond Wall. Use VISION for spatial layout of the 10" thick wall and footing, and READ for exact rebar spacing and soil layer notes.
* **Block 1:** diagram — Cross-section detail of a 4'-0" Max Pond Wall. Use VISION to examine the basic footing layout, and READ for specific reinforcement sizes (#4 vertical, #3 horizontal) and required dimensions.
* **Block 3:** diagram — Elevation view of a Typical Opening in Wall Detail. Use VISION to see the diagonal placement of reinforcement around the diamond opening, and READ for the exact bar specification (ADD (4) #5 X 4'-0").
* **Block 8:** table — Soil Data Parameters. READ the block content for foundational constraints, specifically the 2000 PSF bearing pressure and source lab information.
* **Block 9:** table — Post-Tensioning Stressing Chart. READ for the scheduled tabular data mapping cable lengths (15' to 148') to their corresponding elongation requirements (1" to 11 1/2").
* **Blocks 6, 7, 10, 11, 12:** various — Boilerplate & Administrative. READ for project identification (3759 Valley View / Pecan Valley Homes), Sheet name (Wall Plan Details and Notes, 39 of 39), MLAW Engineers contact info, and the PE seal/signature.
