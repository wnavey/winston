# Sheet 7 — Erosion Control Details

Standard details and specifications for erosion and sediment control.

## Reading Guide

This sheet provides standard erosion control and site management detail drawings for the Valley View Townhomes project. It includes specifications, dimensions, and installation instructions for various sediment control measures such as silt fences, filter dikes, construction entrances, and concrete washouts to ensure compliance with environmental and watershed protection regulations.

**Key values**:
*   **Silt Fence J-Hook Spacing**: 100 ft maximum distance.
*   **Tree Protection Fence**: 1.5 m (5'-0") high chain link; Critical Root Zone radius = 12 mm per mm (1.5' per 20") of trunk diameter.
*   **Stabilized Construction Entrance**: 15 m (50') minimum length; 200 mm (8") minimum thickness; uses 75-125 mm (3-5") open graded rock.
*   **Concrete Truck Washout**: Minimum 10' wide; lined with 10 mil plastic sheeting; berms must be at least 18" high and 12" wide; maximum capacity 50%.
*   **Area Inlet Filter Fabric**: Fabric weight > 3.0 oz/sq yd; Burst strength > 120 psi; Water flow rate > 275 gal/min/sq ft. Extend fabric 12" minimum beyond inlet opening.
*   **Post Embedment (Silt Fence & Mulch Sock)**: Minimum 300 mm (12") depth.
*   **Silt Fence Trench**: 150 mm (6") deep and 150 mm (6") wide.
*   **Triangular Sediment Filter Dike**: W4x4-150 mm x 150 mm (6"x6" 6 GA) wire mesh; 150 mm (6") minimum height; anchored every 300 mm (12").
*   **Maintenance Threshold**: Accumulated silt must be removed from dikes, socks, and fences when it reaches a depth of 150 mm (6") or 2" for inlet protections.
