# Sheet 37 — Block 1

## diagram: Detailed drawing showing the requirements for planting a tree in the City of Austin. Includes instructions for pruning, stakes, mulch, and soil preparation.

PRUNE ONLY AS DIRECTED BY LANDSCAPE ARCHITECT AFTER VISUAL INSPECTION.
REMOVE DEAD OR BROKEN BRANCHES ONLY
SELECT TREES WITH A SINGLE STRONG CENTRAL LEADER, DO NOT PRUNE
REMOVE NURSERY APPLIED TREE WRAP, TAPE, OR STRING FROM TREE TRUNK AND CROWN. REMOVE ANY TAGS OR LABELS.
ARBORBRACE WEBBING, ALLOW SMALL TRUNK MOVEMENT, MAX 2" ANY DIRECTION.
TRUNK TO BE STABLE, NOT RIGID.
STAKESAFE TREE STAKE PROTECTOR, ON ALL STAKES
8' LENGTH BLACK PAINTED STEEL T-POST, DRIVE INTO NATURAL GRADE MIN. 18" BELOW TREE PIT
REMOVE SUCKERS
3" DEPTH ORGANIC WOOD MULCH, KEEP MULCH 6" FROM ROOT CROWN. FORM A NEAT CIRCULAR RING AT EDGE OF TREE PIT.
SCARIFY EDGE OF TREE ROOTBALL AND TREE PIT TO ALLOW ROOT GROWTH.
FINISHED GRADE
UNDISTURBED EARTH
TREE PIT, 3X WIDTH OF ROOTBALL

IDENTIFY TRUNK FLARE AND SET ROOT BALL LEVEL TO GRADE OR SLIGHTLY ABOBE GRASE (1/2")
CENTER ROOTBALL IN PLANTING SOIL. LEAVE BOTTOM OF PLANTING HOLE FIRM. DO NOT AMEND SOIL UNLESS PLANTING IN POOR OR SEVERELY DISTURBED SOIL OR BUILDING RUBBLE. USE WATER TO SETTLE SOIL AND REMOVE AIR POCKETS, AND FIRMLY SET TREE. GENTLY TAMP IF NEEDED.
REMOVE NURSERY MATERIAL SURROUNDING ROOTBALL.
PRUNE GIRDLING ROOTS.
