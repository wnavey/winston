# Sheet 34 — Root Zone Impact Exhibit

Critical root zone impact diagrams, calculations, and tree photos.

## Reading Guide

This sheet is a Root Zone Impact Exhibit detailing the critical root zone (CRZ) impacts and preservation strategies for two specific existing trees (Tree 4034 and Tree 4038H). It provides plan diagrams, site photographs, and exact square footage calculations to demonstrate compliance with tree preservation requirements amid proposed construction of buildings, retaining walls, sidewalks, and driveways.

**Key values**:
*   **Scale**: 1" = 10'-0" for both detail plans
*   **Tree 4034 CRZ Calculations**:
    *   Existing Undisturbed CRZ: 849.8 sqft
    *   Existing CRZ Removed: 444.34 sqft
    *   Undisturbed CRZ to Remain: 405.46 sqft (48% of original)
    *   Existing Unimpacted 1/2 CRZ Removed: 27.95 sqft (11%)
*   **Tree 4038H CRZ Calculations**:
    *   Existing Undisturbed CRZ: 565.9 sqft
    *   Existing CRZ Removed: 209.26 sqft
    *   Undisturbed CRZ to Remain: 356.64 sqft (63% of original)
    *   Impervious Surface Removed from CRZ Area: 673.2 sqft
    *   Pervious CRZ Available After Construction: 1035.4 sqft (180% of original)
    *   Existing Unimpacted 1/2 CRZ Removed: 0 sqft (0%)
*   **Clearances/Overbuilds**: 1' overbuild noted for wall construction (Tree 4034) and curb construction (Tree 4038H)
*   **Tree 4039**: Noted to be preserved and "mitigated in place"

**Content blocks on this sheet**:

*   **Blocks 4, 5**: Legends and Root Zone Calculations. READ for the specific breakdown of square footages showing CRZ undisturbed, removed, remaining, and pervious area added/available for trees 4034 and 4038H.
*   **Block 1**: Diagram and photograph — Critical Root Zone Impact Tree 4034. Use VISION to examine the spatial relationship and impact zones between the existing tree and the proposed building, transformer, retaining wall, and 1' wall construction overbuild. 
*   **Block 2**: Diagram and photograph — Critical Root Zone Impact Tree 4038H. Use VISION to examine the layout showing existing pavement to be removed, the proposed driveway, building, and the mitigation note for adjacent Tree 4039.
*   **Blocks 14, 15, 17**: Detail titles and scales. READ to confirm the specific tree identifier for each detail section and the 1" = 10'-0" scale.
