## Block 1: diagram — Details for a storm drain manhole ring and 813 mm (32") cover, including plan and section views with dimensions.

STORM DRAIN MANHOLE
RING AND 813 mm (32") COVER

[Plan view of manhole ring and cover with dimensions and design details.]
[Section view of manhole ring with dimensions and notes.]

NOTES:
1. ALL CORNERS AND EDGES SHALL HAVE A 1.5 mm (1/16") MINIMUM RADIUS.
2. LIDS SHALL BE CAST WITH TWO 25 mm (1") DIA. STEEL PICK BARS.
3. LID WEIGHTS SHALL BE 325 N (73 LBS.) FOR CAST IRON OR 778 N (175 LBS.) FOR DUCTILE IRON. WEIGHT SHALL BE CAST ON BOTH TOP AND BOTTOM OF LID.
4. MANUFACTURER SHALL PROVIDE INDEPENDENT TESTING LABORATORY REPORT ON 20,000 POUND PROOF LOAD TEST CONDUCTED ACCORDING TO AASHTO M-306.
5. FILLETS SHALL BE 6 mm (1/4") RADIUS UNLESS OTHERWISE SPECIFIED.
6. MANUFACTURER SHALL REMOVE EXCESS IRON AND MACHINE FINISH BEARING SURFACES TO MATCH TOLERANCES.
