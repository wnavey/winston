# Sheet 36 — Biofiltration Pond Plans & Calculations

Detailed planting plans and calculations for the biofiltration pond.

## Reading Guide

This sheet provides the biofiltration pond plans and calculations for the Valley View Townhomes project. It includes a detailed layout of the sedimentation and filtration basins, alongside extensive planting schedules and plant equivalency calculations to ensure biofiltration requirements are met.

**Key values**:
*   Scale: 1" = 10'-0"
*   Sedimentation Basin Area: 769 SQ. FT.
*   Sedimentation Required Plants: 77 (provides 79 1-Gallon equivalents)
*   Filtration Basin Area: 830 SQ. FT.
*   Filtration Required Plants: 166 (provides 122 1-Gallon equivalents)
*   Plant Container Sizes specified: 5 Gallon (Shrubs), 1 Gallon (Bunchgrasses), 4" Flat (Groundcovers)
*   Maximum Spacing requirements range from 2' O.C. MAX to 12' O.C. MAX depending on basin and plant species.
