# Sheet 13 — Drainage Area Map

Existing and proposed drainage area maps with summary tables.

## Reading Guide

This sheet presents both the existing and proposed drainage conditions for the Valley View Townhomes development. It features side-by-side drainage area maps along with comprehensive hydrologic calculation tables detailing area runoff, time of concentration, curve numbers, and a direct comparison of pre- and post-development peak discharge rates to evaluate stormwater impacts.

**Key values**:
*   **Major Proposed Drainage Area (Pro 1.1)**: 1.48 acres (83.78% impervious cover), generating 18.83 cfs during a 100-year storm event.
*   **Major Existing Drainage Area (Ex 1.1)**: 1.27 acres (94.49% impervious cover), generating 16.44 cfs during a 100-year storm event.
*   **Peak Discharge Comparison (100-yr storm)**:
    *   POA 1: Decreased from 21.46 cfs (Existing) to 21.14 cfs (Proposed)
    *   POA 2: Decreased from 3.17 cfs (Existing) to 0.00 cfs (Proposed)
    *   POA 3: Decreased from 2.31 cfs (Existing) to 0.77 cfs (Proposed)
*   **Curve Numbers (CN)**: 80 used for Open Space (Soil D, Good Condition); 98 used for Impervious Areas.
*   **Minimum Time of Concentration (Tc)**: 5.00 minutes applied to the developed proposed area (Pro 1.1).

**Content blocks on this sheet**:

*   **Block 1: drawing — Proposed Drainage Area Map**
    *   Displays the post-development site condition.
    *   Use VISION to examine the spatial layout of proposed Buildings 1-8, new paved areas, contour changes, and the delineated boundaries of proposed sub-basins (Pro 1.1, Pro 1.2, Pro 2, Pro OS) along with flow direction arrows.

*   **Block 2: drawing — Existing Drainage Area Map**
    *   Displays the pre-development site condition.
    *   Use VISION to review the existing topography, site boundaries, established easements, retaining walls, and the current drainage sub-basins (Ex 1.1, 1.2, 2, 3, OS).

*   **Block 7: table — Discharge Summary**
    *   The critical comparison table summarizing the overall drainage impact of the project.
    *   READ to verify that proposed peak flows (cfs) at the defined Points of Analysis (POA 1, 2, 3) do not exceed existing flows across the 2-yr, 10-yr, 25-yr, and 100-yr storm events.

*   **Blocks 4 & 6: tables — Proposed and Existing Drainage Summaries**
    *   Sub-basin level hydrologic summaries.
    *   READ for specific metrics on each individual drainage area, including total acres, precise impervious/pervious cover percentages, CN, Tc, and storm water flows (Atlas 14).

*   **Block 5: table — Time of Concentration**
    *   Detailed path calculations for runoff speed.
    *   READ to verify the hydrologic routing parameters, including overland sheet flow lengths/slopes and shallow concentrated flow velocities used to determine the Tc for each existing and proposed basin. 

*   **Block 8: table — CN Calculations**
    *   Curve number derivations for all areas.
    *   READ to see the exact acreage breakdowns of open space versus impervious cover that justify the weighted average Curve Number applied to each drainage area.

*   **Block 3: seal — Engineer's Seal**
    *   Boilerplate approval stamp for Jason K. Rodgers, PE.
