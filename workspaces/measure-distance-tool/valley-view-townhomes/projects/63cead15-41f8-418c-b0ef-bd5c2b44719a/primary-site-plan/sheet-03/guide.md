# Sheet 3 — General Notes

Comprehensive general notes, sequence of construction, and project legend.

## Reading Guide

This sheet provides a comprehensive collection of general notes, standard specifications, utility capacity approvals, and the site's legend for the Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to establish project-wide regulatory constraints, outline the construction sequence, and document the City of Austin's confirmation of sufficient water and wastewater service.

**Key values:**
*   Project Scope: 39 Condo Units, 28 Living Unit Equivalents (LUE), 3.59 Acres
*   Water Demand: 60 gpm (Estimated Peak Hour), requiring connection to existing 8-inch main
*   Fire Flow Requirement: 1,500 gpm
*   Wastewater Flow: 20 gpm (Estimated Peak Wet Weather), requiring connection to existing 8-inch gravity main
*   Benchmark 1 (Mag Nail in Asphalt): Elevation = 678.52' NAVD 88
*   Benchmark 2 (Mag Nail in Asphalt): Elevation = 681.08' NAVD 88
*   Fire Department Paving Requirement: 80,000 lb. live-vehicle load capability
*   Fire Apparatus Vertical Clearance: 14 feet
*   Fire Hydrant Placement: Center of 4-inch opening must be at least 18 inches above finished grade
*   Electric Transmission Clearances: No digging/grading/trees within 25 feet of transmission structures; 20-foot clearance for aerial equipment/staging
*   Transmission Easement Road Requirement: Must support a 46,000 lb. tandem axle
*   ROW Work Zone Peak Hours: 6:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.
*   ROW Violation Fee: $250 per specific violation type

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

*   **Blocks 12 & 15:** Austin Water "Suitable and Sufficient Existing Service" approval letters for water and wastewater. READ the block content for the approved project specifications, demand calculations, and the specific existing 8-inch mains designated for the tap.
*   **Block 17:** Benchmark data. READ for exact elevations and locations to be used as control points for the site.
*   **Block 19:** Sequence of Construction. READ the block content for the strict 13-step chronological order of operations, particularly regarding when erosion controls and pond grading must occur.
*   **Block 6:** Project Legend. READ for the text descriptions of utilities, and use VISION to familiarize yourself with the symbol shapes (e.g., property lines, utility lines, tree protection zones) to interpret subsequent plan sheets accurately.
*   **Block 9:** Fire Department requirements. READ for strict design constraints regarding emergency access, pavement load ratings, and fire hydrant placement.
*   **Block 16:** Electric Transmission Notes. READ for critical safety clearances, structural load requirements for roads in easements, and barricade rules required by Austin Energy.
*   **Block 7:** Right-of-Way Management Standard Notes. READ for traffic control rules, restricted working hours, and permit violation penalties.
*   **Block 8:** General Construction Notes and Developer Information. READ to identify the property owner (WM 41 Austin RE LLC) and general engineering responsibilities.
*   **Blocks 14 & 18:** Electric Notes and Austin Energy standard requirements. READ for tree pruning, easement access, and code compliance constraints.
*   **Block 11:** Site Plan Release Notes. READ for general City of Austin demolition and utility relocation policies. 
*   **Block 20:** Legal Description. READ to verify the exact lot and subdivision references (Lot 1, Mecey Subdivision).
*   **Blocks 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 13:** Various administrative and boilerplate content (Title block info, Engineer's seal, Texas 811 logo, ADA disclaimers). READ the block content briefly for project tracking metadata, otherwise these can be safely skipped.
