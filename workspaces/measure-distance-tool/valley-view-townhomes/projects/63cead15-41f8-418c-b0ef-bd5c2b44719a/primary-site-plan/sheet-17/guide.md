# Sheet 17 — Conveyance Cross Sections

Conveyance cross-section graphs and corresponding site plan view.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains the conveyance cross-sections and a corresponding site plan snippet for the Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to model and display the hydraulic performance (water surface elevations, velocities, and channel depths) of the site's drainage conveyance system during 25-year and 100-year storm events at three distinct cross-section locations (XS 1, XS 2, and XS 3).

**Key values:**
*   **Manning's Roughness Coefficient (n):** 0.03 used for all cross-sections
*   **Design Flow Rates (Q):** 16.49 cfs (25-Year event), 21.97 cfs (100-Year event) across all sections
*   **Maximum 100-Year Velocity:** 8.70 ft/s (at cross-section XS 3)
*   **Maximum 100-Year Depth:** 0.48 ft (at cross-section XS 3)
*   **Benchmark 1:** Mag Nail in Asphalt, Elevation = 678.52' NAVD 88
*   **Benchmark 2:** Mag Nail in Asphalt, Elevation = 681.08' NAVD 88
*   **Finished Floor Elevations (FFE):** Bldg. 5 = 683.0 ft, Bldg. 6 = 683.0 ft

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

*   **Block 7: site plan — Multi-family development plan view**
    Use VISION to examine the spatial layout, grading contours, drainage easements, and the specific locations of the cross-section cut lines (XS 1, XS 2, XS 3) relative to buildings 5 and 6 and existing retaining walls.
*   **Blocks 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6: diagrams — Cross-section profile graphs**
    Use VISION to examine the channel profiles, side slopes, and plotted water surface elevations for cross-sections XS 1, XS 2, and XS 3 under both 25-year and 100-year storm conditions. 
*   **Blocks 14, 15, 16, 17, 19, 20: tables — Hydraulic data tables**
    READ for exact hydraulic parameters corresponding to each cross-section graph, including Depth (ft), Flow Rate Q (cfs), Area (sqft), Velocity (ft/s), Wetted Perimeter Wp (ft), Critical Depth Yc (ft), Top Width (ft), and Energy Head (ft).

    READ to verify the Manning's roughness coefficient used in the calculations.
