## Block 20: text_block — A general note stating that the project is subject to the Void and Water Flow Mitigation Rule and requires inspection by a geologist for trenches deeper than 5 feet.

### Note
This project is subject to the Void and Water Flow Mitigation Rule (COA ECM 1.12.0 and COA Item No. 658S of the SSM) provision that all trenching greater than 5 feet deep must be inspected by a geologist (Texas P.G.) or a geologist's representative.
