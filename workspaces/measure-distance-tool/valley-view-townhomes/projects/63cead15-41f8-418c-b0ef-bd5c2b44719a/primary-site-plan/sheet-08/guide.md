# Sheet 8 — Tree Removal & Demolition Plan

Site plan detailing existing structure demolition and tree removal.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains the Tree Removal and Demolition Plan for the Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to outline the existing site conditions, specify which structures, pavements, and utilities are to be demolished or retained, and establish strict guidelines for protecting preserved trees during demolition activities.

**Key values:**
*   **Benchmark 1 Elevation:** 678.52' NAVD 88 (GEOID 18)
*   **Benchmark 2 Elevation:** 681.08' NAVD 88 (GEOID 18)
*   **Tree Protection Mulch:** 8" layer of organic hardwood mulch outside of fencing
*   **Tree Trunk Armor:** 2x4 or thicker lumber to match height of building
*   **Max Pruning Permitted:** 25% (must be performed by a qualified arborist)
*   **Water Utilities Modifications:** Add 6" gate valve, remove existing 6" DI water line, remove existing fire hydrant #172943
*   **Specific Demolition Callout:** Demo 79 Lf of concrete retaining wall
*   **Project Location:** 3759 Valley View Road, Austin, Texas 78704 (Lot 1, Mecey Subdivision Vol. 73, Pg. 76)
*   **Sheet Number:** 8 of 39

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

*   **Block 2: Main Site Plan Drawing**
    *   Detailed site plan showing structures to be demolished (asphalt, concrete sidewalks, curbs, retaining walls) and existing utilities.
    *   Use VISION to locate spatial relationships, specific areas slated for demolition, trees designated to remain or be removed, and the 79 LF concrete retaining wall removal.

*   **Block 1: Water Intersection Detail Drawing**
    *   A detail drawing showing a water intersection with existing and proposed valves, lines, and a fire hydrant.
    *   READ for exact sizes (e.g., 6" DI Water Line, 6" gate valve) and VISION to understand the layout of the utility tie-ins.

*   **Block 8: Site Demolition Notes**
    *   A list of 10 notes detailing procedures for utility abandonment, material removal, and tree protection.
    *   READ for critical demolition instructions, especially the requirement to use hand tools within the critical root zones of preserved trees.

*   **Block 4: Tree Protection Notes**
    *   Detailed specifications for protecting trees, including fencing, trunk armor, and pruning rules.
    *   READ for exact material requirements (2x4 lumber, 8" mulch) and the reference to ECM 3.6.1.

*   **Block 7 & 10: Tree Legend and Tree List Reference**
    *   Legend explaining tree symbols (Remain, Heritage, Protected, Removed) and a note directing to the full tree list.
    *   READ to interpret the tree symbols used in Block 2, and note that the complete tree list is located on Sheet 32.

*   **Block 5: Benchmarks**
    *   Descriptions and elevations for two site benchmarks.
    *   READ for precise elevation values (678.52' and 681.08') and datum information.

*   **Blocks 3, 6, 9, 11, 12: Administrative, Legal & Boilerplate**
    *   Contains the legal description, title block, sheet numbers, engineer's seal, Texas 811 logo, and standard City of Austin disclaimers.
    *   READ for project address, lot information, and general liability disclaimers if needed.
