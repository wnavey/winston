NOTES:
1. THE CONTRACTOR IS RESPONSIBLE FOR OBTAINING ALL PERMITS NECESSARY TO LEGALLY COMMENCE WITH THIS WORK.
2. OBTAIN LOCATION AND DEPTH OF EXISTING UTILITY LINES PRIOR TO BEGINNING CONSTRUCTION.
3. CONTRACTOR SHALL DISPOSE OF ALL SPOILS LEGALLY AT AN OFFSITE LOCATION OR AS DIRECTED BY OWNER.

1. CONCRETE TO HAVE A MINIMUM 28 DAY COMPRESSIVE STRENGTH OF 3000 PSI.
