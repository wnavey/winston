# Sheet 26 — Construction Details 5

Standard details for wastewater service connections and sewer cleanouts.

## Reading Guide

This sheet provides standard construction details and specifications for wastewater infrastructure for the Valley View Townhomes project. It primarily features City of Austin standard diagrams and notes covering single and double wastewater service connections, cleanout installations, concrete encasements, and procedures for tapping into existing lined pipes.

**Key values:**
*   **Minimum Pipe Slope:** 1% (for service connections and cleanouts)
*   **Maximum Pipe Deflection/Slope:** 45° max (exceeding 45° requires deep service rules)
*   **Service Depth at Property Line:** 4' minimum, 6' maximum
*   **Minimum Cover Below Final Street Grade:** 42"
*   **Pipe to Manhole Connector Limit:** Limits pipe slope into manhole to 12% max
*   **Sewer Cleanout Clearance:** Must be located a minimum of 6" from all paved areas (sidewalks, driveways, parking lots)
*   **Cleanout Concrete Collar:** 6" thickness
*   **Concrete Encasement for Slip-on Joints:** Minimum of 4" on all sides (vertical rising section)
*   **Host Pipe Opening for Lined Pipe:** At least 2" but no more than 3" wider than the service adaptor diameter
*   **Core Hole Distance (Lined Pipe):** Edge must be at least 3' from the ends of the pipe, other connections, or fittings
*   **Encasement Sleeves:** 8" PVC sleeve (if no pipe bells in riser); 10" PVC sleeve (if pipe bells in riser)
