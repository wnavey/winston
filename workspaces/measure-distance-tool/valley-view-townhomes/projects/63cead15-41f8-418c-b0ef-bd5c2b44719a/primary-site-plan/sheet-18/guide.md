# Sheet 18 — Austin Water General Information & Construction Notes

Austin Water general information, construction notes, and flow tests.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains general information, Austin Water construction notes, service extension request (SER) approval letters, and utility demand data for the Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to establish the baseline water and wastewater requirements, document utility capacities, and outline standard City of Austin construction guidelines.

**Key values:**
*   **Proposed Meters:** 6" Combination Potable & Fire (2000 max GPM); 3/4" Irrigation (30 max GPM)
*   **Infrastructure:** 45 LF of 8" water service; 7 LF of 6" wastewater service
*   **System Pressures:** 78.79 PSI static at highest lot; 61.47 PSI static at lowest lot
*   **Fire Flow:** 1471 GPM available at 20 PSI; 3000 GPM required (reduced to 1000 GPM minimum with sprinklers)
*   **Hydrant Flow Test:** 72 PSI static, 48 PSI residual, 969 GPM flow rate
*   **Water Demands:** 240 GPM domestic; 25 GPM irrigation
*   **Line Velocities:** 2.72 FPS in 6" domestic line; 9.57 FPS in 8" fire line
*   **Fixture Units:** 1,326 Drainage Fixture Units (DFU); 1,326 Water Fixture Units (WSFU)
*   **Capacities (from SER letters):** 28 LUEs; 20 GPM peak wet weather wastewater flow; 60 GPM estimated peak hour water demand

**Content blocks on this sheet:**

*   **Block 19: table — Fire, Domestic and Irrigation Demand Data**
    READ the block content for exact pressure values, building size details (13,344 SQ.FT., Type V-B), calculated fire flows, line velocities, and fixture unit totals.

*   **Block 18: table — AW Infrastructure Information**
    READ the block content for the lengths and sizes of proposed pipe installations (45 LF of 8" water, 7 LF of 6" wastewater).

*   **Block 1: drawing — Potable Meters and Drainage Fixture Unit Table**
    READ the block content for meter sizing (6" fireline, 3/4" irrigation), service units, and the building-by-building breakdown of the 1,326 total Drainage Fixture Units.

*   **Block 12: notes — Water Service Extension Request (SER) Letter**
    READ the block content for the official Austin Water approval confirming the existing 8-inch main is suitable for the 60 GPM peak demand and 1500 GPM sprinkler requirement.

*   **Block 4: form — Wastewater Service Extension Request (SER) Letter**
    READ the block content for the official Austin Water approval confirming the existing 8-inch gravity main (MH No. 15466) can handle the 20 GPM estimated flow.

*   **Block 5: form — Hydrant Flow Test Report**
    READ for exact test data (72 static / 48 residual / 969 GPM flow). Use VISION to examine the attached map for the spatial layout of the test hydrants (Residual 172332, Flow 172943) relative to the site.

*   **Block 20: table — Additional Review Acknowledgement**
    READ the block content to confirm check-box responses regarding automated metering infrastructure, onsite water reuse, and AULCC requirements.

*   **Block 13: notes — Standard Construction Notes**
    READ the block content for the 23 standard Austin Water construction notes regarding trench safety, pressure taps, pipe testing, and compaction requirements.

*   **Blocks 7, 8, 9, 10, 14, 15: various — Additional General Notes**
    READ these blocks for specific procedural requirements covering automated metering (Block 7), inspection scheduling (Block 8), modifications (Block 9), utility verification (Block 10), asbestos handling (Block 14), and electronic file disclaimers (Block 15).

*   **Blocks 2, 3, 6, 11, 16, 17: various — Boilerplate & Administrative**
    Boilerplate content including the main Austin Water title block, expiration stamp, review block, Bleyl Engineering firm info, PE seal for Jason K. Rodgers, and the sheet index block. Routine administrative text; no detailed reading required.
