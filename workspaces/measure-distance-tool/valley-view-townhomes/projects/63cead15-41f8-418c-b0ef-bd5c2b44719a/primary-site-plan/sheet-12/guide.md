# Sheet 12 — Storm Sewer Plan

Displays the proposed storm sewer layout and drainage infrastructure details.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains the proposed Storm Sewer Plan (Sheet 12 of 39) for the Valley View Townhomes project. Its primary purpose is to detail the site's surface and subsurface drainage infrastructure, including the layout of storm sewer pipes, grate inlets, manholes, and junction boxes overlaid on the site grading and building footprints. 

**Key values**:
*   **Scale**: 1" = 20'
*   **Benchmarks**: BM #1 Elevation = 678.52' (NAVD 88), BM #2 Elevation = 681.08' (NAVD 88)
*   **ADA Specifications**: ADA Ramp maximum slope = 8.33%; Level Landing maximum slope = 2.00%
*   **Storm Sewer Pipes**: Drawing indicates extensive use of 12", 18", and 24" HDPE pipe. Examples include:
    *   24" HDPE pipe runs sloped at 1.35% (e.g., lengths of 53.28 lf, 47.98 lf, 26.71 lf, 20.98 lf)
    *   18" HDPE pipe runs sloped at 0.50% (e.g., lengths of 71.10 lf, 63.17 lf, 61.27 lf)
    *   18" HDPE pipe runs sloped at 4.00% (e.g., lengths of 49.02 lf, 25.60 lf, 19.84 lf)
*   **Key Structures**:
    *   4'x4' Junction Box: Rim = 684.80, FL 24" = 673.90
    *   SD Manhole (near Bldg 2): Rim = 684.71, FL 24" = 675.47, FL 18" = 675.57
    *   Multiple 18" and 2'x2' Grate Inlets throughout the site (e.g., 2'x2' Grate Inlet Rim = 684.34, FL 24" = 675.19)
*   **Building Sizes**: Examples include Bldg 2 (4,830 sf, FFE=685.50), Bldg 7 (4,144 sf, FFE=685.0), Bldg 8 (4,997 sf, FFE=685.50)

**Content blocks on this sheet**:

*   **Block 2: drawing — Main Site Plan Drawing**
    Contains the detailed layout of the storm sewer system, including pipe alignments, structure locations, existing/proposed contours, building footprints (Buildings 1-8), and property boundaries.
    *Use VISION to trace drainage flow paths, examine the spatial relationship between infrastructure and building footprints, and READ for specific pipe dimensions, slopes, and structure elevations.*

*   **Block 10: seal — Title Block**
    Provides primary project identification (Valley View Townhomes, Austin, TX) and the engineering firm's information (Bleyl Engineering).
    *READ for project context and location.*

*   **Block 3: notes — Benchmarks**
    Details the vertical control points used for the site plan, specifying the elevations of BM #1 and BM #2.
    *READ for exact survey elevation references.*

*   **Block 4: notes — Legend**
    Defines the line types and symbols used on the drawing, such as existing/proposed contours, spot elevations, and limits of construction. It also notes maximum slopes for ADA ramps and landings.
    *READ for ADA constraints and symbol definitions, VISION to map these symbols onto the main drawing.*

*   **Block 5: notes — Drainage & Curb Notes**
    Provides construction directions for curbs (catch vs. spill curbs) and administrative requirements for detention/filtration facility certification prior to obtaining a Certificate of Occupancy.
    *READ for compliance and curb grading requirements.*

*   **Blocks 6, 7: notes — References & Legal Description**
    Provides the legal plat description (Lot 1, Mecey Subdivision) and points to Sheet 32 for the site's tree list.
    *READ to cross-reference property bounds or tree data.*

*   **Blocks 1, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14: various — Boilerplate, Scale & Administrative**
    Contains the engineer's seal (Jason K. Rodgers), Texas 811 utility contact information, standard utility verification disclaimers, city review disclaimers, the drawing scale (1" = 20'), project tracking numbers, and the sheet index (Sheet 12 of 39).
    *READ Block 12 to confirm the drawing scale; skip the rest unless auditing standard design metadata or disclaimers.*
