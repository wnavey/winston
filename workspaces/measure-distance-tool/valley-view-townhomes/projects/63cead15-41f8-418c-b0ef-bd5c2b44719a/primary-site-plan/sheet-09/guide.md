# Sheet 9 — Site Plan

Detailed site plan layout with associated building and parking data.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains the primary Site Plan for the Valley View Townhomes project (Sheet 9 of 39). It illustrates the overall site layout, including the arrangement of eight multi-family residential buildings, internal driveways, parking, a detention pond, pedestrian walkways, and retaining walls, while providing comprehensive tabular data to verify compliance with zoning, area, coverage, height, and parking requirements.

**Key values:**
*   **Site Area:** 94,192 sf (2.162 acres)
*   **Zoning:** MF-2/Multi-Family Low Density
*   **Total Units:** 39 units (16 Two Bedroom, 23 Three Bedroom) across 8 buildings
*   **Maximum Building Height:** 40' allowed; 36' - 1" is the highest proposed (Building 3)
*   **Impervious Cover:** 59% proposed (55,502 sf) vs 60% allowable
*   **Building Coverage:** 34% proposed (32,296 sf) vs 50% allowable
*   **Gross Floor Area:** 83,986 sf
*   **Vehicle Parking:** 88 total spaces provided (10 surface, 78 garage)
*   **Bicycle Parking:** 5 required; 41 provided (39 long term, 2 short term)
*   **Accessibility Slopes:** Max parking slope 1:50; Max cross-slope 1:50; Max ramp slope 1:12
*   **Benchmarks (NAVD 88):** BM #1 = 678.52'; BM #2 = 681.08'
