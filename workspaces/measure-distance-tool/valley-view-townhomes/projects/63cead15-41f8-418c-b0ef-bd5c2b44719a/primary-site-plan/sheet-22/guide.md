# Sheet 22 — Construction Details 1

Construction details for curbs, sidewalks, driveways, and bicycle parking.

## Reading Guide

This sheet contains standard construction details, primarily City of Austin (COA) standard specifications, for site civil elements. Its primary purpose is to provide the required dimensions, reinforcement, and installation procedures for curbs, gutters, sidewalks, driveways, bicycle parking, and bumper curbs.

**Key values:**
*   **Driveway Thickness:** Passenger vehicle parking lots = 150 mm (6") min.; All others = 175 mm (7") min.
*   **Driveway Reinforcement:** 125 mm (5") min. concrete with one layer 13M (#4) bars placed at max 450 mm (18") O.C. both directions.
*   **Driveway Allowable Grades:** >1500 ADT = 5% max; 500-1500 ADT = 6% max; <500 ADT = 8% (up to 15% max for emergency access).
*   **Type I Driveway Slope:** Elevation typically min. 150 mm (6") plus 20 mm/m (2"/100') over distance.
*   **Sidewalk Reinforcement:** 152x152xMW9xMW9 welded wire fabric or one layer 10M (#3) bars max 450 mm (18") C.C. both directions.
*   **Bicycle Rack Base Plates:** 6.35 mm (1/4") plates, ASTM A-36 1010-1018 low carbon prime steel.
*   **Bicycle Rack Siting:** Must be within 7.32m (24') of main building entry; minimum clearance from utilities is 0.9m (3').
*   **Bumper Curb Concrete:** Class "A" in accordance with Item 403S.
