**ADMINISTRATIVE VARIANCE (24-29”) AND LAND USE COMMISSION VARIANCE (30” AND GREATER)**

**FYI – Current Status: Informational for Formal Submittal**
* U0: Administrative Variance
Currently, the site plan proposes to remove Heritage tree 4038 – 25” Live Oak. An administrative variance may be granted for a heritage tree that does not have at least one stem that is 30 inches in diameter or larger to be removed only after determining by the City Arborist that the tree is dead, diseased, or an imminent hazard. If not dead, diseased or an imminent hazard, clearly show that the tree prevents reasonable use or access of the property and that all design options have been exhausted.
Reference: LDC Section 25-8-642
