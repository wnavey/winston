# Master Comment Report

A comprehensive compilation of city staff review comments requiring updates for a site plan application.

## Sections

1. **General Information and Case Manager Review** (pp. 1-2)
   Provides project details, update deadlines, submittal requirements, and the case manager's initial site plan comments.
   File: `01-general-information-and-case-manager-review.md`
2. **Electric Review** (pp. 2-3)
   Lists the preliminary design requirements and links for Austin Energy review comments.
   File: `02-electric-review.md`
3. **Transportation & Public Works (TPW-TDS) Review** (pp. 3-9)
   Details requirements related to parking, street impact fees, right-of-way, driveways, and trash collection.
   File: `03-transportation-public-works-tpw-tds-review.md`
4. **Drainage Engineering Review** (pp. 9-14)
   Reviews the project's storm water management, drainage easements, retaining walls, dam safety, and DCM waiver requirements.
   File: `04-drainage-engineering-review.md`
5. **Environmental Review** (p. 14)
   Details pending requirements for environmental and landscape inspection fees and ESC fiscal surety.
   File: `05-environmental-review.md`
6. **Fire For Site Plan Review** (pp. 14-15)
   Outlines fire department requirements regarding general notes, access roads, and fire hydrant flow constraints.
   File: `06-fire-for-site-plan-review.md`
7. **Land Development Engineering** (p. 15)
   Identifies items needing a License Agreement and requires adjustments to a driveway detail.
   File: `07-land-development-engineering.md`
8. **PARD / Planning & Design Review** (pp. 15-16)
   Details the pending parkland dedication fee amount required prior to the issuance of the certificate of occupancy.
   File: `08-pard-planning-design-review.md`
9. **Site Plan Review** (pp. 16-17)
   Requires updates to unit counts, site area calculations, building data tables, lighting notes, and administrative notes.
   File: `09-site-plan-review.md`
10. **Site Plan Plumbing Review** (pp. 17-18)
   Reviews the required drainage fixture unit loading and asks for pipe sizing, slopes, and materials to be documented.
   File: `10-site-plan-plumbing-review.md`
11. **AW Utility Development Services** (p. 18)
   Notes that the site is in an area of capacity concern and requires a Service Extension Request for wastewater.
   File: `11-aw-utility-development-services.md`
12. **Water Quality Review** (pp. 18-21)
   Assesses water quality controls, impervious cover treatment, pond design dimensions, and biofiltration compliance.
   File: `12-water-quality-review.md`
13. **AW Pipeline Engineering** (p. 21)
   Provides a link to download redline review comments for the pipeline engineering portion.
   File: `13-aw-pipeline-engineering.md`
14. **City Arborist Review** (pp. 21-25)
   Specifies tree preservation criteria, variance requirements for heritage trees, and mitigation procedures.
   File: `14-city-arborist-review.md`
15. **AW OWB Review & Comment Resolution** (pp. 26-27)
   Reviews building coverage and gross floor area reporting constraints and outlines the comment resolution process.
   File: `15-aw-owb-review-comment-resolution.md`
