## Hypothesis — why the agent got this wrong
The item has two prongs: legible AND obtained directly from Municode. For sourcing, the agent accepted City adoption stamps and standard numbering as proof of Municode provenance -- a stamp on a reproduction states officialness but does not demonstrate the reproduction was pulled from Municode (a re-drawn or degraded copy carries the same stamp). For legibility, the majority asserted 'legible with clear text' while the dissenting run found dimensions and notes blurry; the majority never made a resolution-level vision check of the smallest annotation text. Attestation was substituted for demonstration on both prongs.

1. **agent-prompt-change**: Under the demonstration-over-attestation rule, require a vision legibility check at annotation scale (smallest text readable) before crediting 'legible', and treat stamps/numbering as necessary-but-insufficient for the Municode-sourcing prong.
