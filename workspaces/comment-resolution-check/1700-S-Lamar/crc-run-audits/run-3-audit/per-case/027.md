> The gross site area for the project is confirmed, and the parkland dedication for the multifamily development does not exceed 10 percent of that gross site area.

**Evidence expected**: Site data table

## Agent observation (verbatim)

> Sheet 1 Block 13 states 'TOTAL SITE AREA: 5.21 AC (226,948 SF)'. Sheet 16 Block 4 (Project Tracking Table) confirms the same value: total lot acreage of 5.21 acres (226,985 SF — minor rounding difference of 37 SF between the two sheets). The 10% cap under LDC §25-1-603(D) is 5.21 acres × 10% = 0.521 acres. The required dedication per the formula (459 units × 0.005 = 2.295 acres) exceeds the cap, so the capped value of 0.521 acres governs. This matches the Parkland Early Determination Letter's estimate of 0.52 acres.

## Agent reasoning (verbatim)

> This item requires confirmation of the gross site area and verification that the parkland dedication does not exceed 10% of that area. The U1 plan clearly documents 5.21 acres as the gross site area on both the cover sheet and the site data table. The 10% cap is 0.521 acres, which is less than the uncapped formula result of 2.295 acres (459 × 0.005), so the cap applies. The plan satisfies the baseline requirement by documenting the gross site area that drives the 10% cap calculation.
