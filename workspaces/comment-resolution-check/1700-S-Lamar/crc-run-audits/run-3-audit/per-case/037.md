## Checklist item (verbatim)

> If a supplemental zone is provided, up to 30 percent of its linear frontage is a maximum of 30 feet wide and the remainder is a maximum of 20 feet wide.

**Evidence expected**: Site plan / streetscape plan

## Agent observation (verbatim)

> Vision checks of Sheets 14, 15, and 53 confirm that no Supplemental Zone is labeled or shown along the South Lamar Boulevard frontage. The requirement is conditional: IF a supplemental zone is provided, THEN up to 30% of its linear frontage may be a maximum of 30 feet wide and the remainder must be ≤20 feet wide. Since no supplemental zone exists, the conditional does not trigger.

## Agent reasoning (verbatim)

> The supplemental zone is optional per Sub E § 2.2.2.C. The requirement to verify widths applies only if a supplemental zone is provided. U1 does not provide a supplemental zone along the principal street, so the width-verification requirement is not applicable. This is distinct from the Planting Zone and Clear Zone which are mandatory.

## Agent explanation (verbatim)

> No supplemental zone provided along South Lamar; requirement is conditional and does not apply.

Agent declared NA because 'no Supplemental Zone is labeled or shown along the South Lamar Boulevard frontage.' City flagged this in calibration — likely a supplemental zone IS shown but agent missed the label, or city wants confirmation that lack of supplemental zone is intentional.
