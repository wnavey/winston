# EL 5.0 File 5 Audit — "Encroachments into Electric Easements"

**5.0 source:** `/tmp/bureau-5.0/jurisdictions/austin/review-guides/el/5.md` (worktree pinned to `ced6e10`)

## Summary

The 5.0 guide "Encroachments into Electric Easements" contained 31 checklist items organized around five themes: (1) water-quality-feature clearances from overhead/underground distribution (EL-5.1–5.7), (2) transmission-specific encroachment/access rules (EL-5.6, 5.8, 5.9, 5.18, 5.22, 5.23, 5.26–5.31), (3) generic AE-easement encroachments (buildings, landscaping, utilities, fences, amenities, construction staging — EL-5.10–5.21, 5.24, 5.25), (4) parking within distribution vs. transmission easements (EL-5.17, 5.18), and (5) drainage structures within easements (EL-5.14).

In 5.1, the el/ guide was consolidated from 20 files to 3, and the dedicated "Encroachments into Electric Easements" grouping was dissolved. Most of its content collapsed into a single generic checklist item (`el/2.md EL-2.16`) about non-utility items in easements, supported by regulatory-overview prose that lists the prohibitions but no longer generates discrete checklist items. Transmission-specific construction safety rules partially survive as `el/1.md EL-1.18/1.25` and as plat-notes obligations in `el/3.md EL-3.32` (AEDC 1.16.0(8)). Water-quality-feature-specific clearance items were dropped wholesale — the 5.1 guide retains the same UCM 1.10.3 clearances but only in the general "customer facilities" formulation of `el/1.md EL-1.2/1.3`, not called out for ponds/rain gardens. The parkland-in-transmission-easement prohibition moved cross-department to `park/5.md PARK-5.10`, and vegetation-in-transmission-easement moved to `eptp/10.md EPTP-10.22`. A substantial amount of transmission encroachment detail (25-ft access corridor, 100-ft working area, laydown curbing, gate widths, grading slopes, fire-hydrant setbacks, co-located easements, sprinkler and mulch restrictions) has no discrete 5.1 checklist item and appears dropped or reduced to background prose.

## Notable patterns

1. **Collapse into one generic item.** A large cluster of 5.0 encroachment items (EL-5.10, 5.11, 5.15, 5.17, 5.20, parts of 5.13/5.24) collapsed into the single omnibus EL-2.16. This trades specificity for brevity and will likely reduce recall on feature-specific violations (pavers, bike racks, masonry fence, below-grade footings, tree wells).

2. **Transmission content heavily pruned.** Roughly 10 transmission-specific rules from 5.0 (100-ft working area, laydown curbs, gate widths, 8% slope, 25-ft irrigation, compost-over-foundation, fire hydrants, parking alignment, co-located easements, storm-drain under transmission) have no equivalent 5.1 checklist item. The surviving transmission rules are construction-sequence oriented (EL-1.18, EL-1.25) or plat-notes-oriented (EL-3.32).

3. **Water-quality-feature framing deleted entirely.** All seven WQF-specific items (EL-5.1–5.7) lost their WQF framing. The underlying UCM 1.10.3 clearance lives on generically in EL-1.2/1.3 as "customer facilities"; UCM 1.10.7 (WQF-to-UG-equipment) is completely absent from 5.1 el/. Reviewers looking for rain-garden/bioswale clearance issues will not find targeted checks.

4. **Cross-department migration is limited.** Only two items cleanly moved cross-department: climbing vegetation to EPTP-10.22 and parkland-in-transmission-easement to PARK-5.10/PARK-6.8. Fire hydrants near transmission did NOT move to fire/, despite the fire review guide being the obvious home.

5. **Licensing-nuance loss.** The 5.0 distinction between "license agreement obtained and filed with AE Public Involvement" vs. "to be obtained" (which was flagged as a common violation pattern) is reduced to general language in EL-2.16; the 5.0 anti-pattern is not preserved as an explicit check.
