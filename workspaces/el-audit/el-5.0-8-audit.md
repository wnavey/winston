# EL 5.0 File 8 — "Electric Service Design & Equipment Coordination" Audit

**5.0 source:** `jurisdictions/austin/review-guides/el/8.md` @ commit `ced6e10` (43 checklist items: EL-8.1 – EL-8.43)
**5.1 corpus:** `jurisdictions/austin/review-guides/` @ commit `8fccc32b3` (excluding `el-md-exp/`)
**5.1 EL guides only contain 3 files** (`el/1.md`, `el/2.md`, `el/3.md`). No cross-department relocation of 8.md content was found — all surviving coverage lives within `el/`.

## Summary

The 5.0 "Electric Service Design & Equipment Coordination" file was an end-to-end ESPA/AE-Design checklist. In 5.1, its scope has been largely collapsed into the coordination-and-notes guide `el/3.md` (Austin Energy Service and ROW Utility Coordination), with equipment-placement pieces (transformer pad, pole relocation, parkland/ROW prohibitions) moving to `el/2.md`. A large number of granular "specific equipment type approved by AE Design" items were dropped in favor of two catch-all retained items: EL-3.1 (ESPA approval) and EL-3.7 (transformer/pull-box AE approval). Meter-related items, niche service, vault-in-Network-Area, switchgear, manholes, duct banks, and demolition meter-removal coordination all appear to have been removed.

## Patterns

- **Aggressive consolidation into a single ESPA approval gate.** EL-8.1, 8.4, 8.5, 8.8, 8.9, 8.10, 8.20, 8.36 all collapse into el/3.md EL-3.1 ("ESPA submitted/approved") plus EL-3.2 ("AEU clearance"). The retrained guide treats "does plan match AE-approved design?" as a single yes/no rather than auditing each sheet/feature.
- **Equipment-type-specific approvals dropped wholesale.** Vaults (8.12), pole-mounted service (8.13), switchgear (8.17), duct banks (8.18), manholes (8.19), niche service (8.16) — no 5.1 item mentions these by type. Only pad-mounted transformers (via el/2.md EL-2.22 and el/3.md EL-3.7) survive.
- **Meter/meter-room/meter-to-disconnect entire topic removed.** EL-8.14, EL-8.15, and the regulatory-overview content around UCM § 1.9.3, § 1.9.3.1(I) have no descendants anywhere in the 5.1 corpus (checked el/, eptp/, sde/, sduf/, wwp/). This is the largest single coverage gap.
- **Demolition coordination reduced.** EL-8.32 (meter/service-drop removal before demolition) became a generic "preconstruction meeting with AE before demolition" note requirement (EL-3.9); the UCM 1.10.13 removal-sequence substance is gone.
- **"Required callout" / "statement on plans" items (EL-8.29, EL-8.31) removed.** Fits the pattern observed in other audits of trimming free-standing note-content deficiencies.
- **Existing-facilities-coordination cluster (UCM 1.16.0) hollowed out.** EL-8.24, 8.25, 8.26, 8.27, 8.28, 8.30, 8.33 — the pre-design coordination sub-taxonomy is gone; only pole relocation (EL-2.21) survived as a single catch-all.
- **No cross-department moves detected.** Unlike some 5.0 electrical topics that migrated (clearances → stayed in el/; some elements overlap with sde/park/fire in adjacent audits), no EL-8.x item was found to have moved to a non-EL guide.

## Surprises

1. **Entire meter-placement topic is unreviewed in 5.1.** UCM § 1.9.3 (meter must be outdoors, accessible, ground floor, w/in sight of transformer; prohibited locations) is not in any review guide's checklist — a substantial regression in coverage.
2. **Niche service silently dropped.** Niche configuration is a named, specialized delivery method in UCM § 1.10.4 with unique clearance requirements (35-ft vertical, 3-hr fire walls); the 5.0 item required AE Design business unit consultation. No 5.1 item mentions niches.
3. **Network Area vault restriction lost.** The "vaults only in Network Area" rule — still a hard rule in UCM § 1.5.1.1 — is not enforced by any 5.1 checklist item.
4. **Unified development cluster (EL-8.38, 8.39, 8.40) fully removed** despite UCM § 1.3.8 persisting as active code.
5. **EL-3.1 now carries enormous implicit scope.** Approximately eight 5.0 items depend on it as their only surviving coverage; if ESPA approval documentation is present but the design is substantively non-compliant, 5.1 has no checklist item that will flag it.
