# EL 5.0 File 3 Audit — "Underground Electric Clearances and Utility Separation"

**5.0 source:** `/tmp/bureau-5.0/jurisdictions/austin/review-guides/el/3.md` (worktree pinned to commit `ced6e10`)
**5.1 corpus HEAD (bureau/main):** `8fccc32b3` ("Restore Overview, Documents to Review, and Validation Methodology")
**5.1 el/ directory:** 3 files — `el/1.md` (Overhead/Vertical Clearances), `el/2.md` (Underground Utility Routing and Placement), `el/3.md` (Austin Energy Service and ROW Utility Coordination)

## Summary

The 5.0 `el/3.md` file "Underground Electric Clearances and Utility Separation" contained 42 checklist items (EL-3.1 through EL-3.42) organized around horizontal/vertical clearances between Austin Energy underground facilities and buildings, utilities, gas lines, and transmission easements, plus electric meter placement rules. In 5.1, this file was dissolved; the 5.1 `el/3.md` is a completely different topic (Austin Energy service coordination, moonlight towers, street lighting, AULCC closeout). The surviving rules landed almost entirely in `el/1.md` (the new clearances guide), with a small set of items dropped from the checklist (most notably nearly all electric-meter-placement rules under UCM 1.9.3, and several reviewer-convention items about specific scenarios like guy wire anchors, chilled water tunnels, manholes in parking, wet/dry alternation, parallel parking, and streetlight-to-building clearance). The transmission-easement corpus of rules (EL-3.25, EL-3.26, EL-3.33-3.37) collapsed: the 25-ft/16-ft corridor and the tandem-axle loading rule survived (the axle rule only in the AEDC 1.16.0(8) plat-notes checklist in el/3.md 5.1), while the ≥8% slope, lay-down curb, gate width, and 100-ft proximity rules appear to be dropped from the explicit checklist (regulatory overview in 5.1 el/1.md still mentions UCM 1.14.4 broadly). Gas/fuel/steam granular thresholds (EL-3.27, 3.28, 3.29) are only preserved as the Regulatory Overview text in 5.1 el/1.md plus a single combined EL-1.9 (12-inch) item; the 36-inch and 24-inch/6-inch specifics are not separate checklist rows. Water/wastewater vertical-at-crossings was split/reworked into EL-1.12 (method-dependent), EL-1.9, and EL-1.10.

A parallel experimental guide `el-md-exp/13.md` ("Transformers/Pad-Mounted Equipment") still carries some of the dropped gas/fuel thresholds (EL-13.24) and meter items (in `el-md-exp/1.md`), but it is an experimental overlay, not part of the core 5.1 `el/` guide. Cross-references to it are noted where relevant but do not count as "retained in 5.1 core."

## Notable patterns

1. **Wholesale drop of UCM 1.9.3 (electric meter placement) rules.** EL-3.39 through EL-3.42 covered meter-location deficiencies that were a frequent historical reviewer comment category. None of them survived in core 5.1 el/. UCM 1.9.3.1 only resurfaces in the `el-md-exp/` experimental overlay. This is the biggest thematic gap — four meter-placement rules dropped without replacement.

2. **Transmission-easement-specific rules (UCM 1.14.4.A–G) hollowed out.** Of seven transmission-ROW items (EL-3.25, 3.26, 3.33–3.37), only the 25-ft/16-ft corridor (EL-3.33 → EL-1.19) and tandem-axle loading (EL-3.26 → EL-3.32 note vii) survived. Gate width, slope, lay-down curbs, 100-ft above-ground obstructions, and detention-prohibition were all dropped from the explicit checklist. The tandem-axle rule also migrated from UCM 1.14.4.F to AEDC 1.16.0(8)(vii) and changed threshold from 46,000 lb to 48,000 lb.

3. **Gas/fuel/steam granular thresholds collapsed into the 12-inch rule.** EL-3.27 (36-in), EL-3.28 (24-in/6-in), EL-3.29 (36-in steam) all vanished as standalone checks. Only the 12-inch perpendicular-crossing rule (EL-1.9) carries a checklist row; the 36-inch gas threshold survives in overview prose only. Reviewers will no longer get targeted flags for gas-line clearance deficiencies unless the 12-in default trips.

4. **The 5-ft underground-AE-to-building buffer is effectively gone.** Six 5.0 items (EL-3.1–3.5, EL-3.7, EL-3.24) all keyed off UCM 1.10.5.B's 5-ft building/structure clearance from underground electric. None of them has a specific 5.1 analog. This clearance category is now covered only indirectly by the generic "show clearances" requirement in AEDC 1.16.0(1)/1.16.0(2) (el/3.md 5.1 EL-3.11, EL-3.12).

5. **Many reviewer-convention items dropped without trace.** EL-3.12, 3.13, 3.14, 3.16, 3.18, 3.19, 3.20, 3.21, 3.22 (all "Reviewer Convention" cited) mostly disappeared. This is consistent with an overall push in 5.1 training to cut loosely-sourced items. Only EL-3.33 (transmission corridor), EL-3.17 (AW 5-ft, partial), and the wet-utility/chilled-water themes found cross-department homes.

6. **5.1 el/3.md is a repurposed file.** The 5.0 el/3.md topic (underground clearances) is entirely gone from 5.1 el/3.md, which now covers AE service coordination, AULCC, moonlight towers, and street lighting. Only tenuous overlap exists (AEDC 1.16.0(8) plat-notes checklist carries the transmission-easement axle-loading rule from 5.0 EL-3.26).

7. **Number change worth flagging:** 46,000 lb → ≥48,000 lb tandem axle. The threshold substance itself changed between 5.0 (UCM 1.14.4.F cited 46,000 lb, 5-ft spacing) and 5.1 (AEDC 1.16.0(8)(vii) cited ≥48,000 lb). Out of scope per instructions (citation currency), but noting for the caller since it is not a pure re-labeling.
