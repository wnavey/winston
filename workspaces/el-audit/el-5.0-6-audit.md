- **Scope:** every row in the 5.0 `## Checklist Items` table (EL-6.1 through EL-6.14)

## Summary

The 5.0 guide was an easement-centric grouping covering nomenclature, dimensional widths, all-weather surface, 24/7 access, ground-to-sky protection, niche/transmission/substation/ductbank/transformer access, and laydown curb detailing. In 5.1, the `el/` discipline was compressed from 20 guides to 3 (`1.md` clearances, `2.md` underground routing/placement, `3.md` AE service + ROW coordination). The result for this file is a large contraction: most easement-specific, dimensional, and facility-type-specific access items were dropped or absorbed into broader clearance/placement deficiencies without retaining the original granularity. Nothing from File 6 moved cross-department — the 5.1 retention is entirely within `el/`.

## Patterns and Observations

- **Nothing moved cross-department.** Every 5.1 survivor lives in `el/1.md`, `el/2.md`, or `el/3.md`. Candidate homes (`sde/`, `wwp/`, `ta/`) contain no facility-access or easement-labeling items relocated from EL 5.0 File 6.
- **Frame shift: easement boundary → conductor position.** The 5.0 guide measured encroachments from easement lines; 5.1 EL-1.2/EL-1.3/EL-1.5 explicitly pivot to conductor-anchored measurement and deprecate pole/easement-anchored dimensions as "non-compliant even when stated distances appear adequate." This is the single largest semantic change and downgrades every 5.0 easement-based deficiency to `partial` at best.
- **Construction vs. permanent conflation.** EL-6.10 (permanent 25-ft transmission access route) maps onto EL-1.19, but 5.1 scoped that check to construction-duration. Permanent transmission-corridor access at approval time is no longer an explicit checklist item.
- **Facility-type granularity collapsed.** EL-6.11 (transformer), EL-6.12 (ductbank), EL-6.13 (substation), EL-6.14 (niche) were four separate per-facility-type access checks. Only transformer survives in diluted form via EL-2.22; the other three types have no dedicated deficiency.
- **Specification-level standards dropped wholesale.** All-weather surface spec (HMAC/PCC), 72,180 lb load, laydown curb spec (SSM 430s, 46,000 lb tandem axle), 35-ft niche vertical, 3-hour firewalls — none of these numeric specifications survive anywhere in 5.1 `el/`.
- **Nomenclature enforcement gone.** The "use full 'electric distribution, electric telecommunications, and electric fiber easement' name" and "label as 'aerial'" requirements (EL-6.2, EL-6.3) are fully dropped. EL-1.16's Applicability column tolerates abbreviated labels ("AE easement," "transmission easement," "aerial electric easement") as equivalent rather than requiring a particular form.

## Surprises

- 8 of 14 items (57%) were removed outright, not consolidated. This file took disproportionate losses relative to a typical consolidation ratio.
- The UCM § 1.3.16 all-weather-surface standard and LDC § 25-12-173 Section 202.1 pavement standard, which underpinned multiple deficiencies, are not cited anywhere in the 3 new `el/` guides.
- UCM § 2.9.1.A.1 (easement width) is flagged by 5.1 itself as uncertain — EL-1.17 notes the specific prohibition "cannot be confirmed in current code text."
