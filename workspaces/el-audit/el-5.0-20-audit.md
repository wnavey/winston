## Summary

File 20 was the "catch-all" specialized/advanced file in 5.0. Retraining consolidated coverage of AE-facility, transmission, and easement-license items into the three consolidated 5.1 `el/` guides (mostly `el/3.md` and `el/2.md`). Narrow topics of limited historical deficiency volume were dropped: Project Connect transit coordination, LCRA transmission approval, Key Account routing, railroad bore-pit permit, Central Plant/tree-uplighting AE coordination, fence profile/detail for AE adjacency, Chapter 26 park routing for AE service, ETJ § 30-1-113 application, § 25-11-64 demolition utility verification, AE equipment 2-ft-above-floodplain elevation, 20-ft meter-to-disconnect/line-of-sight, bollards at 4-ft parking proximity, and oil-filled transformer drainage direction. Several items were reworded and moved cross-department (transmission demo-hazard barricades into `el/1.md`; LCRA transmission moved into WWP's LCRA-easement items for the water department use case but true "AE transmission LCRA letter" is removed; floodplain electrical equipment partial-covered in FWP).

## Patterns

1. **Specialized-topic purge.** File 20's role as the 5.0 "catch-all" for narrow AE topics did not survive retraining. ~50% of items (17 of 33) have no 5.1 analogue, overwhelmingly the narrower ones: Project Connect, Key Account, LCRA, bore-pit permits, Central Plant, tree uplighting, ETJ § 30-1-113, demolition § 25-11-64, and AE-property construction-entrance / fire-lane issues. These were likely below the training-pipeline clustering/prevalence threshold after v5.1 reclustering.
2. **Transmission items consolidated into el/1.md overhead-clearances.** 5.0's transmission-review items (EL-20.5, 20.6, 20.7, 20.8, 20.23) became specific deliverable items in `el/1.md` (EL-1.18 demo-sequence barricade/notes, EL-1.19 ROW-corridor, EL-1.26 clearance-envelope delineation) plus the transmission-easement plat-notes item (EL-3.32). The umbrella "AE Transmission review obtained" framing is gone.
3. **Licensing consolidated into el/2.md.** 5.0's EL-20.14 License Agreement with AE Property Agents collapsed into EL-2.16's generic easement-licensing item distinguishing private-AE vs. public-ROW regimes.
4. **Coverage leakage to other departments.** Chilled-water notes moved to WWP, not EL (WWP-41.38). Floodplain electrical equipment moved to FWP (FWP-7.42/43) but under a different framework (LDC building-floodplain, not UCM AE-facility). LCRA moved to WWP but only for wastewater. Project Connect moved entirely to TA.
5. **UCM 1.9.3 (meters/disconnects) and UCM 1.10.4 drainage/bollard footnote fully dropped.** ZLU-15.40 is the only residual AE-1.10.4 clearance item and it is framed as a zoning-setback issue; the bollard clause survives there only incidentally. The UCM 1.9.3 meter-grouping / 20-ft / line-of-sight standard has no 5.1 presence.
6. **Several high-value retained concepts are "partial" only.** EL-20.8 (CAD overlay for transmission encroachment) survives only as the generic AEDC 1.16.0(1) AutoCAD requirement (EL-3.11); the transmission-encroachment trigger condition is lost.
